# Stuck Log

One entry per time you got stuck on a proof step or exercise (written by `/stuck` and `/check`). The point is to surface recurring gaps, so keep entries short.

Gap types: `definition-recall`, `prior-result` (forgot or didn't think of an earlier theorem), `hidden-lemma` (author used an unstated fact), `quantifiers` (order, negation, choosing $\varepsilon$/$N$/$\delta$), `estimate` (inequality or bounding trick), `construction` (needed to build an object or counterexample), `strategy` (didn't know how to start: contradiction, contrapositive, induction, etc.), `notation`, `computation`, `misread`.

# Patterns
<!-- Recurring gap types and weak prerequisites, updated when a type shows up 3+ times. -->
- `notation` (10, as of 2026-11-04): mostly *which distribution an expectation is taken under, and what is random*: $\mathbb{E}[\cdot \mid s_1 = s, \pi]$ as the trajectory law, $\widehat{\mathbb{E}}$ as the empirical law, and the time index in $i_t$. Also operator families: a superscript $\pi$ means "for a fixed policy" ($\mathcal{T}^\pi$, $Q^\pi$), while $\star$ or no superscript means optimal ($\mathcal{T}$, $Q^\star$). Habit: for every $\mathbb{E}$, name the measure and the random variables before reading on. Review: [[Empirical Distribution Function]], [[Conditional Expectation]], [[Trajectory]].
