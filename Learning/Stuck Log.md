# Stuck Log

One entry per time you got stuck on a proof step or exercise (written by `/stuck` and `/check`). The point is to surface recurring gaps, so keep entries short.

Gap types: `definition-recall`, `prior-result` (forgot or didn't think of an earlier theorem), `hidden-lemma` (author used an unstated fact), `quantifiers` (order, negation, choosing $\varepsilon$/$N$/$\delta$), `estimate` (inequality or bounding trick), `construction` (needed to build an object or counterexample), `strategy` (didn't know how to start: contradiction, contrapositive, induction, etc.), `notation`, `computation`, `misread`.
