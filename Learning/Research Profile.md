# Projects
These are the hooks for Application cards and quiz questions.
- **Paper 1:** label-invariant estimators of truncated spectral expansions; gradients through eigenvalue (avoided) crossings without $O(1/\text{gap})$ spikes; soft-cutoff roulette, Weyl tail correction.
- **Track B:** diffusion model generating 2D objects with their physics (phase field $\chi$, materials $\kappa, c$) in the fixed Neumann eigenbasis of the disk; the Laplacian-spectral neural operator (LSNO) as denoiser; function-space well-posedness, equivariance.
- **Paper 1b:** spectral exact posterior sampling (EPS) for linear inverse problems; pivot is diagonal iff $A^\top A$ commutes with the forward covariance.
- **Paper 2:** spectral vs. spatial (random-walk / walk-on-spheres) estimators; hybrid spectral control variate.
- **Paper 3 (PhD):** commutation criterion, learned propagators, Koopman.
- **Paper 4:** operators on Gaussian splats.
- **CS 542 project:** gap-uniform LSTD with Laplacian features (RL instance of Paper 1's gap law).
