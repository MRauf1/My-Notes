# Definition
> [!info] Samadhi[^1]
> (Concentration, one-pointedness of mind) The faculty of the mind that focuses single-mindedly on one object without interruption. True concentration is *wholesome* one-pointedness, free from greed, hatred, and delusion.

# Properties
- It is largely a *forced* activity. It can be developed by sheer willpower and keeps some of that forced flavor.[^2]
- It is exclusive: it settles on one item and ignores everything else. [[Mindfulness]] is inclusive.[^3]
- **Lens analogy.** Concentration is the lens that gives the burning intensity needed to see into the deeper mind. Mindfulness chooses what the lens focuses on and looks through it to see what is there.[^4]
- It is a tool that can be used for good or ill. On its own it gives no perspective on oneself and does not understand what it uncovers; only mindfulness brings wisdom.[^5]
- Its development is blocked by the [[Five Hindrances]].[^6]
- **Balance with mindfulness.** The two are cultivated together and support each other. Stress concentration at the beginning, until monkey mind cools down, and whenever you feel frantic. Stress mindfulness when you fall into a stupor, and overall.[^7]
- Excess concentration without mindfulness leads to *mental dullness*, an "ecstatic stupor" in pleasant feelings. The cure is mindfulness.[^8]
- Deep concentration slows down the thought process and speeds up the awareness viewing it.[^9]
- Vipassana only needs *momentary concentration* (see [[Vipassana Meditation]]). Deep absorption (*Jhana*) is cultivated in [[Samatha Meditation]].[^10]
- One of the [[Threefold Training]].
