# Definition

[[Lens]] in the [[Eye]] that provides additional focus.[^1] It is attached to muscles that can change its shape.

# Properties
- Its shape, and hence its [[Optical Power]], is adjusted by these muscles during [[Accommodation]] to bring sources at different distances into sharp focus on the [[Retina]].
