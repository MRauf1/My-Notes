# Review[^1]

Great book about all of calculus by Stewart. Very much engineering based and has lots of practice problems. Although some sections are harder to follow without the more rigorous theory, such as the sections on Vector Calculus. Such sections become much easier to understand once you study the relevant sections on real analysis and differential geometry.

[^1]: [Calculus: Early Transcendentals](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EEFDQ9Y5?page=1)
