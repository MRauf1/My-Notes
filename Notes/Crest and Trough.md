# Definition
> [!info] Crest and Trough[^1]
> For a [[Sinusoidal Wave]] $u = A\cos(kx - \omega t + \varphi_0)$:
> - a **crest** is a point (or, in several dimensions, a surface) of maximum displacement, $u = +A$, where the [[Phase (Waves)|phase]] is $0 \pmod{2\pi}$;
> - a **trough** is a point of minimum displacement, $u = -A$, where the phase is $\pi \pmod{2\pi}$.

# Properties
- Consecutive crests are one [[Wavelength|wavelength]] apart, and a crest and its neighbouring trough are $\lambda/2$ apart.
- In several dimensions the crests form [[Wavefront|wavefronts]]; diagrams of waves usually draw the crest surfaces.
- When crest meets crest, waves add (constructive interference); when crest meets trough, they cancel (destructive) ([[Interference (Waves)]]).
