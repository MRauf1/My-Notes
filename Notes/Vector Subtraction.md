# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Vector]] Subtraction)
> For vectors $\mathbf{v}, \mathbf{w} \in \mathbf{F}^n$, vector subtraction is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \mathbf{v} - \mathbf{w} := \begin{bmatrix}
> v_1 - w_1 \\
> v_2 - w_2 \\
> \dots \\
> v_n - w_n
> \end{bmatrix}
> \end{align}
> $$

Vector subtraction is the elementwise subtraction of the vectors; equivalently, $\mathbf{v} - \mathbf{w} = \mathbf{v} + (-\mathbf{w})$, the [[Vector Addition|addition]] of $\mathbf{v}$ and the negation of $\mathbf{w}$.

[[Geometry|Geometrically]], if $\mathbf{v}$ and $\mathbf{w}$ are drawn from the same starting point, $\mathbf{d} = \mathbf{v} - \mathbf{w}$ is the vector from the tip of $\mathbf{w}$ to the tip of $\mathbf{v}$.
