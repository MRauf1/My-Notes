# Definition
> [!info] Adlerian Psychology (Individual Psychology)[^1]
> The school of psychology founded by [[Alfred Adler]] in the early twentieth century. It explains behavior by the goals a person is moving toward ([[Teleology (Adlerian Psychology)|teleology]]), not by past causes. It treats the person as an indivisible whole ([[Holism (Adlerian Psychology)|holism]]) who gives meaning to a subjective world, sees every problem as a problem in relationships with others, and sets [[Community Feeling]] as the aim of life.

- [[Lifestyle (Adlerian Psychology)]]: one's chosen way of seeing oneself and the world.

# Properties
- **Subjective world.** No one lives in an objective world, only in a subjective one they have given meaning to. The world seems complicated because one makes it complicated. The issue is not how the world is but how one is, so if "I" change, the world changes.[^2]
- **Origin.** Adler was a core member of Freud's Vienna Psychoanalytic Society. He split from it (1911) because his ideas ran counter to Freud's, and founded "individual psychology". Unlike Jung, he related to Freud as a peer rather than a father figure. With Freud and Jung he is counted among the three founding figures of depth psychology.[^3]
- **Psychology of use, not possession.** Freudian etiology asks what one *has* (traumas, drives, upbringing) and ends in determinism. Adlerian psychology asks what *use* one makes of it: "The important thing is not what one is born with but what use one makes of that equipment."[^4]
- **Psychology of courage.** Unhappiness comes from a lack of courage, not from the past, the environment or a lack of ability. "We do not lack ability. We just lack courage."[^5]
- **Psychology for changing oneself.** It does not aim to change others. When one changes, those around one have to change too. Adler: "Someone has to start… you should start. With no regard to whether others are cooperative or not."[^6]
- **Opposed to nihilism.** People are controlled neither by emotion nor by the past.[^7]
- **Counter-intuitive positions.** It denies etiology and trauma and adopts teleology. It treats all problems as interpersonal and rejects both seeking recognition and giving praise. It replaces vertical relationships with horizontal ones.[^8]
- **Two behavioral objectives:** to be self-reliant and to live in harmony with society. **Two psychological objectives** that support them: the consciousness that "I have the ability" and the consciousness that "people are my comrades". The first pair corresponds to [[Self-Acceptance]], the second to [[Confidence in Others]] and [[Contribution to Others]]. These objectives are reached by facing the [[Life Tasks]].[^9]
- Influenced [[Humanism|humanistic psychology]] ([[Abraham Maslow]]) and cognitive therapies ([[Cognitive Therapy]], [[Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy]]). Yalom notes that the present- and future-oriented existential approach is close to Adlerian teleology ([[Existential Psychotherapy]]).
- **Evidence status.** Much of the book is philosophy rather than tested theory. Several empirical corrections are recorded in the linked concept notes (trauma, heritability of personality, praise, life planning).
- **Not falsifiable.** Freud's psychoanalysis, Jung's analytical psychology and Adler's individual psychology all conflict with the falsifiability criterion of science (Popper). Kishimi accordingly presents Adler's work as philosophy ([[Philosophy as a Way of Life]]).[^kk1]
- **Not a religion.** Adler wanted his psychology to be common sense for everyone, not doctrine for specialists. He is "a philosopher who existed on the same level as us", and his ideas must keep being updated rather than held as fundamentalism.[^kk2]
- **Keeping it simple is difficult.** The world and life are simple, but knowing and agreeing with Adler changes nothing. The first step is the biggest turning point, but the real test is the courage to keep walking through ordinary days. Happiness cannot be enjoyed by staying where one is.[^kk3]
- **Everything comes back to love.** Love, self-reliance and community feeling are connected ([[Love]]).[^kk4]
