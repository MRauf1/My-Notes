# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Sequence)[^1]
> Sequence is a [[Function]] with the [[Domain]] $\{n \in \mathbb{Z} | n \geq m\}$. $n$th index of the sequence is denoted as $s_n$ and the sequence itself is denoted as $(s_n)_{n = m}^{\infty} = (s_m, s_{m + 1}, \dots)$.

> [!info] Definition 2 ($\omega$-Tuple)[^2]
> Given a set $X$, an $\omega$-tuple of elements of $X$ is a [[Function]] $x: \mathbb{Z}^+ \to X$, also called an (infinite) sequence; $x_i = x(i)$ is its $i$th coordinate, and $x$ is written $(x_1, x_2, \dots)$ or $(x_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}^+}$. These are the elements of the countable [[Cartesian Product]] $\prod_{i \in \mathbb{Z}^+} A_i$.

Unlike [[Set]], sequence is ordered and can have repeating elements.
