# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Connected Subsets of $\mathbb{R}$)[^1]
> A [[Subset]] $E$ of $\mathbb{R}$ is [[Connected Metric Space|connected]] if and only if it has the following property: whenever $x \in E$, $y \in E$, and $x < z < y$, then $z \in E$.

This characterizes the connected [[Subset|subsets]] of the real line exactly as the [[Interval|intervals]] (including segments, rays, and $\mathbb{R}$ itself): a subset of $\mathbb{R}$ is connected precisely when it contains every point between any two of its points.

[^1]: [Principles of Mathematical Analysis](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/3BD27IHF?page=51)
