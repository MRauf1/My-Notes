# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Matrix]] Transpose)[^1]
> [[Operation]] that switches the rows and columns of a [[Matrix]] $A$.
> $$
> \begin{align}
> [A^T]_{ij} = a_{ji}
> \end{align}
> $$
