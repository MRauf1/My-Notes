# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Corollary 6.10)[^1]
> If $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$ is [[Upper Triangular Matrix|upper triangular]], then
> $$
> \det A = a_{11} \cdots a_{nn}
> $$

This extends to [[Lower Triangular Matrix|lower triangular]] matrices too: the determinant of any triangular matrix is the product of its main diagonal entries. This follows since the transpose of a lower triangular matrix is upper triangular with the same diagonal entries, and $\det(A^T) = \det(A)$ ([[Determinant Matrix Transpose]]).
