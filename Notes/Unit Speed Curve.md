# Definition

[[Curve]] with [[Curve Speed]] of $1$.
Equivalently, a curve $\gamma: I \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^3$ is unit speed if its [[Tangent Map|differential]] preserves length: $|d\gamma(X)| = |X|$ for every tangent vector $X$ on $I$ (also called *arc-length* or *isometric* parameterization; a "completely relaxed rubber band"). Then $T = d\gamma(X)$ is the unit tangent for $X$ the positively oriented unit vector on $I$.[^1] See [[Isometric Parameterization]]; for surfaces the analogue generally does not exist and is replaced by a [[Conformal Map|conformal]] parameterization.

[^1]: [Crane, p. 35](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9N6JDPI2?page=36&annotation=PHZZCDMR)
