# Definition
> [!info] Relativistic Energy-Momentum Relation
> $$E^2 = (pc)^2 + (mc^2)^2$$
> Relates a particle's total energy $E$ to its momentum $p$ and rest [[Mass|mass]] $m$, with $c$ the speed of light in vacuum. This is the general relation, of which [[Mass-Energy Equivalence]] is the special case of a particle at rest.

# Properties
- Reduces to [[Mass-Energy Equivalence]], $E = mc^2$, when the particle is at rest ($p = 0$).
- For a massless particle ($m = 0$), reduces to $E = pc$, e.g. for a [[Photon]].
