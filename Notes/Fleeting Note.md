# Definition
> [!info] Fleeting Note[^1]
> A quick note for capturing an idea while you are busy with something else. Always keep something to write with at hand. Fleeting notes are reminders of thoughts, not the thoughts themselves, and should be processed into [[Permanent Note|permanent notes]] within a day or so, then thrown away.

# Properties
- A sign that a fleeting note was left unprocessed too long: you no longer understand what you meant (you forgot what it was meant to remind you of), or it seems banal (you forgot the context that gave it meaning).[^2]
- Treating *all* notes as fleeting means nothing ever accumulates.[^3]
- Part of the [[Smart Notes Workflow]] in a [[Zettelkasten]].
