# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Isaac Newton]] Third Law)[^1]
> When one body exerts [[Force]] on the other, the second exerts an equal in magnitude, but opposite in direction [[Force]] on the first ($\mathbf{F}_{AB} = - \mathbf{F}_{BA}$).
> Each action has an equal and opposite reaction.

# Properties
- For a system of three or more particles, this pairwise equality holds for each pair's mutual interaction individually; the *net* force on any one particle (the sum of its interactions with every other particle) need not be equal and opposite to the net force on any other single particle.
- Together with [[Newton's Second Law]], is the basis for [[Conservation of Momentum]]: since each pair of mutual (internal) forces cancels when summed, the internal forces within an isolated system cannot change its total momentum.
