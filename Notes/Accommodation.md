# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Accommodation)[^1]
> The process by which muscles attached to the [[Eye Lens]] change its shape, and hence its [[Optical Power]], to bring sources at different distances into sharp focus on the [[Retina]].

# Properties
- Necessary because the [[Lensmaker's Equation]] implies that a lens of fixed shape sharply focuses only a single object distance onto the retina at a time, so nearby and distant sources require different optical powers to stay in focus.
- Not required by [[Pinhole Camera|pinhole optics]], since a pinhole selects a single thin pencil of rays per image point regardless of the source's distance — at the cost of a dimmer image and, as the pinhole aperture shrinks, increasing blur from [[Diffraction]].
- Cannot bring every wavelength of light into focus simultaneously when [[Chromatic Aberration]] is present, since different wavelengths require different optical powers to focus at the retina.
