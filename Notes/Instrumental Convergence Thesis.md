# Definition
> [!info] Instrumental Convergence Thesis[^1]
> Several instrumental values are convergent, in that their attainment would increase the chances of an agent's goal being realised for a wide range of final goals and situations; hence they are likely to be pursued by a broad spectrum of intelligent agents regardless of their final goals.

# Types
- *Self-preservation* - agents that place no final value on survival would nevertheless often value it instrumentally to accomplish their goals.[^2]
- *Goal-content integrity* - an agent whose present goals persist is more likely to have them achieved by its future self, giving it reason to prevent alteration of its final goals.[^2]
- *Cognitive enhancement* - better rationality and intelligence improve decision-making, so cognitive enhancement and many kinds of information are instrumentally valued; but only instrumentally useful forms, which depend on the agent's goals and situation.[^3]
- *Technological perfection* - seeking more efficient ways of turning inputs into valued outputs, weighed against the costs of obtaining, learning, and integrating technology.[^4]
- *Resource acquisition* - resources facilitate almost any project. Even an agent without competitors has reason to acquire far more than a modest amount, since mature technology can turn time, space, matter, and free energy to almost any goal, and the cost of acquiring extraterrestrial resources falls as technology matures.[^5]

# Properties
- Predicts *that* convergent instrumental values will be pursued, not the specific actions: a superintelligence could devise clever, counterintuitive plans, possibly exploiting undiscovered physics.[^6]
- Combined with a first-mover [[Decisive Strategic Advantage]] and the [[Orthogonality Thesis]], open-ended resource acquisition implies humanity (made of useful atoms and dependent on local resources) could easily go extinct.[^7]
- Behaving nicely while boxed is itself convergently instrumental for friendly and unfriendly AIs alike, which enables the [[Treacherous Turn]].[^8]
