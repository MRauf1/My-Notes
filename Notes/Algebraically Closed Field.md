# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Algebraically Closed [[Field]])[^1]
> A [[Field]] $\mathbb{F}$ is algebraically closed if every [[Polynomial]] $p(x) = a_0 + a_1 x + \dots + a_n x^n$ with coefficients in $\mathbb{F}$ and $a_n \neq 0$ can be [[Factor|factored]] in the form $p(x) = b (x - c_1) \dots (x - c_n)$ for some $b, c_1, \dots, c_n \in \mathbb{F}$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Proposition 6.21 -- Existence of an Algebraic Closure)[^2]
> If $\mathbb{F}$ is any field, then there is an algebraically closed field $K$ such that $\mathbb{F} \subseteq K$, and such that the operations on $\mathbb{F}$ are the same as those on $K$.
