# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Wheel [[Graph]])[^1]
> [[Graph]] that is like a [[Cycle Graph]] with an additional central [[Vertex]] that is connected to every other [[Vertex]].
> It is denoted as $W_n$ and has $n + 1$ [[Vertex]].
