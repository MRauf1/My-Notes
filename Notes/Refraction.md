# Definition

[[Light|Light]] changing direction when changing medium.[^1]

First described by [[Ptolemy|Ptolemy]].

# Properties
- Quantified by [[Snell's Law]], which relates the incident and refracted ray angles to the [[Index of Refraction|indices of refraction]] of the two materials.
- Underlies how a [[Lens]] bends light rays to focus them onto a sensor.
- Its wavelength dependence is what lets a prism separate white light into its component colors; [[Diffraction]] is a second, wave-based way to separate light into its spectral components.
