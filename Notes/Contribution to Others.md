# Definition
> [!info] Contribution to Others (Adlerian Psychology)[^1]
> Acting on the community, thinking not "What can others do for me?" but "What can I do for others?" It is the third ingredient of [[Community Feeling]] and the source of the [[Feeling of Contribution]], through which one becomes aware of one's own worth. It is not self-sacrifice.

# Properties
- **Not self-sacrifice.** Adler warns that people who sacrifice their lives for others have conformed to society too much.[^2]
- **Requires seeing others as comrades.** One feels contribution, for example in housework, because one sees one's family as comrades ([[Confidence in Others]]). The attitude itself changes the atmosphere and makes it easier for others to help.[^3]
- **The guiding star.** Adler: "Life in general has no meaning. Whatever meaning life has must be assigned to it by the individual." For the freedom-seeking person, the compass to keep in sight is contribution to others. Moving in that direction, there is happiness.[^4]
- Related: [[Prosocial Behavior]], [[Meaningful Life]], [[Existential Vacuum]], [[Self-Transcendence]].
