# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Probability Function|Probability]] Mass Function)[^1]
> For a [[Discrete Random Variable|discrete RV]] $X$, its [[Probability|probability]] $P(X = x)$ denoted by $f(x)$ or $f_X$ is the pmf of $X$.

# Properties
- [[Probability Mass Function Definitional Properties]]
- A pmf over $K$ classes is a $K$-dimensional vector with elements in $[0,1]$ that sum to $1$, i.e., a point on the $(K-1)$-[[Simplex]] $\Delta^{K-1}$.[^2]
