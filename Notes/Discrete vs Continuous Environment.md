# Definition
> [!info] Discrete vs. Continuous Environment[^1]
> A dimension of a [[Task Environment|task environment]] that applies to the state of the environment, to the way time is handled, and to the percepts and actions of the agent, according to whether each takes on a finite/countable set of values or a continuous range of values.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 64](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=64&annotation=62YXFKIQ)
