# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Integral]] [[Operator]])[^1]
> Let $k: \mathbb{R}^2 \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be [[Continuous Function]]. Then the integral operator $T_k: C[a, b] \rightarrow C[a, b]$ is defined by $T_k f(x) = \int_a^b k(x, y) f(x) dy$.
> The [[Function]] $k$ is called the (integral) [[Operator Kernel|kernel]] of the [[Operator]] $T_k$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=107)
