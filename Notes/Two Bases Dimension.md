# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Two [[Basis]] [[Dimension]])[^1]
> Suppose that $(v_1, \dots, v_m)$ and $(w_1, \dots, w_n)$ are both [[Basis]] of $V$. Then $m = n$.
