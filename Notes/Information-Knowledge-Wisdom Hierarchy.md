# Definition
> [!info] Information-Knowledge-Wisdom Hierarchy[^1]
> Information is not self-explanatory but context-dependent, meaningful only through the lenses of culture and history. When information is contextualised, it becomes [[Knowledge|knowledge]]; when knowledge compels convictions, it becomes wisdom.

# Properties
- Developing convictions historically required solitude for sustained reflection; the internet's flood of others' opinions deprives users of that solitude, and with diminished solitude comes diminished fortitude to form convictions and remain faithful to them on novel, lonely paths.[^1]
- Only convictions combined with wisdom enable people to access and explore new horizons.[^1]
- Digitisation paradoxically makes more information available while shrinking the space for deep, concentrated thought: the constant media stream raises the cost, and thus lowers the frequency, of contemplation (cf. [[Deep Work]]).[^2]
