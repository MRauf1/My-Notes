# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Rank]] [[Algorithm]])[^1]
> To find the [[Rank]] of [[Matrix]] $A$:
> 1) Put $A$ into [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]].
> 2) $rank(A)$ is the number of [[Pivot Variable]].
