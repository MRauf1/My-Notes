# Definition
> [!info] Deficiency and Growth Motivation[^1]
> ([[Abraham Maslow|Maslow]]) Two orientations of basic motivation. *Deficiency motivation* arises when basic psychological needs (safety, belongingness, identification, love, respect, prestige) went unmet early in life, and psychoneurosis is a deficiency disease. *Growth motivation* belongs to those whose needs are met, who are free to realise their potential and self-actualise, governed by inner rather than social determinants.

# Properties
- Growth-motivated people are less dependent on others' praise and rewards, may prefer privacy, and see others as whole beings; deficiency-motivated people relate to others by usefulness, as to "cows, horses, and sheep … waiters, taxicab drivers".[^2]
- Corresponding loves: **D-love** (selfish love-need, gratifiable, possessive) and **B-love** (unneeding love for the other's being: admiring, less jealous, more autonomous, fostering the other's growth); see [[Love]].[^3]
- Adverse development forces striving for safety rather than growth, producing [[Existential Guilt]].[^4]
- Related to [[Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs]].
