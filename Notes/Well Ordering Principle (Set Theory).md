# Definition
> [!info] Well Ordering Principle (Zermelo's Well-Ordering Theorem)[^1]
> Every nonempty [[Set]] $X$ can be [[Well-Ordered Set|well ordered]].

This is the general case of the [[Well Ordering Principle]] for $\mathbb{N}$: there the natural order happens to be a well ordering, whereas here an (in general non-constructive) well ordering is asserted to exist on any set, e.g. on $\mathbb{R}$.

# Properties
- Equivalent to the [[Axiom of Choice]], [[Zorn's Lemma]], and the [[Hausdorff Maximal Principle]].[^1]
- Makes [[Principle of Transfinite Induction|transfinite induction]] available on any set.
- Used to construct the [[Set of Countable Ordinals]].
