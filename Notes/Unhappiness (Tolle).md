# Definition
> [!info] Unhappiness (Tolle)[^1]
> "The primary cause of unhappiness is never the situation but your thoughts about it." The situation is always neutral; it is as it is. Unhappiness covers up your natural state of well-being and inner peace.

# Properties
- **Practice.** Acknowledge it without identifying with it: not "I'm unhappy" but "There is unhappiness in me", then investigate. Separate the fact from your thoughts about it, and stay with the facts instead of making up stories. See how your thinking creates your emotions, and be the awareness behind both.[^1]
- "Don't seek happiness." Seeking is its antithesis. Happiness is elusive, but freedom from unhappiness is available now by facing what is.[^2]
- Compare the cognitive model in [[The Philosophy of Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Stoic Philosophy as Rational and Cognitive Psychotherapy (Robertson)|CBT and Stoicism]]: it is not events but our judgments about them that disturb us.
- **Suffering's purpose.** Suffering is caused by identification with form and also erodes it. Eventually it burns up the ego, but only if you suffer consciously. "I shouldn't have to suffer" makes it worse; you must say yes to suffering to transcend it.[^3]
