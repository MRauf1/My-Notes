# Definition
> [!info] Community Feeling (Social Interest)[^1]
> ([[Alfred Adler|Adler]], *Gemeinschaftsgefühl*) Feeling that others are one's comrades and that one has a place in the community ("it's okay to be here"). It is the goal of interpersonal relations and the **objective of life** in [[Adlerian Psychology]]. Adler's "community" includes everything: family, school, nation, all of humanity across past and future, plants, animals and even inanimate objects. It is also called "social interest".

# Properties
- **Three ingredients.** [[Self-Acceptance]], [[Confidence in Others]] and [[Contribution to Others]]. Seeing others as comrades requires self-acceptance and confidence in others, but community feeling is reached only with contribution as well.[^2]
- **From attachment to self to concern for others.** People who cannot separate tasks and crave recognition are self-centered. The switch to concern for others is necessary ([[Desire for Recognition]]).[^3]
- **Life's protagonist, not the world's.** The "I" is the protagonist of its own life, but only a member and part of the community. Self-centered people jump to being "the world's protagonist" and treat others as "people who will do something for me". When others disappoint them, they see enemies and soon lose their comrades.[^4]
- **Belonging is earned.** A sense of belonging is a basic human desire. It is not given at birth but gained by actively committing to the community and facing one's [[Life Tasks]]: asking not "What will this person give me?" but "What can I give this person?"[^5]
- **Listen to the voice of the larger community.** Everyone belongs to larger communities, up to the earth and the universe, and is never truly alone. When a small community (school, workplace) is treated as everything, adversity there leaves no belonging at all, and people retreat into smaller ones (home) or violence. Look outside the teacup: there is always a larger community.[^6]
- Behavioral objective "live in harmony with society" and psychological objective "people are my comrades" correspond to confidence in others and contribution.[^7]
- Related: [[Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs]] (belonging), [[Self-Transcendence]], [[Prosocial Behavior]].
- **Innate, not acquired.** Social interest means concern for the people who make up society. Community feeling is inherent in everyone and tied to human identity. Just as there is no turtle without a shell, there is no human cut off from others. It is not *gained* but *dug up* from within, especially through friendship, where contribution is tested. Adler: "Community feeling is always a reflection of the weakness of the body." A place in the community is still earned by commitment, while the feeling itself is innate.[^kk1]
- **Belonging is the most fundamental need.** Nothing scares humans more than isolation, which threatens physical and mental security and leads to social and eventually biological death. Problem behavior seeks belonging through a *special* position ([[Five Stages of Problem Behavior]]).[^kk2]
- **Start with the person in front of you.** Mother Teresa, asked how to promote world peace: "Go home and love your family." Small daily acts of confidence in the person in front of you add up to ending conflict. To free the world from conflict, one first frees oneself from it, taking the first step as part of the whole.[^kk3]
- **Give first.** One is given to because one gives. Do not become "a beggar of the spirit".[^kk4]

# Evidence
- The need to belong is a fundamental human motivation. Its frustration harms health and adjustment (Baumeister & Leary, 1995).
