# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Similar Matrix]] [[Trace]])[^1]
> If $A, B$ are [[Similar Matrix]], then $tr(A) = tr(B)$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Corollary)[^1]
> If $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$ and $\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{B}'$ are any two [[Basis]] of $V$, then $tr([T]_{\mathcal{B}}) = tr([T]_{\mathcal{B}'})$.
