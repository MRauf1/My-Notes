# Definition
> [!info] Umbilic Point[^1]
> A point on a surface where the [[Principal Curvature|principal curvatures]] coincide, $\kappa_1 = \kappa_2$. There, the [[Normal Curvature]] is the same in every direction, so principal directions are not uniquely defined.

# Properties
- At an umbilic, the [[Shape Operator]] is a multiple of the identity: $S = \kappa\,\mathrm{Id}$, so $H^2 = K$ ([[Mean Curvature]], [[Gaussian Curvature]]).
- A connected surface consisting entirely of umbilics is part of a plane or a [[Sphere]].

[^1]: [Crane, p. 41](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9N6JDPI2?page=42&annotation=8E57JJG2)
