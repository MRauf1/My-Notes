# Biography

Survived the Nazi concentration camps, but lost all of his family except for his sister.

Creator of [[Logotherapy]].

Put big emphasis on a human's search for meaning in life, and argued that this desire for meaning shaped a lot of humans' behavior.

- Declined an American visa before deportation to stay with his parents; his father, mother, brother, and wife died in the camps, only his sister surviving. He was prisoner number 119,104, mostly digging and laying railway tracks, and worked as a doctor only in the last weeks.[^1]
- Wrote *Man's Search for Meaning*, whose autobiographical first part serves as the "existential validation" of logotherapy; see [[Psychology of Concentration Camp Prisoners]] and [[Last of the Human Freedoms]].[^2]
