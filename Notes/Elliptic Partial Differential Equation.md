# Definition
> [!info] Elliptic Partial Differential Equation[^1]
> The [[Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]] $\sum_{i,j} a_{ij} u_{x_i x_j} + \sum_i a_i u_{x_i} + a_0 u = 0$ is elliptic if the [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalues]] $d_1, \dots, d_n$ of its coefficient matrix $A$ are all positive or all negative. Equivalently, $A$ (or $-A$) is a [[Positive Definite Matrix]]. In two variables: $a_{12}^2 < a_{11} a_{22}$.

# Properties
- Canonical form $u_{x_1 x_1} + \cdots + u_{x_n x_n} + \cdots = 0$; prototype: the [[Laplace Equation]] (and Poisson equation).
- Has no time-like direction; models steady states and is naturally paired with [[Boundary Condition|boundary conditions]] only.
- See [[Canonical Form of Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]].
