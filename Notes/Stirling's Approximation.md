# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Stirling's Approximation)
> For large $n$,
> $$
> \begin{align}
> n! \approx \sqrt{2 \pi n} (\frac{n}{e})^n
> \end{align}
> $$

In terms of the [[Gamma Function]],[^2] $\Gamma(k + 1) \approx \sqrt{2\pi}\,k^{k + 1/2}e^{-k}$, an excellent approximation for large $k$, which shows how fast $k!$ grows.

# Properties
- In logarithmic form, $\ln N! \simeq N\ln N - N$, which is used to derive the [[Entropy]] of a macrostate from its multiplicity.[^1]
