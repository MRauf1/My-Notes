# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Coordinates]] n-Dimensional Space [[Linear Map Isomorphism]])[^1]
> Let $V$ be $n$-dimensional [[Vector Space]] over $\mathbb{F}$. Let $\mathcal{B}$ be a [[Basis]] for $V$. Then the [[Linear Map]] $C_{\mathcal{B}}: V \rightarrow \mathbb{F}^n$ defined by $C_{\mathcal{B}}(v) = [v]_{\mathcal{B}}$ is a [[Linear Map Isomorphism]].

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=206)
