# Definition
> [!info] Samatha Meditation[^1]
> (Concentration or tranquility meditation) [[Meditation]] in which the mind is brought to rest, focused on only one item and not allowed to wander. A deep calm then pervades body and mind.

# Properties
- Its deepest states of blissful relaxation are called *Jhana*. Most concentrative systems stop there.[^2]
- Its states are beautiful and alluring, but only temporary.[^1]
- It develops [[Samadhi]], which [[Vipassana Meditation]] uses as a tool for insight.[^3]
- Counterpart of [[Vipassana Meditation]]; both are described in [[Theravada Buddhism|Theravada]] literature.[^4]
