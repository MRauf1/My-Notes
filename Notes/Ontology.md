# Definition

The study of existence/being/what is real.[^1]

Ontology is a subfield of [[Metaphysics]]: metaphysics covers the broader nature of reality (causation, identity, time, possibility), while ontology narrows in specifically on what exists and how existing things are categorized.

[^1]: [Introduction to Philosophy](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/M84L5RRJ?page=185)
