# Definition
> [!info] Literature Note[^1]
> A short note made while reading, in your own words, recording what you don't want to forget or might use in your own thinking or writing, together with the bibliographic details. Literature notes are kept in a reference system, separate from the slip-box.

# Properties
- Write brief accounts of a text's main ideas instead of collecting quotes. Quoting skips the understanding; rephrasing is a translation from one context into another (Luhmann, Lonka).[^2]
- Good readers read with questions in mind and think beyond the frame of the text, its question and argument, instead of adopting it as given (see [[Elaboration]]).[^3]
- Part of the [[Smart Notes Workflow]]; feeds [[Permanent Note|permanent notes]].
