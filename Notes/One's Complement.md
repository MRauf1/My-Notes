# Definition
> [!info] One's Complement[^1]
> A signed-number notation that represents the most negative value as $10\ldots000_2$ and the most positive value as $01\ldots11_2$, leaving an equal number of negatives and positives but producing two representations of zero: a positive $00\ldots00_2$ and a negative $11\ldots11_2$. The term is also used for the bitwise inversion operation itself: flipping every 0 to 1 and every 1 to 0.

# Properties
- An alternative to [[Two's Complement]] for representing signed numbers; two's complement was chosen for most hardware instead because it has a single representation of zero.
