# Definition
> [!info] Exhaustive Events[^1][^2]
> A collection of [[Event|events]] $A_1, A_2, \dots$ is exhaustive if its union is the [[Sample Space]]: $\bigcup_n A_n = \mathcal{C}$.

A collection that is both [[Mutually Exclusive Events|mutually exclusive]] and exhaustive forms a [[Partition]] of $\mathcal{C}$: every outcome lies in exactly one $A_n$.

# Properties
- If $\{A_n\}$ is a partition, $\sum_n P(A_n) = 1$.
- Partitions are the setting of the [[Law of Total Probability]] and [[Bayes' Theorem]].
