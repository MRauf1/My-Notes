# Definition
> [!info] Percept and Percept Sequence[^1]
> A percept is the content an [[Agent|agent]]'s sensors are perceiving at a given instant. A percept sequence is the complete history of everything the agent has ever perceived.

# Properties
- An agent's choice of action at any instant can depend on its built-in knowledge and on the entire percept sequence observed to date, but not on anything it hasn't perceived.
- Specifying an agent's action for every possible percept sequence says everything there is to say about that agent; this mapping is the [[Agent Function|agent function]].

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 54](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=54&annotation=ZPRQC7R7)
