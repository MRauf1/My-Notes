# Definition
> [!info] Simplicial Manifold[^1]
> A (combinatorial or abstract) simplicial $n$-manifold is a pure simplicial $n$-complex ([[Abstract Simplicial Complex]]) in which the [[Simplicial Link|link]] of every vertex is a simplicial $(n-1)$-sphere.
>
> A **simplicial $n$-sphere** is a simplicial triangulation of the $n$-dimensional [[Sphere|sphere]]
> $$\begin{align}
> S^n := \{x \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1} : \|x\| = 1\}.
> \end{align}$$

This is the combinatorial counterpart of an $n$-dimensional [[Manifold]]: the closed star of each vertex is a combinatorial $n$-ball (see [[Topological Disk]] for the $n$-dimensional generalization of the disk).

# Types
- $n = 1$: the link of every vertex is a simplicial $S^0$ (a pair of points), so a simplicial 1-manifold is a disjoint collection of closed loops.
- $n = 2$: [[Simplicial Surface]]; every link is a simplicial $S^1$, i.e., a closed loop of edges.
- $n = 3$: a tetrahedral mesh in which every vertex is enclosed by a triangulated $S^2$.

# Properties
- $S^2$ is the ordinary unit sphere, $S^1$ the unit circle, and $S^0$ a pair of points.[^1]
