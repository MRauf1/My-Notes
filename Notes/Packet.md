# Definition
> [!definition] Packet[^1]
> A packet is the small chunk in which a computer transmits data over a network, consisting of a header — identifying information such as the origin, destination, and basic protocol — and a payload, the actual application data being sent.

# Properties
- Lets a host communicate with multiple others "simultaneously," since packets can be sent, received, and processed in any order regardless of where they came from or are going.[^2]
- Breaking a message into smaller packets also makes it easier to detect and compensate for transmission errors.[^2]
