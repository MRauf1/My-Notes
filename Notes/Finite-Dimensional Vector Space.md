# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Finite]]-[[Dimension]] [[Vector Space]])[^1]
> [[Vector Space]] $V$ is finite-dimensional if there is a finite list of [[Vector]] $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ in $V$ such that $V \subseteq \langle v_1, \dots, v_n \rangle$.

Notice that it follows immediately that $\langle v_1, \dots, v_n \rangle \subseteq V$ and hence it follows that $V = \langle v_1, \dots, v_n \rangle$.
