# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Antisymmetric [[Relation]])[^1]
> [[Relation]] $R: A \rightarrow A$ is antisymmetric if for all $x, y \in A$, $((x, y) \in R \land (y, x) \in R) \implies x = y$.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=203)
