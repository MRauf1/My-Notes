# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Nullity)[^1]
> The nullity of a [[Linear Map]] $T$ is $null(T) := dim(ker(T))$.
> The nullity of a [[Matrix]] $A$ is $null(A) := dim(ker(A))$.
