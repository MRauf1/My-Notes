# Definition
> [!info] Ultimate Rescuer[^1]
> The belief in a personal omnipotent intercessor, a force or being (a parent, therapist, partner, or God) that eternally observes, loves, and protects us and will ultimately rescue us from the abyss. One of the two basic defenses against [[Death Anxiety]], alongside [[Specialness (Existential Psychology)|specialness]].

# Properties
- Sought when belief in one's specialness fails to relieve pain.[^1]
- Less effective than specialness: more likely to break down, and intrinsically restrictive.[^2]
- Clinical forms: enfeebling oneself so that help from "out there" cannot be withheld, avoiding choice and growth so as to retain protection in perpetuity, "eluding death by refusing to live", and a "freeze and camouflage" suspension of living in the hope that death will overlook one.[^3]
- Therapy centres on analysis of transference; being one's own parent is frightening.[^4]
- Interdependent with specialness: because an omnipotent being cares for us we are special, and because we are special such forces care for us; "our ultimate rescuer is … our eternal servant."[^5]
- Overlaps with escaping [[Existential Isolation]] through fusion; decisions threaten it by forcing responsibility and isolation.[^6]
