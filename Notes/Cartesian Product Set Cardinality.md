# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Cartesian Product]] [[Set Cardinality]])
> For [[Set]] $A, B$, the cardinality of their cartesian product is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> |A \times B| = |A| \cdot |B|
> \end{align}
> $$

In counting this is called the mn-rule or multiplication rule: with $A = \{x_1, \dots, x_m\}$ and $B = \{y_1, \dots, y_n\}$ there are $mn$ ordered pairs $(x_i, y_j)$.[^1]

# Properties
- By induction, $|A_1 \times \dots \times A_k| = |A_1| \cdots |A_k|$; in particular a set of $n$ elements has $n^k$ $k$-tuples (repetition allowed).[^1]
- Restricting to $k$-tuples with distinct components gives $n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)$ [[Permutation (Combinatorics)|$k$-permutations]].

[^1]: [Introduction to Mathematical Statistics](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/P3TUBR4A?page=32)
