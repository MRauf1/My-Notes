# Definition
> [!info] Life Tasks[^1]
> ([[Alfred Adler|Adler]]) The interpersonal relationships a person cannot avoid in living in society, in three categories by increasing distance and depth: **tasks of work**, **tasks of friendship** and **tasks of love**. Facing them is how the objectives of [[Adlerian Psychology]] (self-reliance and harmony with society) are achieved.

# Properties
- **Love without restriction.** Relationships where people restrict each other eventually fall apart. A relationship that feels oppressive is not love, even with passion. Love is when "whenever I am with this person, I can behave very freely."[^2]
- One becomes an individual only in a social context, so self-reliance and cooperation are joint objectives.[^3]
- Belonging is gained by actively facing one's life tasks, not by merely existing. See [[Community Feeling]].[^4]
- Avoiding them with pretexts is the [[Life-Lie]]. Facing them is a matter of courage, not ability. See [[Encouragement (Adlerian Psychology)]].[^5]
- **Tasks of relationships.** The tasks concern the relationships tied to work, friendship and love, not the labor itself. **Work** relationships rest on trust (conditional), **friendship** on confidence (unconditional), and **love** is the hardest. A person who never embarks on friendship cannot find a place in the community. See [[Division of Labor (Adlerian Psychology)]].[^kk1]
- Seeking belonging only through work success, while avoiding friendship, comes from not liking oneself.[^kk2]
