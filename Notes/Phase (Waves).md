# Definition
> [!info] Phase (Waves)[^1]
> For a [[Sinusoidal Wave]] $u = A\cos\theta(x, t)$, the **phase** is the argument
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \theta(x, t) = kx - \omega t + \varphi_0,
> \end{align}
> $$
> which locates a point within the oscillation cycle. It is defined modulo $2\pi$: $\theta = 0$ at a crest, $\pi$ at a trough. The constant $\varphi_0$ is the **initial phase** (phase constant).

For a complex wave $\psi = |\psi|e^{i\theta}$, the phase is $\arg\psi$ ([[Argument (Complex Number)]]).

# Properties
- Points of equal phase form the [[Wavefront|wavefronts]].
- Two waves of the same frequency differ by a constant **phase difference** $\Delta\theta$, which decides their [[Interference (Waves)|interference]].
- Changing $\varphi_0$ is a [[Phase Shift|phase shift]].
- Phase is invisible to intensity detectors, since $|e^{i\theta}| = 1$. Only phase *differences* between superposed waves become visible as intensity.
