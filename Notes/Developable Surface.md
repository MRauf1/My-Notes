# Definition
> [!info] Developable Surface[^1]
> A surface with zero [[Gaussian Curvature]] everywhere, $K \equiv 0$. Such a surface can be "developed", i.e. flattened into the plane without stretching or tearing.

Precisely, by the Theorema Egregium, $K \equiv 0$ is equivalent to the surface being *locally* isometric to the plane (an [[Isometric Parameterization]] exists around each point); a global flattening may fail for topological reasons.

# Properties
- At every point at least one [[Principal Curvature]] vanishes.
- Smooth developable surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^3$ are (pieces of) ruled surfaces: planes, [[Cylinder|cylinders]], [[Cone|cones]], and tangent developables of space curves.
