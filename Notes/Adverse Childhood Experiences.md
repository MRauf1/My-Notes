# Definition
> [!info] Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs)[^1]
> Abuse, neglect and household dysfunction (e.g., domestic violence, substance abuse) before age 18. The ACE Study found a graded dose-response between the number of ACE categories and adult depression, suicide attempts, alcoholism, drug use, smoking, heart disease and cancer (Felitti et al., 1998).

# Properties
- **Toxic stress.** Strong, frequent or prolonged activation of the stress response without a supportive adult to buffer it. It can alter developing brain architecture and stress regulation (Shonkoff et al., 2012). Goggins describes repeated stress spikes resetting the baseline to permanent "fight or flight". His symptoms included a stutter, hair loss, social anxiety and learning difficulties.[^1]
- **Crime risk.** Goggins cites a 53% higher chance of juvenile arrest and 38% higher odds of violent crime.[^2] Prospective cohort studies of maltreated children (Widom) confirm elevated risks.
- **Buffering.** A stable, supportive adult is the main protective factor. For Goggins this was a teacher with a no-excuses yet caring approach.[^3]
