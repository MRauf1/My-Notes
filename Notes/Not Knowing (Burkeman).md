# Definition
> [!info] Not Knowing (Burkeman)[^1]
> Real wisdom lies not in getting life figured out but in grasping the sense in which you never will figure it out completely. At any moment we may not even understand what is happening or what a reasonable response would be. "Getting a handle on things" may be an obstacle to a fuller experience of life.

# Properties
- Since life is inherently confusing and precarious, joy, if it is to be found at all, must be found now, in the midst of the confusion.[^2]
- "Life is like playing a violin solo in public and learning the instrument as one goes on." (Samuel Butler)[^3]
- Living "merely for the sake of life, without justifications or achievements" after catastrophe is possible, and can even be generous to others.[^2]
- What separates people who accomplish great things from those who don't is not knowing what they're doing (nobody does) but not minding not knowing. "Everything they ever did was done by people," so you too can do or contribute to astounding things.[^4]
