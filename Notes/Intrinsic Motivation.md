# Definition

[[Motivation]] arising from internal factors.[^1]

"I have never found that pay and pay alone would either bring together or hold good people. I think it was the game itself" (Harvey Firestone).[^2] Herzberg's two-factor theory similarly held that the work itself motivates, while pay mainly prevents dissatisfaction. The two-factor model is only partly supported; the intrinsic-motivation part holds up better.
