# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Eigenvalue Bound by Operator Norm)[^1]
> If $\lambda \in \mathbb{C}$ is an [[Eigenvalue]] of $A \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$, then
> $$
> |\lambda| \leq \lVert A \rVert_{op}
> $$
