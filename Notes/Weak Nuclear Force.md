# Definition
> [!info] Weak Nuclear Force
> One of the [[Four Fundamental Interactions]]: unlike the other interactions, it does not bind matter together but instead changes one type of subatomic particle into another (e.g. changing a quark's "flavor"), driving processes such as radioactive beta decay and the fusion reactions that power stars.

# Properties
- Roughly a million times weaker than the [[Strong Nuclear Force]], though still far stronger than [[Gravitation]]; acts over an even shorter range (about $10^{-18}$ m) than the strong interaction.
- Mediated by the heavy W and Z bosons.
- Together with the electromagnetic interaction, unified theoretically into a single electroweak interaction at sufficiently high energy.
