# Definition

Continuous state of excessive, uncontrollable, and pointless worry and apprehension.[^1]

- Treatments: [[Worry Postponement]], [[Decatastrophizing]], [[Progressive Muscle Relaxation]]. 91.4% of the worries people with GAD record do not come true (LaFreniere and Newman, 2020).
- A core maintaining factor is [[Intolerance of Uncertainty]].
