# Definition
> [!info] Ultrahyperbolic Partial Differential Equation[^1]
> The [[Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]] $\sum_{i,j} a_{ij} u_{x_i x_j} + \sum_i a_i u_{x_i} + a_0 u = 0$ is ultrahyperbolic if none of the [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalues]] $d_1, \dots, d_n$ of its coefficient matrix $A$ vanish, but at least two are positive and at least two are negative.

# Properties
- Requires $n \geq 4$; e.g. canonical principal part $u_{x_1 x_1} + u_{x_2 x_2} - u_{x_3 x_3} - u_{x_4 x_4}$.
- Has more than one time-like direction; unlike the other three types it has no standard physical prototype.
- See [[Canonical Form of Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]].
