# Definition
> [!info] Whole Brain Emulation[^1]
> Also known as *uploading*: producing intelligent software by scanning and closely modelling the computational structure of a biological brain.

# Properties
- Requires no understanding of how human cognition works or how to program an AI, only of the low-level functional characteristics of the brain's basic computational elements; no fundamental conceptual or theoretical breakthrough is needed, though advanced enabling technologies (scanning, translation, simulation) are.[^1]
- One of the [[Paths to Superintelligence]].
- Emulations would by default carry human motivations, unlike neuromorphic AI, which draws on brain principles without human motivations.[^2]
- Even if emulations come first, a second transition to mature AI would remain, since mature AI renders emulation obsolete (except for preserving individual minds) but not vice versa.[^3]
- Rough compute: about $10^{18}$ ops/s per emulation, so $10^{27}$ ops for 100 subjective years; the cosmic endowment could thus host at least $10^{58}$ emulated human lives.[^4]
- In a [[Multipolar Scenario|multipolar]] economy, emulations could be copied and erased at will, raising the risk of [[Mind Crime]].
