# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Significant Digits)[^1]
> The significant digits of a number are its leading nonzero digit and all digits following it.

If an approximate value has a [[Relative Error]] of about $10^{-p}$, then its decimal representation has about $p$ correct significant digits.

[^1]: [Scientific Computing](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EP5UUXW5?page=27)
