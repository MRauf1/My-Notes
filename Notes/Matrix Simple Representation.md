# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Matrix]] Simple Representation)[^1]
> Let $V, W$ be [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space]] and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$. Then there are [[Basis]] $\mathcal{B}_V, \mathcal{B}_W$ of $V, W$ respectively, such that if $A = [T]_{\mathcal{B}_V, \mathcal{B}_W}$, then $a_{ii} = 1$ for $1 \leq i \leq rank(T)$ and all other entries are $0$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=211)
