# Definition
> [!info] Reward[^1]
> Part of an agent's incoming percept, distinguished by the [[Critic|critic]] as a reward (or penalty), that provides direct feedback on the quality of the agent's behavior with respect to a fixed performance standard.

# Properties
- Hard-wired performance standards, such as pain and hunger in animals, can be understood as reward/penalty signals in this sense.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 75](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=75&annotation=ACKXI58N)
