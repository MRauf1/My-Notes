# Definition
> [!info] Power[^1]
> $$P = \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{v}$$
> The rate at which a [[Force]] $\mathbf{F}$ delivers [[Work]] to an object moving with velocity $\mathbf{v}$.

# Properties
- Equal to the rate of change of [[Kinetic Energy]], by the [[Work-Energy Theorem]]: $P = dT/dt$.
- Measured in joules per second, called the watt (W).
- Multiplying power by a duration of time recovers the work done over that duration; e.g. a kilowatt-hour is a unit of work (energy), not of power.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 13: Work and Potential Energy (A)](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_13.html)
