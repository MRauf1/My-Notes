# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Homogeneity)[^1]
> In a contingency table with rows $i$ (groups) and columns $j$ (response categories), the rows are homogeneous if the conditional distribution of the response is the same in every row:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \pi_{j \mid i} = \pi_{j \mid i'} \quad \text{for all } i, i', j
> \end{align}
> $$
> For a $2 \times 2$ table with success probabilities $\pi_1, \pi_2$, homogeneity means $\pi_1 = \pi_2$: equivalently the [[Difference of Proportions|difference of proportions]] is $0$, the [[Relative Risk|relative risk]] is $1$, and the [[Odds Ratio|odds ratio]] is $1$.
