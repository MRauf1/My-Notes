# Definition
> [!info] Self-Transcendence[^1]
> A source of meaning that transcends self-interest, striving toward something or someone outside or "above" oneself (as in altruism, causes, and creativity), in contrast to self-directed meanings such as hedonism and self-actualisation.

# Properties
- Buber: begin with oneself but not end with oneself; "What for? … Not for my own sake"; excessive concern with personal salvation may lose it.[^1]
- Frankl: excessive self-actualisation thwarts meaning; like a boomerang that returns only when it misses, or an eye that sees itself only when it cannot see outward; "happiness cannot be pursued, it can only ensue".[^2]
- Maslow: truly self-actualised people are not preoccupied with self-expression but dedicate themselves to self-transcendent goals.[^3]
- Meanings evolve over the life cycle toward self-transcendence ([[Generativity]]).[^4]
- Basis of [[Dereflection]]; a type of [[Cosmic and Terrestrial Meaning|terrestrial meaning]].
