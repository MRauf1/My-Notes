# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Cumulative Function|Cumulative]] Distribution Function)[^1]
> For a [[Random Variable]] $X$, its cdf is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> F_X(x) = P(X \leq x)
> \end{align}
> $$
> The [[Derivative]] of cdf is the [[Probability Mass Function]]/[[Probability Density Function]] assuming $f_X$ is [[Continuous Function]].
