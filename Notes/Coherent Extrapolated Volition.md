# Definition
> [!info] Coherent Extrapolated Volition[^1]
> Yudkowsky's proposed final goal for a seed AI: humanity's coherent extrapolated volition is "our wish if we knew more, thought faster, were more the people we wished we were, had grown up farther together; where the extrapolation converges rather than diverges, where our wishes cohere rather than interfere; extrapolated as we wish that extrapolated, interpreted as we wish that interpreted."

# Properties
- Interpretation of the clauses:[^2]
	- *Thought faster* - were smarter and had thought things through more.
	- *Grown up farther together* - had learned, enhanced, and self-improved under suitable social interaction.
	- *Converges rather than diverges* - act only on features of the extrapolation predictable with high confidence, refraining from wild guesses while still enforcing apprehensible broad outlines.
	- *Cohere rather than interfere* - act where there is broad agreement among individuals' extrapolated volitions; the initial dynamic "should be conservative about saying 'yes,' and listen carefully for 'no.'"
	- *Extrapolated/interpreted as we wish* - the extrapolation rules should themselves respect second-order desires (desires about what to desire).
- Meant to be robust and self-correcting, capturing the *source* of our values rather than requiring us to enumerate them once and for all.[^3]
- Rationales: encapsulate moral growth; avoid hijacking the destiny of humankind; avoid creating a motive for present-day humans to fight over the initial dynamic; keep humankind ultimately in charge of its destiny.[^4]
- Those excluded from the extrapolation base are not disregarded if the base's CEV extends moral consideration to them, though their interests may be weighted less; and CEV reduces but does not eliminate motives for conflict.[^5]
- The central example of [[Indirect Normativity]].
