# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Vector]] [[Orthogonal Vector]] Representation)[^1]
> Given $v, w \in V$, $v$ can be written as $v = aw + u$, where $a \in \mathbb{F}$ and $u \in V$ is [[Orthogonal Vector]] to $w$. Moreover, if $w \neq 0$, then we can take $a = \frac{\langle v, w \rangle}{\lVert w \rVert^2}$ and $u = v - \frac{\langle v, w \rangle}{\lVert w \rVert^2} w$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=251)
