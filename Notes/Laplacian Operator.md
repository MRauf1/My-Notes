# Definition
> [!info] Laplacian Operator[^1]
> The [[Linear Partial Differential Operator]] on $\mathbb{R}^n$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \Delta = \nabla^2 = \nabla \cdot \nabla = \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_1^2} + \cdots + \frac{\partial^2}{\partial x_n^2},
> \end{align}
> $$
> i.e. $\Delta u = \operatorname{div} \operatorname{grad} u$, the [[Divergence of Vector Field|divergence]] of the [[Gradient Vector|gradient]].

# Properties
- In 2D, $\Delta u = u_{xx} + u_{yy}$; in 3D, $\Delta u = u_{xx} + u_{yy} + u_{zz}$.
- Appears in the [[Wave Equation]], [[Diffusion Equation]], [[Laplace Equation]], and [[Schrödinger Equation]].
- Its zeros are the [[Harmonic Function|harmonic functions]].
- Invariant under rotations and translations of $\mathbb{R}^n$.
