# Definition
> [!info] Evaporation[^1]
> The continual, molecule-by-molecule escape of atoms from a liquid's surface into an adjoining gas, occurring when a surface molecule happens to receive, through random collisions, more than the average energy needed to break free of the attraction of its neighbors.

# Properties
- A statistical, microscopic process consistent with the [[Atomic Hypothesis]].
- Balanced by the reverse process of molecules returning from the gas to the liquid; together the two constitute a [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] when their rates are equal.
- Produces [[Evaporative Cooling]], since the escaping molecules carry away above-average kinetic energy.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 1: Atoms in Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_01.html)
