# Definition

Just like a craftsman gaining meaning from crafting a work of art, so too can you gain meaning through [[Deep Work]] as a result of what you produce.[^1]

# Properties
- **Philosophical argument (Dreyfus and Kelly).** The Enlightenment made the individual responsible for deciding what is meaningful, which risks nihilism. The craftsman's task "is not to generate meaning, but rather to cultivate in himself the skill of discerning the meanings that are already there", which gives a glimpse of the sacred.[^2]
- Any pursuit, physical or cognitive, that supports high skill can produce this. "A wooden wheel is not noble, but its shaping can be": you need not a rarified job but a rarified approach to your work. Cultivating craftsmanship is itself a deep task.[^3]
- Psychological argument: [[Experience Sampling Method|ESM]] and [[Flow State|flow]]. Neurological argument: [[Attention]] constructs your world.
