# Definition
> [!info] Indirect Normativity[^1]
> An approach to choosing a superintelligence's final values in which, rather than specifying a concrete normative standard directly, one specifies a process for deriving a standard and builds the system to carry out that process and adopt its result, offloading to the superintelligence some of the reasoning needed to select the values.

# Types
- [[Coherent Extrapolated Volition]] (CEV) - carry out humanity's idealised, extrapolated wishes.
- *Moral rightness* (MR) - do what is morally right, relying on the AI to determine which actions fit that description, with a fallback specified in case moral realism is false. Removes CEV's free parameters, but might not give us what we want, which could be extremely harmful.[^2]
- *Moral permissibility* (MP) - among morally permissible actions, take one humanity's CEV would prefer; undergo a controlled shutdown if the instruction lacks a well-specified meaning, we are radically confused about it, moral realism is false, or creating the AI was itself impermissible. MR and MP risk sacrificing our (potentially vastly extended) lives for a greater good.[^3]
- *"Do what I mean"* - do whatever we would have had most reason to ask the AI to do, sparing us the risk of choosing between more specific objectives.[^4]

# Properties
- Motivated by the *principle of epistemic deference*: a future superintelligence occupies an epistemically superior vantage point, so we should defer to its opinion whenever feasible.[^1]
- Beyond goal content, one must also choose the AI's decision theory and epistemology, possibly indirectly (e.g. an interim decision theory $D'$ governing the search for the ideal $D$); epistemology may converge benignly since abundant evidence washes out moderate differences in priors.[^5]
- It is wiser to minimise the risk of catastrophic error than to maximise the chance of every detail being optimised: the cosmic endowment is large enough to tolerate some waste, and a reliable, self-correcting design landing in the right "attractor basin" may home in on our objectives.[^6]
- One of the [[Motivation Selection Methods]].
