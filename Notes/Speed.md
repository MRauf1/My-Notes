# Definition
> [!info] Speed[^1]
> The magnitude of the [[Velocity Vector]]:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> v = |\mathbf{v}|
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- A scalar quantity, unlike [[Velocity Vector|velocity]], which also carries direction; in one-dimensional motion the two coincide in magnitude, which is why "speed" and "velocity" are often used interchangeably when direction is not in question.
- Defined only as an instantaneous quantity, in the limit of a vanishingly small time interval: dividing distance traveled by a finite time interval gives only the average speed over that interval, which can differ substantially from the instantaneous speed if the motion is not uniform during it.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 8: Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_08.html)
