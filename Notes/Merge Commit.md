# Definition
> [!info] Merge Commit[^1]
> A merge commit is the commit [[Git]] automatically creates to record the snapshot produced by a [[Three-Way Merge]]; it is distinguished from an ordinary commit by having more than one parent commit.

[^1]: [Pro Git](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Y53JUJJW?page=82&annotation=L3R8VM38)
