# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Fragment Blending)[^1]
> The final stage of the [[Graphics Pipeline]]: combining the (possibly several) [[Fragment|fragments]] generated by primitives that overlap the same pixel into that pixel's final color, producing the framebuffer image.

# Properties
- Most commonly implemented as [[Hidden Surface Removal]] by choosing the color of the fragment with the smallest depth (closest to the eye), via the [[Z-Buffer Algorithm]].

[^1]: [Fundamentals of Computer Graphics](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/7B6A4MRC?page=187&annotation=LCWRCR8N)
