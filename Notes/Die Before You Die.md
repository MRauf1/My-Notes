# Definition
> [!info] Die Before You Die[^1]
> The spiritual practice of meditating deeply on the mortality of all physical forms, including your own. Death strips away everything that is not you; to "die before you die" is to let go now of identification with form and find that "there is no death" for what you essentially are.

# Properties
- Approaching death, and death itself, is a great opportunity for spiritual realization.[^2]
- A related practice: "Die to the past every moment." Refer to the past only when it is absolutely relevant to the present.[^3]
- Compare the Stoic practice of *memento mori* ([[Stoic View of Death]]).
