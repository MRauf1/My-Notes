# Definition
> [!info] Creativity (Connections)[^1]
> The ability to recognize relationships, make associations and connections, and see things in an original way (Andreasen, 2014). "Creativity is just connecting things… they didn't really do it, they just saw something." (Steve Jobs)

# Properties
- **Abstraction.** Engineers who are better at abstracting from a problem find more creative *and* more practical solutions, even for the original problem (Gassmann and Zeschky, 2008). Abstraction is key to comparing concepts, making analogies, and combining ideas, especially across disciplines (Goldstone and Wilensky, 2008).[^2]
- **Oppositions.** Constructing oppositions is the most reliable way to generate new ideas (Albert Rothenberg).[^3]
- **Familiarity breeds openness.** Counterintuitively, the more familiar we are with existing ideas, the more open we are to new ones (Rheinberger). Artists who spent more time exploring an aesthetic problem produced solutions later judged more creative (Getzels and Csikszentmihalyi, 1976).[^4]
- **Virtuosity.** Intimate familiarity with a problem and with your tools is the precondition for discovering their possibilities. Intuition is not opposed to rationality; it is "sedimented experience" on which explicit knowledge builds (see [[Intuition]]).[^5]
- Constraints help rather than hurt (see [[Paradox of Choice]]). Related: [[Taste (Creativity)]], [[Flexible Focus]].
