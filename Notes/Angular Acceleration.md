# Definition
> [!info] Angular Acceleration[^1]
> The rate of change of [[Angular Velocity]] of a rotating body:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \alpha = \frac{d\omega}{dt} = \frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2}.
> \end{align}
> $$
> In three dimensions, $\boldsymbol{\alpha} = d\boldsymbol{\omega}/dt$.

# Properties
- Rotational analog of linear [[Acceleration Vector|acceleration]] $a = dv/dt$; measured in rad/s².
- For a [[Rigid Body]] rotating about a fixed axis, the net external [[Torque]] produces angular acceleration via $\tau = I\alpha$, where $I$ is the [[Moment of Inertia]] — the rotational analog of [[Newton's Second Law]].

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 18: Rotation in Two Dimensions](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_18.html)
