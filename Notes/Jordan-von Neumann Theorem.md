# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Jordan-von Neumann Theorem)
> [[Normed Space]] is a [[Inner Product Space]] (its [[Norm]] comes from an [[Inner Product]]) if and only if for every pair of [[Vector]] $x, y$, the [[Parallelogram Identity]] is true.

The [[Polarization Identity]] gives the explicit formula for the inner product that must be recovered from the norm.
