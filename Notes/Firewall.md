# Definition
> [!definition] Firewall[^1]
> A firewall is a software and/or hardware configuration, usually sitting on a [[Router|router]] between the Internet and a smaller network, that tries to keep harmful traffic out by filtering packets.

# Properties
- Filters packets it receives, sends, or forwards, typically based on criteria such as source/destination address, source/destination port, or network interface.[^2]
