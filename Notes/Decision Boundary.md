# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Decision Boundary)[^1]
> The boundary in input space separating regions that a classifier assigns to different classes.

# Properties
- For a [[Perceptron]], training by minimizing a classification loss corresponds geometrically to shifting and rotating the decision boundary until it separates the differently-labeled data.

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Neural Networks](https://visionbook.mit.edu/neural_nets.html)
