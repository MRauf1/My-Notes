# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Parent [[Vertex]])[^1]
> In a [[Tree]], when 2 [[Vertex]] are connected, the one closest to the [[Root Vertex]] is called the parent vertex, and the other [[Vertex]] is its [[Child Vertex]].
