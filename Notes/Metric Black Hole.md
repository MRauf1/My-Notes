# Definition
> [!info] Metric Black Hole (Newport)[^1]
> As knowledge work grows more complex, the value of an individual's effort, and the bottom-line cost of depth-destroying behaviors, become hard to measure. They fall into an opaque region resistant to measurement.

# Properties
- Because the costs are invisible, it shields the [[Principle of Least Resistance]] and [[Busyness as Proxy for Productivity]] from scrutiny, pushing workplaces toward [[Shallow Work]] even though the economy rewards depth.[^2]
- Even short interruptions significantly lengthen total task completion time, a cost that is rarely measured.[^3]
- Experiment (Perlow, Boston Consulting Group): consultants given one fully disconnected day per week didn't lose clients. They reported more enjoyment, better communication, more learning, and "a better product delivered to the client."[^4]
- Business trends such as serendipity, fast responses, and exposure promise benefits arguably dwarfed by those of deep work.[^5]
