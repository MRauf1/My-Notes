Identity is not fixed; it can grow and is constantly changing. Tomorrow's identity may be different from yesterday's.[^1] This is consistent with evidence that personality traits keep changing across adulthood (Roberts, Walton and Viechtbauer, 2006).

Clear advises not to let any single identity dominate, so that losing one role doesn't mean losing yourself.[^1] The closest research is the self-complexity hypothesis: having many distinct self-aspects buffers against stress (Linville, 1987). A meta-analysis found the buffering effect weak and inconsistent (Rafaeli-Mor and Steinberg, 2002), so treat this as plausible rather than established.
