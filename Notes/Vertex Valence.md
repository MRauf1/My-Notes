# Definition
> [!info] Vertex Valence[^1]
> The valence of a vertex in a [[Combinatorial Surface]] is the number of edges that contain that vertex. A vertex of a [[Simplicial Surface]] is **regular** when its valence equals $6$ (otherwise it is *irregular*).

# Properties
- Analogue of the [[Degree of Vertex|degree of a vertex]] in a [[Graph]].
- Valence $6$ is the valence of every vertex in the regular triangular tiling of the plane, which is why it is called regular.
