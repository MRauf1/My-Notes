# Definition

Subfield of [[Mechanics]] that deals with describing motion.[^1]

# [[Vector]]
- [[Position Vector]]
- [[Velocity Vector]]
- [[Acceleration Vector]]

# Properties
- [[Position Vector|Position]], [[Velocity Vector|velocity]], and [[Acceleration Vector|acceleration]] form a chain of successive [[Derivative|derivatives]] with respect to time; equivalently, by the [[Fundamental Theorem of Calculus]], velocity is the [[Antiderivative]] of acceleration and position is the antiderivative of velocity — see [[Equations of Motion for Constant Acceleration]] for the constant-acceleration case.
- The problem of precisely defining instantaneous [[Velocity Vector|velocity]] — as the limit of distance traveled over time, as the time interval shrinks to zero — was the original motivation for Newton and Leibniz to independently invent differential calculus.
