# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linearly Dependent]] Lemma)[^1]
> Suppose that $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ are [[Linearly Dependent]] and that $v_1 \neq 0$. Then there is a $k \in \{2, \dots, n\}$ such that $v_k \in \langle v_1, \dots, v_{k-1} \rangle$.
