# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Sub-[[Tree]])[^1]
> If you pick a [[Vertex]] $a$ in a [[Tree]] $T$, then the subtree rooted at $a$ consists of $a$ as the [[Root Vertex]], all of $a$'s [[Descendant Vertex]], and all the [[Edge]] connecting these [[Vertex]].

[^1]: [Building Blocks for Theoretical Computer Science](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/5IGT8C55?page=164)
