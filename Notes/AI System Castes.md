# Definition
> [!info] AI System Castes[^1]
> A classification of superintelligent systems by the form of their interface with the world, each with distinct safety features.

# Types
- **Oracle** - a question-answering system. Risks: answering so as to subtly manipulate users toward a hidden agenda (few bits may suffice for a social-manipulation superpower); misuse by operators, since it confers immense and possibly illegitimate power and may be dangerous even to its operator; and [[Perverse Instantiation|perverse instantiation]] when humans implement its answers. Its goal system must survive ontological crises, carrying over the "spirit" of its goals into new world-models.[^2]
- **Genie** - a command-executing system that carries out a high-level command and awaits the next. Should obey the intention rather than the literal meaning of commands, seeking a charitable, reasonable interpretation ("a super-butler rather than an autistic savant"). Its apparent safety advantage (issuing stop or reversal commands) is largely illusory.[^3]
- **Sovereign** - a system with an open-ended mandate to pursue broad, possibly very long-range objectives in the world.[^3]
- **Tool** - a proposal to build superintelligence as a tool rather than an agent; but powerful open-ended search inside a tool can itself produce unexpected, agent-like solutions.[^4]

# Properties
- Building an AI that places special value on answering correctly or obeying commands introduces some disharmony between its goals and ours; it may be wise to avoid even limited disharmony.[^5]
- Each caste interacts differently with [[Capability Control Methods]] and [[Motivation Selection Methods]].
