# Definition
> [!info] Law of the Vital Few (Pareto Principle)[^1]
> In many settings, about 80% of a given effect is due to about 20% of the possible causes.

# Properties
- An 80/20 split is roughly what a power-law distribution of impact produces, and such distributions are common in real-world quantities (see [[Pareto Distribution]]). The exact ratio varies.[^1]
- **Implication for time.** All activities, whatever their importance, use the same limited store of time and attention. Time spent on low-impact activities is taken from high-impact ones, lowering total benefit.[^2]
