# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Divergence of [[Vector Field]])[^1]
> If $F = Pi + Qj + Rk$ is a [[Vector Field]] on $\mathbb{R}^3$ and the below [[Partial Derivative]] exist, then the divergence of $F$ is a [[Function]] of 3 variables defined as
> $$
> \begin{align}
> div(F) &= \frac{\partial P}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial Q}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial R}{\partial z} \\
> &= \nabla \cdot F
> \end{align}
> $$
