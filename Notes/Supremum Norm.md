# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 (Supremum Norm)[^1]
> The supremum norm on $C([0,1])$ is defined by
> $$
> \lVert f \rVert_\infty := \max_{0 \leq x \leq 1} |f(x)|
> $$

This is the functional analog of the [[Infinity Norm]] on $\mathbb{R}^n$/$\mathbb{C}^n$.
