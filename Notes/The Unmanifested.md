# Definition
> [!info] The Unmanifested (Tolle)[^1]
> The formless, timeless Source of all existence, from which the world of form ("the manifested") arises as the energy of *chi*. It is not separate from the manifested: it is present in the world as silence and as space. It only liberates when it is entered consciously.

# Types
Portals into the Unmanifested (ultimately all one):[^2]
- **The Now**: the main portal and an essential part of every other one.
- **The [[Inner Body]]**.
- **Cessation of thinking**, which happens automatically in intense presence.
- **Surrender** ([[Surrender (Spirituality)]]).
- **Silence**: every sound is born from silence, surrounded by it, and dies back into it. Attending to outer silence creates inner stillness.
- **Space**: you cannot think and be aware of space at the same time, so attending to space is attending to the space of no-mind.
- **Physical death**: an involuntary portal that opens briefly. Tolle connects it with the radiant light of near-death reports and says it is usually missed by those who have not met the Unmanifested in life.

# Properties
- We enter it unconsciously every night in deep dreamless sleep. Lucid dreaming is possible but not liberating.[^3]
- Space and silence are two aspects of the same "nothing", the externalization of inner stillness. Physical bodies are mostly empty space. This echoes the Heart Sutra: "Form is emptiness, emptiness is form" (see [[Sunyata]]).[^4]
- "Nothing" cannot be grasped. The moment the mind makes it into something, it is missed.[^5]
- Silence exists for us only because of sound, and space only because of objects. Likewise the world is needed for the Unmanifested to be realized: it "knows itself" through the world and through you.[^6]
- Time is linked with the manifested as the timeless Now is linked with the Unmanifested.[^7]
- Love is not a portal but what comes through one, as the "feeling-realization" of oneness.[^8]
