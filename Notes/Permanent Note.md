# Definition
> [!info] Permanent Note[^1]
> A note in the [[Zettelkasten|slip-box]] that develops one idea, written in full sentences "as if you were writing for someone else", so it can be understood without its original context. It is made by turning [[Fleeting Note|fleeting]] and [[Literature Note|literature notes]] into thoughts relevant to your own research and interests. The aim is not to collect but to develop ideas, arguments, and discussions.

# Properties
- **One idea per note**, linked to related notes and reachable from the index.[^1]
- **Built-in feedback.** Writing a thought out shows whether you really thought it through. Combining it with earlier notes exposes contradictions, inconsistencies, and repetitions. It is a form of self-testing that is always available (see [[Virtuous Circle of Learning]]).[^2]
- Writing a permanent note is translation, not copying: "No written piece is ever a copy of a thought in our mind."[^3]
- Treating *every* note as permanent dilutes good ideas so the slip-box never reaches critical mass.[^4]
- Part of the [[Smart Notes Workflow]].
