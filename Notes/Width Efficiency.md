# Definition
> [!info] Width Efficiency[^1]
> There exist classes of wide [[Shallow Neural Network|shallow networks]] that can only be expressed by narrow networks of polynomial depth (Lu et al., 2017).

# Properties
- The polynomial lower bound here is less restrictive than the exponential lower bound on width in [[Depth Separation|depth efficiency]], suggesting that depth matters more than width.
- For [[ReLU Function|ReLU]] networks, the price of making the width small is only a linear increase in depth (Vardi et al., 2022).
