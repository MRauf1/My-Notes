# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Orthogonal Decomposition Theorem)[^1]
> Let $V$ be an [[Inner Product Space]]. If $U$ is a finite-dimensional subspace of $V$, then every vector $v \in V$ can be uniquely written in the form
> $$
> \begin{align}
> v = u + w
> \end{align}
> $$
> where $u \in U$ and $w \in U^\perp$.

# Properties
- Equivalently, $V = U \oplus U^\perp$, i.e. $V$ is the [[Orthogonal Direct Sum]] of $U$ and its [[Orthogonal Complement]].
- The vector $u$ in the decomposition is the [[Orthogonal Projection]] of $v$ onto $U$.
