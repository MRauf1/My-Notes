# Definition
> [!info] Algorithmic Echo Chamber[^1]
> The self-reinforcing filtration of information in cyberspace: algorithmic personalisation of search, streaming, news, and books amplifies some subjects and sources and, as a practical necessity, omits others entirely, creating personal echo chambers and fomenting discordance between them.

# Properties
- Since what each person consumes (and assumes reflects reality) differs from what others consume, individuals drift into different perceived realities.[^1]
- Technology once expected to transcend national differences and disperse objective truth may become the means by which civilisations and individuals diverge into mutually unintelligible realities.[^2]
- Part of [[AI-Driven Societal Transformation]].
