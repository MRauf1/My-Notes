# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Change of Basis Matrix]] [[Inverse]])[^1]
> Let $\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{B}'$ be two [[Basis]] of $V$, and let $S$ be the [[Change of Basis Matrix]] from $\mathcal{B}$ to $\mathcal{B}'$. Then the [[Change of Basis Matrix]] from $\mathcal{B}'$ to $\mathcal{B}$ is $S^{-1}$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=220)
