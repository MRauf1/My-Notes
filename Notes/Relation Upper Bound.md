# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Upper Bound)[^1]
> Let $R: A \rightarrow A$, $B \subseteq A$, $a \in A$. Then $a$ is the upper bound for $B$ if for all $x \in B, xRa$.

> [!info] Definition 2 (Upper Bound for a Strict Partial Order)[^2]
> Let $\prec$ be a [[Strict Partial Order]] on $A$ and $B \subseteq A$. An upper bound on $B$ is an element $c \in A$ such that for every $b \in B$, either $b = c$ or $b \prec c$.

[[Relation Largest Element]] of $B$ is the upper bound of $B$.
