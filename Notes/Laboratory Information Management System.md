# Definition
> [!info] Laboratory Information Management System[^1]
> Software providing a pipeline from instrument or simulation data into a data archive: data are calibrated and cleaned (including filling gaps), re-gridded, loaded into a database, and published on the Internet for access.

# Properties
- Gray called for generic, Beowulf-like LIMS packages and templates letting wet-lab scientists collect, store, and publish data; commercial LIMS exist but are expensive, industrial, and specific to particular instruments or communities.[^2]
- A *database* fundamentally means that data is self-describing and has a schema ([[Database (Computing)]]).[^3]
- A basic tool for the capture and curation stages of [[Data-Intensive Science]].
