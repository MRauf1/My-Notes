# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Change of Basis Matrix]] to [[Standard Basis]])[^1]
> Let $\mathcal{E}$ be the [[Standard Basis]] of $\mathbb{F}^n$, and let $\mathcal{B}$ be any other [[Basis]] of $\mathbb{F}^n$. Then the columns of the [[Change of Basis Matrix]] $S = [I]_{\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{E}}$ are the column/[[Basis]] [[Vector]] in $\mathcal{B}$, in order.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=220)
