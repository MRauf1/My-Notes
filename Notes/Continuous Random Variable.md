# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Continuous]] [[Random Variable|Random Variable]])[^1]
> Random variable $X$ whose [[Universe of Discourse|outcome space]] $S$ has a [[Uncountably Infinite]] number of elements.
> Its [[Cumulative Distribution Function]] is [[Continuous Function]] for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$.
