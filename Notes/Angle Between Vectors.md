# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Angle]] Between [[Vector]])[^1]
> The [[Angle]] between 2 [[Vector]] $x, y$ is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \theta = \cos^{-1}(\frac{x \cdot y}{\lVert x \rVert \lVert y \rVert})
> \end{align}
> $$

It is related to [[Dot Product]], [[Vector Norm]], [[Cosine Similarity]].
