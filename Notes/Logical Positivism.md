# Definition
> [!info] Logical Positivism[^1]
> The doctrine, developed by the Vienna Circle, that all knowledge can be characterized by logical theories connected, ultimately, to observation sentences that correspond to sensory inputs. It thereby combines rationalism and [[Empiricism|empiricism]].

# Properties
- The [[Confirmation (Epistemology)|confirmation theory]] of Carnap and Hempel extends this program by quantifying the degree of belief that should be assigned to logical sentences based on their connection to observations that confirm or disconfirm them.
