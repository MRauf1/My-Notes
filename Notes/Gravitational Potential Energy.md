# Definition
> [!info] Gravitational Potential Energy[^1]
> $$U = mgh$$
> The energy an object of mass $m$ possesses by virtue of its height $h$ above a reference level, in a uniform gravitational field of strength $g$; equivalently, since weight $W = mg$ is itself the force of gravity, $U = Wh$.

# Properties
- A special case of [[Potential Energy]] in general: $\Delta U = F \cdot d$, with the gravitational force (the weight) playing the role of $F$.
- Valid only near a planet's surface, where $g$ is approximately constant; breaks down at large heights, where [[Gravitation|gravitational attraction]] weakens with distance.
- Converts into [[Kinetic Energy]] as an object falls, and back as it rises, under [[Conservation of Energy]].

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 4: Conservation of Energy](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_04.html)
