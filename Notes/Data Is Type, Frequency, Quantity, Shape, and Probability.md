# Definition
> [!info] Data Is Type, Frequency, Quantity, Shape, and Probability[^1]
> The second founding principle of [[Data-Oriented Design]]: data is not just its structure — understanding data means also understanding its type, frequency, quantity, shape, and statistical/probabilistic qualities.

# Properties
- Corrects the common misconception that [[Data-Oriented Design]] is only about avoiding cache misses or splitting hot and cold data apart within a class; structuring data this way is a generally useful technique, but only one aspect of the data, not the whole of it.

[^1]: [Data-Oriented Design](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/QRB7VVMF?page=15&annotation=33MG4TAT)
