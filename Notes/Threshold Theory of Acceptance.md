# Definition
> [!info] Threshold Theory of Acceptance[^1]
> Agent $A$ rationally [[Acceptance|accepts]] $H$ if and only if $A$'s [[Credence|degree of belief]] in $H$ is greater than some threshold $t$.

# Properties
- Conflicts with [[Acceptance Closure]] whenever two hypotheses each individually exceed $t$ while their conjunction falls below $t$, as exhibited by the [[Lottery Paradox]].
- Raising $t$ does not resolve this conflict, since for any $t < 1$ a large enough lottery can be constructed where each ticket's losing credence exceeds $t$ while their conjunction does not.
- The version with $t = 1$ is the [[Certainty Theory of Acceptance]].

[^1]: [A Critical Introduction to Formal Epistemology](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9XYCZDPF?page=28)
