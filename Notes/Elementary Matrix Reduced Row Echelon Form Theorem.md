# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Elementary Matrix]] [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]])[^1]
> Given any $A \in M_{m, n}(\mathbb{F})$, there is a [[Matrix]] $B \in M_{m, n}(\mathbb{F})$ in [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]] and [[Elementary Matrix]] $E_1, \dots, E_k \in M_m(\mathbb{F})$ such that $B = E_k \dots E_1 A$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=124)
