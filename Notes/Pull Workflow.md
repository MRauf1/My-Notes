# Definition

The worker pulls in work to his plate once they are ready.

Various experiments have shown that pull workflows can be more efficient and effective than [[Push Workflow]].[^1]


# Properties
- In a pull-based process, backlogs are impossible: the pipeline's pace adapts to its slowest stage, which exposes where the system is out of balance. At the Broad Institute, sequencer usage more than doubled and average sample processing time fell by more than 85%. A technology group's work in progress fell by almost 50% while its completion rate rose.[^2]
- **Personal pull system.** Keep a *holding tank* of projects and an *active list* of at most three. Schedule time only for active projects, and pull a new one in when a slot frees up. You can simulate this without others noticing. Be transparent about what you will deliver, even if promises change. A little friction slows incoming work.[^3]
- A tool for doing fewer things in [[Slow Productivity]].
