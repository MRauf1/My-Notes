# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Theorem 6.17 -- Leibniz Formula)[^1]
> For $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$,
> $$
> \det A = \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} (\text{sgn}\,\sigma) \, a_{1,\sigma(1)} \cdots a_{n,\sigma(n)} = \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} (\text{sgn}\,\sigma) \, a_{\sigma(1),1} \cdots a_{\sigma(n),n}
> $$
