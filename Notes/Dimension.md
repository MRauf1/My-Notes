# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Dimension of [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space]])[^1]
> Let $V$ be [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space]].
> 1) If $V \neq \{0\}$, then the dimension of $V$, written $dim(V)$, is the length of any [[Basis]] of $V$. If $dim(V) = n$, we say that $V$ is $n$-dimensional.
> 2) If $V = \{0\}$, then we define the dimension of $V$ to be 0.
