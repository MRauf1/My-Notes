# Definition
> [!info] Inner and Outer Purpose (Tolle)[^1]
> Life's journey has two purposes:
> - **Outer purpose**: arriving at a goal or destination, which implies the future.
> - **Inner purpose**: the quality of consciousness at this moment. It concerns *how* you do things, not where you are going or what you are doing.

# Properties
- If the destination takes up more attention than the step you are taking now, you miss the inner purpose. The outer journey may have a million steps; the inner journey has only one, the present step.[^1]
- You can fail completely at the outer purpose and succeed fully at the inner one, or the reverse, which is more common.[^2]
- Every outer purpose eventually "fails" because of impermanence (cf. [[Anicca]]). Seeing this, you stop expecting it to make you happy and make it subservient to the inner purpose. Once the inner purpose is realized, the outer one becomes a game you may keep playing because you enjoy it.[^3]
- "How" matters more than "what". Joylessness in an activity may only require changing *how* you do it, giving more attention to the doing than to the result (cf. [[Karma Yoga]], [[Means to an End]]).[^4]
- Free of [[Psychological Time|psychological time]], goals are pursued neither with grim determination nor avoided out of fear of failure. Neither happiness nor sense of self depends on the outcome: "Everything is honored, but nothing matters."[^5]
- Developed further in Chapter 9 of *A New Earth* ([[A New Earth (Tolle)]]).
- *A New Earth*: inner purpose concerns Being and is primary; outer purpose concerns doing, can change over time, and is secondary, though the two are intertwined. "Your inner purpose is to awaken." Living in line with it is the foundation for the outer purpose.[^6]
- Awakening is a shift in consciousness in which thinking and awareness separate. For most people it is a process, not an event.[^7]
- Meaning sought only in doing and the future is relative and temporary, and is destroyed by time. Meaning from helping or winning depends on others being worse off or failing.[^8]
- Anxiety and stress arise when you turn away from the present moment because something else seemed more important, i.e., when you forget your primary purpose.[^9]
- Loss, old age, or tragedy can collapse the outer purpose and crack the ego's shell, revealing the inner purpose and then a deeper outer purpose aligned with it.[^10]
- Full treatment of the "how": [[Awakened Doing]].[^11]
