# Definition
> [!info] Two Core Spiritual Insights (Tolle)[^1]
> Tolle claims that beneath their surface differences, most ancient religions and spiritual traditions agree on two insights:
> 1. The "normal" state of the human mind contains a strong element of dysfunction or even madness.
> 2. The good news: a radical transformation of human consciousness is possible.

# Properties
- Fear, greed, and the desire for power drive war and conflict between nations and in personal relationships. They are not the dysfunction itself but products of it: a deep-seated collective delusion in every human mind. Science and technology have magnified its destructive impact.[^2]
- The founders' teachings were distorted even as disciples wrote them down, so religions became largely divisive rather than unifying. The more you make beliefs (thoughts) your identity, the more cut off you are from the spiritual dimension; many "religious" people are stuck at that level.[^3]
- "Thinking without awareness is the main dilemma of human existence." Thinking is only a tiny part of the consciousness we are.[^4]
- "If you can recognize illusion as illusion, it dissolves."[^5]
- Tolle frames humanity's choice as "Evolve or die" and predicts that rigid egoic structures (institutions, corporations, governments) will collapse from within, most rigid first.[^6]
- He further claims that a "new heaven" (transformed consciousness) will be mirrored by a "new earth", including "synchronistic" geographic and climatic upheavals. This is a metaphysical claim with no empirical support.[^7]
- Everything, even a stone, has unfathomable depth and is ultimately unknowable. We perceive only the surface, and underneath, everything is connected with everything else and with the Source.[^8]
- Contemplating a flower, crystal, or bird alertly and without naming it opens a window into the formless. With enough presence, one can sense the same essence in every life form and love it as oneself.[^9]
