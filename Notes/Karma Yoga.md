# Definition
> [!info] Karma Yoga[^1]
> In Hindu teaching (notably the *Bhagavad Gita*), the "path of consecrated action": acting with non-attachment to the fruit of one's action. Attention goes to the action itself, and the result "will come of its own accord".

# Properties
- Without dependence on the future for fulfillment, neither failure nor success can change one's inner state.[^2]
- Tolle's version of it is giving full attention to the doing rather than the result (see [[Inner and Outer Purpose]], [[Means to an End]]).[^1]
