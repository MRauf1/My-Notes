# Definition
> [!info] Ion[^1]
> An atom that has either gained a few extra electrons or lost a few electrons.

# Properties
- Carries a net electric charge, unlike a neutral atom.
- Participates in [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] processes such as dissolution and precipitation, where ions leave and rejoin a solid without changing partners.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 1: Atoms in Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_01.html)
