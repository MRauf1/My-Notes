# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Limit of Function]] Basic Properties)[^1]
> Assume that $\lim_{x \rightarrow a} f(x), \lim_{x \rightarrow a} g(x)$ both exist and $\lim_{x \rightarrow a} g(x) \neq 0$. Then
> - $\lim_{x \rightarrow a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \frac{\lim_{x \rightarrow a} f(x)}{\lim_{x \rightarrow a} g(x)}$

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 ([[Limit of Function]] on [[Metric Space]])[^2]
> Let $E \subseteq X$, a [[Metric Space]], $p$ a [[Limit Point]] of $E$, and $f, g$ [[Complex-Valued Function|complex functions]] on $E$ with $\lim_{x \rightarrow p} f(x) = A$ and $\lim_{x \rightarrow p} g(x) = B$. Then
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \lim_{x \rightarrow p} \left(\frac{f}{g}\right)(x) = \frac{A}{B} \quad \text{if } B \neq 0
> \end{align}
> $$
