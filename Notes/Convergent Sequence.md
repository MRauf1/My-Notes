# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Convergent Sequence)
> [[Sequence]] is convergent if a limit exists.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Cauchy Criterion in $\mathbb{R}$)[^1]
> A [[Sequence]] of [[Real Number|real numbers]] is convergent if and only if it is a [[Cauchy Sequence]].

The equivalence relies on the [[Completeness Axiom]] of $\mathbb{R}$ and does NOT hold in an arbitrary [[Metric Space]]:
- In any [[Metric Space]], convergent $\implies$ Cauchy ([[Convergent Sequence is Cauchy Sequence]]).[^2]
- Cauchy $\implies$ convergent holds exactly when the space is a [[Complete Metric Space]].[^2]
