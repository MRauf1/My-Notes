# Definition
> [!info] Carol Dweck[^1]
> Carol S. Dweck, American psychologist regarded as one of the leading researchers in personality, social psychology, and developmental psychology; originator of the theory of [[Mindset|mindsets]] ([[Fixed Mindset|fixed]] versus [[Growth Mindset|growth]]).

# Properties
- Her work belongs to a tradition in psychology showing the power of people's beliefs, conscious or not, over what they want and whether they get it, and how changing even simple beliefs can have profound effects.[^2]
- She reports having held a fixed mindset herself: believing herself more talented and worthy than others, dreading being ordinary, and needing constant validation on intelligence, attractiveness, and likability "scorecards".[^3]
- Known for [[Mindset (Dweck)]].
