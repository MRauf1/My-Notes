# Definition
> [!info] Principle of Induction[^1]
> David Hume's principle that general rules are acquired through exposure to repeated associations between their elements.

# Properties
- An [[Empiricism|empiricist]] account of how knowledge of general rules arises from experience rather than from reason alone.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 24](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=24&annotation=2R6DU2LD)
