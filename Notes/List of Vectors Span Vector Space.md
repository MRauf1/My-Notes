# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (List of [[Vector]] [[Span]] [[Vector Space]])[^1]
> A list of [[Vector]] $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ in $\mathbb{F}^m$ [[Span|spans]] $\mathbb{F}^m$ if and only if the [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]] of $\begin{bmatrix}\mid & & \mid \\ v_1 & \dots & v_n \\ \mid & & \mid \end{bmatrix}$ has a [[Pivot Variable]] in every row.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=137)
