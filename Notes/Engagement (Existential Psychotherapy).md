# Definition
> [!info] Engagement (Existential Psychotherapy)[^1]
> Wholehearted immersion in any of life's activities, the therapeutic answer to meaninglessness regardless of its source: it disarms the detached "galactic" view and enhances one's ability to pattern life's events coherently. The therapist cannot create engagement but removes obstacles to a desire to engage that is always present.

# Properties
- **The galactic (nebula's-eye) view**: seen from cosmic distance, striving is absurd; Schopenhauer's pessimism concluded "nothing is worth our striving".[^2]
- Antidotes: Nagel, if nothing matters then it should not matter that nothing matters, so return to life "laced with irony"; Bach, the view renders things meaningless only while one holds it; "when things matter, they don't need meaning to matter".[^3]
- Current doubt does not vitiate the reality of past mattering.[^4]
- Pure meaninglessness is best approached obliquely: "One must immerse oneself in the river of life and let the question drift away" (the Buddha's view that the question is not edifying). Meaninglessness is interwoven with leisure and disengagement.[^5]
- Therapy for [[Existential Vacuum]]; see [[Cosmic and Terrestrial Meaning]].
