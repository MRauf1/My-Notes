# Definition
> [!info] Pattern Recognition[^1]
> The automatic discovery of regularities in data through computer algorithms, and the use of these regularities to take actions such as classifying the data into categories.

Pattern recognition grew out of engineering, whereas [[Machine Learning|machine learning]] grew out of computer science; the two are best viewed as two facets of the same field.[^1]

# Properties
- The central goal is [[Generalization|generalization]]: the training data covers only a tiny fraction of all possible inputs, so the learned function must correctly handle inputs that differ from the training examples.[^2]
- Raw inputs are usually transformed by [[Feature Extraction|feature extraction]] into a space in which the problem is easier to solve.
- Divides into [[Supervised Learning]], [[Unsupervised Learning]], and [[Reinforcement Learning]].
- Typically decomposed into an inference stage (learning probabilities) and a decision stage ([[Decision Theory]]); see [[Three Approaches to Decision Problems]].
