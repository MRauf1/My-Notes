# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Thin Lens Approximation)[^1]
> The assumption, commonly used in analyzing imaging systems, that a lens has negligible thickness compared with the other distances along the optical axis.

# Properties
- Used together with the [[Paraxial Approximation]] to derive the [[Lensmaker's Equation]] describing how a [[Lens]] focuses light.
- Under this approximation, both parabolic and spherical lens surface shapes satisfy the surface condition required for focusing.

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Lenses](https://visionbook.mit.edu/lenses.html)
