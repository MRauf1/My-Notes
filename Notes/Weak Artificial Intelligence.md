# Definition
> [!info] Weak Artificial Intelligence[^1]
> The view that the principal value of the computer in the study of the mind is that it gives us a very powerful tool, e.g. for formulating and testing hypotheses about the mind in a rigorous and precise way, without claiming that the computer itself has a mind.

# Properties
- Contrasted with [[Strong Artificial Intelligence]]; Searle has no objection to weak AI.
- Compatible with the [[Chinese Room Argument]], which only targets the claim that running a program is sufficient for a mind.

[^1]: [Searle, p. 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/F89IHIZA?page=2&annotation=3XLPXMVX)
