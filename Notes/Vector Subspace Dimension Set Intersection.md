# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Vector Subspace]] [[Dimension]] [[Set Intersection]])[^1]
> Suppose that $U_1, U_2$ are [[Vector Subspace]] of [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space]] $V$, and that $dim(U_1) + dim(U_2) > dim(V)$. Then $U_1 \cap U_2 \neq \{0\}$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=185)
