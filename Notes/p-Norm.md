# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 (p-Norm)[^1]
> For any $p \geq 1$, the $p$-norm on $\mathbb{R}^n$ or $\mathbb{C}^n$ is
> $$
> \lVert x \rVert_p = \left( \sum_{j=1}^n |x_j|^p \right)^{1/p}
> $$

# Types
- [[1-Norm]] ($p = 1$)
- [[Vector Norm]] (the 2-norm $\lVert x \rVert_2$, which comes from the standard [[Dot Product]]/[[Inner Product]] on $\mathbb{R}^n$ or $\mathbb{C}^n$)
- [[Infinity Norm]] (the limiting case $p \to \infty$)
