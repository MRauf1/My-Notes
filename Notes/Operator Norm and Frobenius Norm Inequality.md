# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Operator Norm and Frobenius Norm Inequality)[^1]
> If $A \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{C})$, then
> $$
> \lVert A \rVert_{op} \leq \lVert A \rVert_F \leq \sqrt{n} \lVert A \rVert_{op}
> $$
