# Definition
> [!definition] System-on-a-Chip (SoC)[^1]
> A system-on-a-chip (SoC) is a design that crams a processor, memory, and peripheral interfaces into a single small space, enabling small, capable hardware for [[Embedded System|embedded systems]].

[^1]: [How Linux Works: What Every Superuser Should Know](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/B4TILA8A?page=374&annotation=J4S76J3W)
