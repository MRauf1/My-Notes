# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Matrix]] [[Conjugate]] Transpose)[^1]
> [[Matrix Transpose]] but for [[Complex Number]].
> $$
> \begin{align}
> [B^*]_{ij} = \overline{b_{ji}}
> \end{align}
> $$
> If working with [[Real Number]], then it is equivalent to [[Matrix Transpose]].
