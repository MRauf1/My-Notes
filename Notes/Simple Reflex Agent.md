# Definition
> [!info] Simple Reflex Agent[^1]
> An [[Agent Program|agent program]] that selects actions on the basis of the current percept alone, ignoring the rest of the percept history.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 67](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=67&annotation=4ZW8MVXR)
