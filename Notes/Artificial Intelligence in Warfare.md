# Definition
> [!info] Artificial Intelligence in Warfare[^1]
> The use of AI in military strategy and operations, whose capacity for autonomy and separate logic introduces a layer of incalculability into conflict.

# Properties
- Traditional strategy assumes a human adversary whose decision calculus fits a recognisable framework shaped by experience; an AI piloting aircraft or scanning for targets follows its own logic, possibly inscrutable, unsusceptible to traditional signals and feints, and faster than human thought.[^1]
	- *Creator's note*: effectively game theory against an AI rather than a human opponent.
- Traditional conflict aims at the adversary's psychology, but an algorithm knows only its instructions and objectives, not morale or doubt.[^2]
- AI already exceeds human capability in some domains, e.g. AI pilots outperforming humans in simulated dogfights (DARPA AlphaDogfight) with manoeuvres beyond human pilots.[^3]
- Unlike previous weapons, AI is simultaneously dual-use, easily spread, and potentially substantially destructive.[^4]
- A form of the weaponisation concern in [[Ethical Concerns of Artificial Intelligence]].
