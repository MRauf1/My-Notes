# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Lens)[^1]
> A specially shaped piece of transparent material positioned to focus light from a surface point onto a sensor. An ideal lens has the property that every light ray from a given surface point that passes through the lens is refracted onto a common position at the sensor, no matter what part of the lens the ray passes through.

# Properties
- Overcomes the poor light-gathering ability of a [[Pinhole Camera]]: widening a pinhole's [[Aperture]] lets in more light but also allows light from many surface positions to land on a given sensor position, producing a bright but blurry image, whereas a lens can capture more light while still focusing each sensor position to a single surface point.
- Bends light rays according to [[Snell's Law]] as they refract through the lens surface.
- Analyzed under [[Geometric Optics]], together with the [[Paraxial Approximation]] and [[Thin Lens Approximation]].
- The relationship between a lens's shape and its focusing behavior is given by the [[Lensmaker's Equation]].
