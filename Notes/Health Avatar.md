# Definition
> [!info] Health Avatar[^1]
> The electronic representation of an individual's health, directly measured or inferred by statistical models or clinicians: a unified model to which the person's health data from multiple sources are attached.

# Properties
- Lets clinicians see an integrated view of different specialists' treatment plans, more than care records alone.[^1]
- Starts as a simple multi-system model and grows in depth to narrow the gap between avatar and reality; it is a unified statistical model of current clinical understanding applied to the patient, not a molecular-level simulation (deemed implausible).[^2]
- Extends to communities, where individual avatars interact with a community avatar modelling population health.[^3]
- The central proposal of [[Data-Intensive Healthcare]].
