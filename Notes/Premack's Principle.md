# Definition
> [!info] Premack's Principle[^1]
> More probable behaviors reinforce less probable behaviors (David Premack, 1959). Access to a preferred activity can be made contingent on a less preferred one ("first work, then play").

# Properties
- Well established in operant research and widely used in behavior therapy and classroom management.
- Basis of [[Temptation Bundling]].
- Related: [[Operant Conditioning]], [[Law of Craving]].
