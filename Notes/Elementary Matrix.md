# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Elementary [[Matrix]])[^1]
> [[Matrix]] corresponding to [[Row Operations]].
> These [[Matrix]] are always [[Square Matrix]] and always have [[Matrix Inverse]].

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Lemma 6.14 -- Determinant of Elementary Matrices)[^2]
> Let $1 \leq i, j \leq n$ with $i \neq j$ and $c \in \mathbb{F}$. Let $P_{c,i,j}$ be the elementary matrix for adding $c$ times row $j$ to row $i$ (R1), and let $R_{i,j}$ be the elementary matrix for swapping rows $i$ and $j$ (R3). Then:
> - $\det(P_{c,i,j}) = 1$
> - $\det(R_{i,j}) = -1$
