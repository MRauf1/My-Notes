# Definition
> [!info] Epistemic Possibility[^1]
> A [[Proposition|proposition]] $H$ is possible for an agent at a time when their [[Credence|credence]] in $H$ is greater than $0$; $H$ is impossible for the agent when their credence in $H$ is $0$.

# Properties
- Relative to an agent at a time, unlike other notions of possibility, since it is defined in terms of [[Credence|credence]], which is itself agent- and time-relative.

[^1]: [A Critical Introduction to Formal Epistemology](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9XYCZDPF?page=10)
