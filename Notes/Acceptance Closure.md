# Definition
> [!info] Acceptance Closure[^1]
> If agent $A$ rationally [[Acceptance|accepts]] $H_1$ and rationally accepts $H_2$, then $A$ should accept $H_1 \& H_2$.

# Properties
- Conflicts with the [[Threshold Theory of Acceptance]], since [[Credence|credence]] in each of two hypotheses individually is typically greater than the credence in their conjunction, i.e. $P(H_1) > P(H_1 \& H_2)$ and $P(H_2) > P(H_1 \& H_2)$, as exhibited by the [[Lottery Paradox]].

[^1]: [A Critical Introduction to Formal Epistemology](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9XYCZDPF?page=28)
