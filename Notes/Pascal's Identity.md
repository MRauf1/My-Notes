# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Pascal's Identity)
> For $n, k \in \mathbb{N}$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> {n \choose k} = {n-1 \choose k} + {n-1 \choose k-1}
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- Equivalent to $b_n = b_{n-1} \circ [1, 1]$, the recursion generating the [[Binomial Filter]].
