# Review[^1]

A freely available textbook made by UIUC's Systems Programming class. In the version that I read, the textbook is very much a rough draft with a lot of typos, spelling mistakes, lacking citations, and what not. The textbook explains concepts of systems programming, but focuses on the implementation of the basic versions of those concepts, while telling the reader what the more complex and modern variants are like.

[^1]: [Systems Programming](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/8Y3AE875?page=1)
