# Definition
> [!info] Acceptance[^1]
> Technical concept of all-or-nothing belief in a [[Proposition|proposition]] $H$, treated as a coarse-grained version of [[Credence|degree of belief]].

# Properties
- An agent can only stand in one of three relations to $H$: accept $H$, accept not-$H$, or withhold acceptance.
- Accepting $H$ eliminates all competing hypotheses inconsistent with $H$ from consideration.
- Some probabilists reject acceptance as a useful concept for epistemology, holding it should be replaced by [[Credence|degree of belief]].

[^1]: [A Critical Introduction to Formal Epistemology](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9XYCZDPF?page=28)
