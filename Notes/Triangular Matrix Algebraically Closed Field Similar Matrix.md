# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Upper Triangular Matrix]] [[Lower Triangular Matrix]] [[Algebraically Closed Field]] [[Similar Matrix]])[^1]
> Suppose that $V$ is a [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space]] over an [[Algebraically Closed Field]] $\mathbb{F}$, and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Then there is a [[Basis]] $\mathcal{B}$ of $V$ such that $[T]_{\mathcal{B}}$ is [[Upper Triangular Matrix]]/[[Lower Triangular Matrix]].
> Equivalently, suppose that $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$ and $\mathbb{F}$ is [[Algebraically Closed Field]]. Then $A$ is [[Similar Matrix]] to some [[Upper Triangular Matrix]]/[[Lower Triangular Matrix]].

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=239)
