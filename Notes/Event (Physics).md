# Definition
> [!info] Event[^1]
> A point $(x,y,z,t)$ in [[Spacetime]]: a definite position occurring at a definite time, the basic "location" about which the geometry of spacetime is built.

# Properties
- Plays the role in spacetime that a point plays in ordinary space; a physical object, occupying position over an interval of time, traces out a continuous sequence of events rather than a single one.
- The [[Spacetime Interval]] between two events, unlike the ordinary distance between two points, can be positive, negative, or zero, and this sign classifies the causal relationship between the events (see [[Light Cone]]).

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 17: Space-Time](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_17.html)
