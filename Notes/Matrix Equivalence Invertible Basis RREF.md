# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Matrix]] Equivalence [[Invertible Linear Map]] [[Basis]] [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]])[^1]
> Let $A \in M_{m, n}(\mathbb{F})$. The following statements are equivalent:
> 1) $A$ is [[Invertible Linear Map]].
> 2) The columns of $A$ are a [[Basis]] of $\mathbb{F}^m$.
> 3) The [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]] of $A$ is $I_m$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=178)
