# Definition
> [!info] Symbolic Immortality[^1]
> (Lifton) Culturally pervasive modes by which humans seek to transcend death symbolically:
> 1. **Biological** - living on through one's progeny.
> 2. **Theological** - living on in a higher plane of existence.
> 3. **Creative** - living on through one's works or impact on others.
> 4. **Eternal nature** - rejoining the life forces of nature.
> 5. **Experiential transcendence** - "losing oneself" in a state so intense that time and death disappear (a "continuous present").

# Properties
- A form of death transcendence driven by [[Death Anxiety]].[^1]
- Becker: our universal ambition is continued experience, pursued also by "counting", mattering, or leaving something behind; much of the quest for meaning is contaminated by this.[^2]
