# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Reflexive Relation]] [[Closure]])[^1]
> Let $R$ be a [[Relation]] on [[Set]] $A$. The reflexive closure of $R$ is the smallest set $S \subseteq A \times A$ such that $R \subseteq S$ and $S$ is a [[Reflexive Relation]]. In other words,
> 1) $R \subseteq S$
> 2) $S$ is [[Reflexive Relation]]
> 3) For every [[Relation]] $T \subseteq A \times A$, if $R \subseteq T$ and $T$ is [[Reflexive Relation]], then $S \subseteq T$.
> A reflexive closure for a [[Relation]] $R$ always exists and is unique.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=216)
