# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Big Theta)[^1]
> Given two [[Function]] $f, g$, if $f(n)$ is $O(g(n))$ and $g(n)$ is $O(f(n))$, then $f(n)$ is $\Theta(g(n))$ (and vice versa).

It's a tighter bound than [[Big O]].
