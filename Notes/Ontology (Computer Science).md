# Definition
> [!info] Ontology (Computer Science)[^1]
> A formal, machine-readable specification of concepts and terms in a domain and the relations between them, expressed in standardised semantic representation languages (e.g. of the semantic Web).

# Properties
- Data integration, fusion, and mining; workflow orchestration; provenance, lineage, and data quality; and validation and trust all require semantic representation and mediation for science to become fully data-intensive.[^1]
- Linking data and literature inevitably leads to ontologies, schemas, and controlled vocabularies, i.e. questions of what things mean, on which communities disagree.[^2]
- Distinct from philosophical [[Ontology]], though it borrows the idea of categorising what exists; see also [[Knowledge Representation (Artificial Intelligence)]].
