# Definition
> [!info] Barrier (Synchronization)[^1]
> A synchronization primitive that keeps a multi-stage, multi-threaded computation from advancing to its next stage before every participating thread has finished the current one: a thread that reaches the barrier waits there until all other threads have also reached it, and only then do they all proceed together.

[^1]: [Systems Programming](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/8Y3AE875?page=171&annotation=Q5EWB46L)
