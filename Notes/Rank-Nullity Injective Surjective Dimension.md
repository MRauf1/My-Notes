# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Rank-Nullity Theorem]] [[Injective Function]] [[Surjective Function]] [[Dimension]])[^1]
> Let $T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$.
> 1) If $dim(V) > dim(W)$, then $T$ cannot be [[Injective Function]].
> 2) If $dim(V) < dim(W)$, then $T$ cannot be [[Surjective Function]].

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=200)
