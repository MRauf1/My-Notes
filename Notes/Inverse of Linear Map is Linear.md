# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Invertible Linear Map]])[^1]
> If $T$ is [[Linear Map]] and [[Invertible Linear Map]], then $T^{-1}$ is also [[Linear Map]] and [[Invertible Linear Map]].
> Likewise, its [[Matrix]] also has an [[Matrix Inverse]].

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=99)
