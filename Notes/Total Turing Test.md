# Definition
> [!info] Total Turing Test[^1]
> An extension of the [[Turing Test]] that requires a robot to interact with objects and people in the real world, rather than only exchanging written responses with an interrogator.

# Properties
- In addition to the four capabilities required by the [[Turing Test]], passing the total Turing test requires [[Computer Vision|computer vision]] and speech recognition to perceive the world, and robotics to manipulate objects and move about.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 20](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=20&annotation=EQ85UG3J)
