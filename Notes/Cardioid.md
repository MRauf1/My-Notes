# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Cardioid)[^1]
> A cardioid is a [[Limacon]] with $|a| = |b|$, i.e. the graph in the [[Polar Coordinate System]] of
> $$
> \begin{align}
> r = a\big(1 \pm \cos(\theta)\big) \quad \text{or} \quad r = a\big(1 \pm \sin(\theta)\big), \qquad a \neq 0
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- Heart-shaped, with a cusp at the origin (where $r = 0$) and maximum distance $2|a|$ from the origin.
- Boundary case between inner-loop and dimpled limaçons.
