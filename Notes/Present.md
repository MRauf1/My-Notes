# Definition

...

Live in the present and use the past and the future only when needed to. Things always happen in the present. Past is a former now. Future is an imagined now.[^1]

Treat every moment as if it is the last moment that you'll get to experience.[^2]

See [[Psychological Time]] for Tolle's full account of the Now.

**Trying to be present (Burkeman).** The effort to "live in the moment" is itself instrumental: it treats the present as a means to an enriching experience, and so obscures the experience. It implies you are separate from the moment, when that moment is all you are.[^3]

**Day-tight compartments (Carnegie).** Following Osler: "Shut off the future as tightly as the past". The best preparation for tomorrow is "doing today's work superbly today." This is compatible with planning: "By all means take thought for the tomorrow… But have no anxiety."[^4] For the evidence-based version, see [[Worry Postponement]] and [[Decatastrophizing]].
