# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Strict [[Partial Order]])[^1]
> A [[Relation]] $R$ is a strict partial order if it is [[Irreflexive Relation]] and [[Transitive Relation]].

[[Antisymmetric Relation]] property is implied by the definition.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=218)
