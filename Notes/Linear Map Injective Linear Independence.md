# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linear Map]] [[Injective Function]] [[Linearly Independent]])[^1]
> Suppose that $T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$ is [[Injective Function]] and that $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ are [[Linearly Independent]] in $V$. Then $(T(v_1), \dots, T(v_n))$ is [[Linearly Independent]] in $W$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=168)
