# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Curl of Vector Field]] of [[Gradient Vector Field]])[^1]
> If $f$ is a [[Function]] with [[Continuous Function]] second-order [[Partial Derivative]], then
> $$
> \begin{align}
> curl(\nabla f) = 0
> \end{align}
> $$

If $F$ is a [[Conservative Vector Field]], then its [[Curl of Vector Field]] is 0.

[^1]: [Calculus: Early Transcendentals](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EEFDQ9Y5?page=1136)
