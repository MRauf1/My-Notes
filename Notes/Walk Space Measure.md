# Definition
> [!info] Walk Space Measure[^1]
> The measure $\mu_{\text{walk}}$ given by the product of [[Area Measure|area measures]] along each step of a path; used to integrate over random walks of any length.

# Properties
- An instance of a [[Measure (Measure Theory)|measure]].
