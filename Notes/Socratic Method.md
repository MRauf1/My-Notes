# Definition

Method of [[Socrates]] characterized by asking others questions about their beliefs rather than explaining one's own beliefs.[^1]

By asking questions and examining the other person's claims, the method assists the other person in discovering the truth on their own (a process of self-discovery).

Also called *elenchus* ("testing", "refutation"). Insight often comes from working through unresolved intellectual conflicts, and to change someone's philosophy of life one must show that their beliefs aren't in their own best interest. CBT calls its questioning "Socratic", usually without recognising that Stoicism was the main sect of Socratic therapeutics ([[Hellenistic Philosophy]], [[Cognitive Therapy]]).[^rob1]
