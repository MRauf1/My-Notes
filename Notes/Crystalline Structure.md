# Definition
> [!info] Crystalline Structure[^1]
> The atomic arrangement characteristic of a solid, in which atoms are fixed in an ordered array such that the position of an atom on one side of the crystal is determined by the positions of atoms arbitrarily far away on the other side — unlike a liquid, whose atoms lack this long-range positional order.

# Properties
- Distinguishes solids from liquids within the [[Atomic Hypothesis]].
- Provides the ordered lattice whose local disruption by thermal vibration constitutes [[Melting]].

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 1: Atoms in Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_01.html)
