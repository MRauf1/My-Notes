# Definition
> [!info] Tolerance of Ambiguity[^1]
> (Hamming) The capacity of great scientists to believe a theory enough to go ahead with it while doubting it enough to notice its errors and faults, so they can step forward and create the replacement theory.

# Properties
- Believe too much and you never notice the flaws; doubt too much and you never get started. It requires "a lovely balance".[^1]
- Great scientists know why their theories are true and also remember the slight misfits; tracking apparent flaws and how the theory might change to explain them is often where the great contributions lie.[^2]
- The individual-level counterpart of how [[Anomaly (Philosophy of Science)|anomalies]] against a trusted [[Paradigm (Philosophy of Science)|paradigm]] seed [[Scientific Revolution|scientific revolutions]] (Kuhn); cf. "try to disprove" in [[Research Heuristics]].
