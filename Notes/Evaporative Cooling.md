# Definition
> [!info] Evaporative Cooling[^1]
> A liquid's temperature falls as it evaporates, because the molecules that escape during [[Evaporation]] are precisely those with above-average kinetic energy, leaving the remaining molecules with a lower average kinetic energy.

# Properties
- A direct consequence of the [[Kinetic Theory of Heat]] and [[Evaporation]].
- Sustained net cooling requires continually disturbing the [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] between escaping and returning molecules, so that evaporation exceeds condensation.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 1: Atoms in Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_01.html)
