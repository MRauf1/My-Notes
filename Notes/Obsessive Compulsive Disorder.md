# Definition

Experiencing of thoughts and urges that are intrusive and unwanted (obsessions) and/or the need to engage in repetitive behaviors or mental acts (compulsions).[^1]

# Causes
- Moderate genetic component
	- About two dozen genes may be involved in OCD, which regulate the function of [[Serotonin]], [[Dopamine]], [[Glutamate]] (Needs more research)

# Associated [[Brain]] Parts
- [[Cortico-Striato-Thalamo-Cortical Circuit]]

# OCD's Learned Responses
- [[Classical Conditioning]]
- [[Operant Conditioning]]

# Treatment
- **Exposure and response prevention (ERP).** Gradual exposure to obsessional triggers while refraining from compulsions; the first-line psychological treatment, with large effects in meta-analyses (Olatunji et al., 2013). Programs help patients see the disorder's demands as irrational and not their own, and commit to more important values that motivate enduring the exposures.[^manson] See [[Exposure Therapy]].
