# Definition

After [[Learning|learning]] a material, repetitively revisiting the material every few days/weeks/months.[^1] 

Helps to solidify the [[Memory|memory]] of the material and convert that memory into [[Long-Term Memory|long-term memory]].

Carnegie advises reviewing a book every month and applying its principles at every opportunity. Both are sound: spacing and applying material beat cramming. He also recommends rereading each chapter and underlining. These are among the *least* effective study techniques; practice testing and distributed practice are among the most effective (Dunlosky et al., 2013).[^2]
