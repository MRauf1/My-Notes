# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Power Spectrum)[^1]
> The light intensity of a [[Light Ray|light ray]] as a function of wavelength, $\ell(\lambda)$.

# Properties
- Within a fixed viewing context, the power spectrum is the most important factor determining the color appearance of light, though color appearance also depends on other factors, such as the surrounding image context.
- When light reflects off a surface or is transmitted through an attenuating filter, its power spectrum is altered in a wavelength-dependent way, described by the surface's [[Reflectance Spectrum]] or the filter's transmittance spectrum, respectively.
- The human eye measures only a three-dimensional projection of a light's power spectrum, given by the [[Trichromatic Theory of Color Vision|eye's three cone responses]], rather than the full power spectrum itself.

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Color](https://visionbook.mit.edu/color.html)
