# Definition
> [!info] Underdetermination of Theory by Evidence[^1]
> The thesis that observational evidence cannot by itself determine a unique theory: more than one theoretical construction can always be placed upon a given collection of data.

# Properties
- Observation must drastically restrict admissible scientific belief, but cannot alone determine it; an apparently arbitrary element of personal and historical accident always shapes a community's beliefs.[^2]
- Historically, alternatives are not hard to invent early in a paradigm's development, yet scientists invent them only in [[Pre-Paradigm Science|pre-paradigm]] periods and [[Crisis (Philosophy of Science)|crises]], since confident use of a working paradigm makes science move fastest.[^1]
- Related to the ill-posedness of [[Inverse Reinforcement Learning]] and to [[Theory-Ladenness of Observation]].
