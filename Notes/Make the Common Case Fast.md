# Definition
> [!info] Make the Common Case Fast[^1]
> Making the common case fast tends to enhance performance more effectively than optimizing the rare case. Ironically, the common case is often simpler than the rare case, and hence is often easier to enhance.

# Properties
- One of the [[Great Ideas in Computer Architecture]].
- Justifies design trade-offs where infrequent operations are handled correctly but slowly, while frequent operations are handled as efficiently as possible.

[^1]: [Computer Organization and Design: The Hardware/Software Interface](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/YWPB5EDC?page=34&annotation=33I4C3VU)
