# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Exercise 6.2.7 -- Hadamard's Inequality)[^1]
> If $A \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ has columns $a_1, \dots, a_n$, then
> $$
> |\det A| \leq \prod_{j=1}^n \lVert a_j \rVert
> $$

Geometrically, this says the volume of the parallelepiped spanned by the columns of $A$ is at most the product of their lengths, with equality exactly when the columns are pairwise orthogonal.
