# Definition
> [!info] Abundance (Tolle)[^1]
> Tolle holds that abundance and scarcity are inner states that "manifest as your reality". If the thought of lack (of money, recognition, or love) has become part of who you think you are, you will always experience lack. Acknowledging the good already in your life is the foundation of all abundance.

# Properties
- Whatever you think others are withholding (praise, appreciation, help, care), give it to them. If you don't have it, act as if you did: "You cannot receive what you don't give."[^1]
- The source of abundance is within, but start by recognizing the fullness of life around you. "Abundance comes only to those who already have it."[^1]
- The claim that inner states "manifest" external reality is not empirically supported. The defensible core is about perception and behavior: gratitude and giving change how lack is experienced (cf. [[Productivity Debt]], [[Salvation (Spirituality)]]).
