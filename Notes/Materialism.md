# Definition
> [!info] Materialism[^1]
> The view that the brain's operation according to the laws of physics constitutes the mind; free will is simply the way that the perception of available choices appears to the choosing entity. Also called physicalism or naturalism.

# Properties
- Stands as an alternative to [[Dualism]], rejecting the existence of any part of the mind that is exempt from physical law.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 24](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=24&annotation=PRT5HNKU)
