# Definition
> [!info] Life Situation (Tolle)[^1]
> The circumstances and experiences of one's life, which exist in time. It is distinct from *life* itself, which is now and is one's deepest Being, already whole and complete. You can improve your life situation, but you cannot improve your life.

# Properties
- **Problems vs. situations.** There are no problems in the Now, only situations, which are either dealt with now or accepted as part of the "isness" of the moment until they change. A "problem" means dwelling on a situation mentally, with no real intention or possibility of acting now, and unconsciously making it part of your sense of self. Problems are mind-made, need time to survive, and create pain.[^2]
- The mind unconsciously loves problems because they give it an identity.[^3]
- Ask whether you have a problem *at this moment*. Carrying "a hundred things" you may have to do in the future buries the one thing you can do now.[^4]
- Until the time-bound mind ([[Psychological Time]]) is dealt with, problems are interchangeable: if all your problems were removed, you would soon have a similar set.[^5]
- In a true emergency the mind stops, you become fully present, and the situation is not a problem.[^6]
- Action that arises from present-moment awareness is clear, incisive, and more likely to be effective.[^7]
- **Waiting** is wanting the future and rejecting the present, which sets up inner conflict. Many people spend their whole lives waiting to start living. If you are present, you never need to wait.[^8]
- Gratitude for the present moment and the fullness of life now is true prosperity. Wealth pursued out of dissatisfaction leaves the inner condition of lack in place.[^9]
- "You cannot be both unhappy and fully present in the Now."[^10]
