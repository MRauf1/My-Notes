# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linear Map]] [[Image]])[^1]
> If $T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$, then [[Image]] of $T$ is a [[Vector Subspace]] of $W$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 ([[Linear Map]] [[Image]] [[Matrix]])[^2]
> Let $T \in \mathcal{L}(\mathbb{F}^n, \mathbb{F}^m)$ that is represented by $A \in M_{m, n}(\mathbb{F})$. Then the [[Image]] of $T$ is the [[Span]] of the columns of $A$ (the [[Matrix Column Space]]).

> [!abstract] Theorem 3 ([[Basis]] [[Span]] Image)
> Let $T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$ where $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ form a [[Basis]] of $V$. Then the list $(T(v_1), \dots, T(v_n))$ [[Span]] $image(T)$.
