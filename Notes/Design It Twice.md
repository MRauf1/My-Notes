# Definition
> [!info] Design It Twice[^1]
> The practice of designing a system or component at least twice before implementing it, since the first design one considers is unlikely to be the best.

# Properties
- Encourages considering multiple candidate designs and comparing their strengths and weaknesses against each other before committing to one.
- The best final design sometimes combines the strongest elements of several candidate designs.
- A concrete practice of [[Strategic Programming]], intended to pay off in the long run despite its short-term upfront cost.

[^1]: [A Philosophy of Software Design](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/283RR677?page=1)
