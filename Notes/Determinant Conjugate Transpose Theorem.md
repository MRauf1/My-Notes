# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Corollary 6.13)[^1]
> If $A \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$, then $\det(A^*) = \overline{\det A}$. If $V$ is a finite-dimensional inner product space and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$, then $\det(T^*) = \overline{\det T}$.
