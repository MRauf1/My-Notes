# Definition
> [!info] Atomic Clock[^1]
> An atomic clock is a device that measures time using the period of a natural atomic vibration as its internal reference, rather than a mechanical or astronomical process.

# Properties
- Its basic internal period is insensitive to temperature and other external effects, making it a far more constant time reference than the earth's rotation, and it defines the modern [[Second]].
- Keeps time to an accuracy of one part in $10^9$ or better.
- A hydrogen-vibration atomic clock built by Norman Ramsey at Harvard improved on earlier designs, with accuracy potentially $100$ times better still.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 5: Time and Distance](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_05.html)
