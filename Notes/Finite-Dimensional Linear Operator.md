# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 (Finite-Dimensional [[Linear Operator]])[^1]
> A linear operator $T: V \rightarrow W$ is finite-dimensional if $V$ and $W$ are [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space|finite-dimensional vector spaces]].

Once a [[Basis]] is chosen for $V$ and $W$, $T$ is isomorphic to a unique [[Matrix]]; a different choice of basis yields a different (but similar) matrix for the same operator.
