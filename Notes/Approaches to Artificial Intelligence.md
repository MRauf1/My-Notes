# Definition
> [!info] Approaches to Artificial Intelligence[^1]
> A taxonomy of AI research along two independent dimensions: (i) whether intelligence is measured against fidelity to human performance or against an abstract, formal standard of rationality, and (ii) whether the subject matter is internal thought processes/reasoning or external, intelligent behavior. These two dimensions yield four possible combinations.

# Types
- Thinking Humanly - modeling internal human cognitive processes, studied by [[Cognitive Science]].
- Acting Humanly - matching human behavior, as tested by the [[Turing Test]].
- Thinking Rationally - deriving conclusions by correct, formal reasoning, associated with the logicist tradition in [[Logic]].
- Acting Rationally - selecting the action expected to achieve the best outcome, embodied by the [[Rational Agent]].

# Properties
- The acting-rationally approach has prevailed throughout most of the field's history, forming the [[Standard Model of Artificial Intelligence|standard model]].
