# Definition
> [!info] Attention Economy[^1]
> An economy in which human attention is the scarce commodity that platforms compete for. *Persuasive design* borrows techniques from slot machines, notably *variable rewards*: when you can't predict whether refreshing will bring new posts, you keep trying, as on a slot machine.

# Properties
- **Distortion.** Because it prioritizes whatever is most compelling rather than most true or useful, the attention economy distorts our picture of the world.[^2]
	- Each moral-emotional word in a tweet increased its retweet rate by about 20% (Brady et al., 2017).
	- False news spread farther and faster than true news on Twitter (Vosoughi, Roy and Aral, 2018).
- **Self-monitoring.** Attention can hardly monitor itself, because the only faculty for noticing what is happening to your attention is the attention already captured.[^3]
- **Collaborators.** We are not just victims. Part of us wants to be distracted (see [[Distraction]]).[^4]
- Related: [[Poverty of Attention]], [[Operant Conditioning]] (variable-ratio schedules), [[Supernormal Stimulus]], [[Negativity Bias]].
