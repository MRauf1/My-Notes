# Definition

For [[Function|function]] $f: \mathbb{A} \rightarrow \mathbb{B}$, the range is the [[Set|set]] $B$.[^1] Although, more accepted is the version where range is synonymous with [[Image]], while [[Codomain]] is what $B$ is.
