# Definition
> [!info] Handwritten Note-Taking[^1]
> In lecture studies (Mueller and Oppenheimer, 2014), students who took notes by hand understood the content much better than laptop note-takers, and the difference was still measurable a week later. Handwriting is slower and harder to correct, so you can't transcribe everything. You are forced to grasp the gist and translate it into your own words.

# Properties
- Pure copying becomes impossible, which forces processing (cf. [[Elaboration]], [[Literature Note]]).[^1]
- Later replications (e.g., Morehead, Dunlosky, and Rawson, 2019) found the handwriting advantage smaller and less reliable than first reported. The robust lesson is to paraphrase rather than transcribe, whatever the medium.
