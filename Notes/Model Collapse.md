# Definition
> [!info] Model Collapse[^1]
> The degradation (also called AI collapse) that occurs when AI models are trained on too much AI-generated rather than genuine human-generated content: lacking sufficient genuine content, the model becomes ungrounded from reality, caught in a mode of thought with no connection to the real world.

# Properties
- Arises because modern AI does not pursue or intuit truth through the physical world or its laws, but relies heavily on human-generated data (often without attribution) and substantial human feedback.[^1]
- Consequently, models cannot be made much less reliant on human intellectual labour without risking contamination of the collective body of information with AI-generated content; there is a clear limit to how much "new information" AI can generate in a domain.[^1]
- Hampers meaningful interaction at the human/AI interface, and is one of the costs weighed in [[Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence]].
- A related risk in research is circular citation loops created by AI-generated literature ([[AI-Assisted Mathematics]]).

[^1]: [Klowden and Tao, 2026, p. 17](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/D49IZRQS?page=17&annotation=CIP2TJLG)
