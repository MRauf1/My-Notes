# Definition
> [!info] Parabolic Partial Differential Equation[^1]
> The [[Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]] $\sum_{i,j} a_{ij} u_{x_i x_j} + \sum_i a_i u_{x_i} + a_0 u = 0$ is parabolic if exactly one of the [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalues]] $d_1, \dots, d_n$ of its coefficient matrix $A$ is zero and all the others have the same sign. In two variables: $a_{12}^2 = a_{11} a_{22}$ (with $A \neq 0$).

# Properties
- Canonical form $u_{x_2 x_2} + \cdots + u_{x_n x_n} + \cdots = 0$, with at most a first-order derivative in the degenerate direction; prototype: the [[Diffusion Equation]] (and [[Heat Equation]]).
- The degenerate direction plays the role of time; needs one [[Initial Condition]].
- See [[Canonical Form of Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]].
