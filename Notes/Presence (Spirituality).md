# Definition
> [!info] Presence (Tolle)[^1]
> Consciousness freed from identification with physical and mental forms; pure or "enlightened" consciousness. In Tolle's terms it is Being becoming conscious of itself, or life attaining self-consciousness. It is experienced as a state of being still yet highly alert, with all attention in the Now.

# Properties
- It cannot be thought about or understood by the mind: "Understanding presence is being present."[^2]
- In intense presence one is free of thought. When conscious attention drops below a certain level, thought rushes back in and one is "back in time".[^3]
- It resembles an alert kind of *waiting*, in which something could happen at any moment and you would miss it unless absolutely awake and still.[^4]
- Body awareness keeps you present (see [[Inner Body]]).[^5]
- Presence is needed to perceive beauty and the sacredness of nature. Beyond external forms is an ineffable inner essence that only shows itself when you are present; the mind can neither recognize nor create beauty. Calling something "a pretty flower" is mechanical labeling.[^6]
- "The wider the time gap between perception and thought, the more depth there is to you as a human being."[^7]
- Simple ways in: listen to the silence, or bring attention to the present moment.[^8]
- There is no "mine" or "yours" in presence; presence is one. Group practice and teachers can help intensify it during a transitional period, but one must not come to depend on them.[^9]
- Tolle's cosmology holds that everything (even a stone) has some degree of consciousness, which takes on and gets lost in forms. Suffering eventually forces consciousness to disidentify from form and wake up at a deeper level. Each act of [[Watching the Thinker|watching the mind]] is a small instance of this.[^10]
- It grows by being practiced in ordinary, smooth situations. How you meet life's challenges is the best indicator of your level of consciousness.[^11]
