# Definition
> [!info] Decisive Strategic Advantage[^1]
> A level of technological and other advantages sufficient to enable an agent to achieve complete world domination; relatedly, an agent possesses a "superpower" (e.g. intelligence amplification, technology research, economic productivity) only if its capability in that area substantially exceeds the combined capabilities of the rest of global civilisation, so at most one agent can possess a given superpower at a time.

# Properties
- A fast or moderate [[Superintelligence Takeoff|takeoff]] makes it likely that one project obtains a decisive strategic advantage; that is why takeoff speed matters for *what* the outcome is, not merely when.[^2]
- A superintelligence with a decisive strategic advantage could form a stable [[Singleton]] determining the disposition of humanity's cosmic endowment.[^2]
- Once obtained, control methods can no longer be imposed, so the [[AI Control Problem]] must be solved beforehand.[^3]
- Plans that seem natural to humans need not seem so to a superintelligence with such an advantage, and vice versa.[^4]
