# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Inverse]] of [[Relation]])[^1]
> For a [[Relation]] $R: A \rightarrow B$, the inverse relation is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> R^{-1} = \{(b, a) \in B \times A | (a, b) \in R\}
> \end{align}
> $$

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=186)
