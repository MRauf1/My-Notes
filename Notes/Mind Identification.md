# Definition
> [!info] Mind Identification (Tolle)[^1]
> Deriving one's sense of self from the content and activity of the mind, i.e., taking the thinker to be who you are. This makes thinking compulsive and creates a false, mind-made self (the [[Ego|ego]]) that casts a shadow of fear and suffering. Here "mind" includes not only thought but also emotions and all unconscious mental-emotional reactive patterns.

# Properties
- **The voice in the head.** Almost everyone hears an involuntary inner voice that comments, judges, compares, complains, likes, and dislikes. It relives the past or rehearses the future, often imagining negative outcomes ([[Worry|worry]]). It belongs to the *conditioned* mind, shaped by personal history and inherited culture, so it sees and judges the present through the eyes of the past and distorts it.[^2]
- **Compulsive thinking is an addiction.** You cannot stop because you believe you would cease to exist if you did. "You usually don't use it at all. It uses you." Because almost everyone has it, it is considered normal.[^3]
- **Descartes' error.** "I think, therefore I am" equates thinking with Being and identity with thinking (cf. [[Descartes Evil Demon Thought Experiment|Descartes]]).[^4]
- **Screen of concepts.** Identification puts an opaque screen of labels, images, judgments, and definitions between you and yourself, other people, and nature. This creates the illusion of separateness. One may still *believe* in oneness but no longer *know* it.[^5]
- **Mind as a tool.** The mind is a superb instrument if used rightly. It is meant to be picked up for a specific task and put down afterward. The mind itself is not dysfunctional; dysfunction sets in when you look for your self in it.[^6]
- **Thinking ≠ consciousness.** Thought is a small part of consciousness. Thought cannot exist without consciousness, but consciousness does not need thought. The things that matter most (beauty, love, creativity, joy, inner peace) arise from beyond the mind.[^7]
- **Creativity.** The mind is essentially a survival machine: good at attack, defense, and gathering and analyzing information, but not creative. Creative insight comes from stillness ("no-mind"), and the mind then gives it form. Effective creative work oscillates between thought and stillness every few minutes. Compare [[Unconscious Thought Theory]].[^8]
- Time and mind are inseparable: identification with the mind means being trapped in [[Psychological Time|psychological time]].[^9]
- Problems of the mind cannot be solved at the level of the mind. Studying its complexities will not take you beyond it.[^10]
- The way out is [[Watching the Thinker|disidentification]].
- Tolle calls the predominance of mind a stage in the evolution of consciousness. Humanity must move past it "as a matter of urgency", or the mind will destroy it.[^11]
