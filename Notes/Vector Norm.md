# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Vector Norm)
> The norm/[[Magnitude|magnitude]]/[[Length|length]] of a [[Vector|vector]] $\mathbf{v} \in \mathbf{F}^n$ is[^1]
> $$
> \begin{align}
> ||\mathbf{v}|| = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 + \dots + v_n^2} = \sqrt{v \cdot v}
> \end{align}
> $$
> where $v \cdot v$ is a [[Dot Product]].
> If $\lVert v \rVert = 1$, then $v$ is a [[Unit Vector]].
