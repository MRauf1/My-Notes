# Definition

Mathematical phrase involving [[Number|numbers]] ([[Constant|constants]] and [[Variable|variables]]) and [[Operation|operations]]. [^1] Unlike an [[Equation|equation]] or [[Inequality|inequality]], which relate two expressions, an expression by itself asserts nothing and has no truth value.

> > [!example]- Example 1.1 (Basic Expression)
> > $$
> > \begin{align}
> > x + 2 - y
> > \end{align}
> > $$
