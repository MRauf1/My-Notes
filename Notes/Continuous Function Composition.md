# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Continuous Function]] [[Function Composition]])[^1]
> If $g$ is continuous at $a$ and $f$ is continuous at $g(a)$, then their composition $(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))$ is also continuous at $a$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 ([[Continuous Function]] [[Function Composition]] on [[Metric Space]])[^2]
> Let $X, Y, Z$ be [[Metric Space|metric spaces]], $E \subseteq X$, $f: E \rightarrow Y$, $g: f(E) \rightarrow Z$, and $h: E \rightarrow Z$ defined by $h(x) = g(f(x))$ ($x \in E$). If $f$ is continuous at $p \in E$ and $g$ is continuous at $f(p)$, then $h = g \circ f$ is continuous at $p$.
