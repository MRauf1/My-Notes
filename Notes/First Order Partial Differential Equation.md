# Definition

[[Partial Differential Equation]] of order 1.

> [!info] First Order Partial Differential Equation[^1]
> The most general first-order PDE in two independent variables is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> F(x, y, u, u_x, u_y) = 0.
> \end{align}
> $$

# Types
- [[Transport Equation]]

# Properties
- A linear homogeneous one, $a(x, y) u_x + b(x, y) u_y = 0$, says the [[Directional Derivative]] of $u$ along $(a, b)$ vanishes, so $u$ is constant along the [[Characteristic Curve|characteristic curves]] $dy/dx = b/a$; solved by the [[Geometric Method]] or the [[Coordinate Change Method]].[^2]
