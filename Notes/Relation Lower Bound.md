# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Lower Bound)[^1]
> Let $R: A \rightarrow A$, $B subseteq A$, $a \in A$. Then $a$ is the lower bound for $B$ if for all $x \in B, aRx$.

[[Relation Smallest Element]] of $B$ is the lower bound of $B$.
