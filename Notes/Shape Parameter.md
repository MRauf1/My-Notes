# Definition
> [!info] Shape Parameter[^1]
> A parameter is a shape parameter if changing it changes the form of the pdf or pmf itself, not merely its location or spread.

Examples: $\alpha$ of the [[Gamma Distribution]] (from monotone decreasing for $\alpha \leq 1$ to increasingly symmetric bell shapes as $\alpha$ grows), $p$ of the [[Binomial Distribution]], $\lambda$ of the [[Poisson Distribution]], the degrees of freedom of the [[t-Distribution]] and [[Chi-Squared Distribution]], and both parameters of the [[Beta Distribution]].

# Properties
- Standardized characteristics such as [[Probability Distribution Skewness|skewness]] and [[Kurtosis]] depend only on the shape parameters, since [[Location Parameter|location]] and [[Scale Parameter|scale]] cancel out of them.
