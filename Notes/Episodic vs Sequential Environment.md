# Definition
> [!info] Episodic vs. Sequential Environment[^1]
> A dimension of a [[Task Environment|task environment]]. In an episodic environment, the agent's experience is divided into atomic episodes in which it receives a percept and performs a single action, and the next episode does not depend on actions taken in previous episodes. In a sequential environment, the current decision could affect all future decisions.

# Properties
- Episodic environments are much simpler than sequential environments, since the agent does not need to think ahead.
