# Definition
> [!info] Cognitive Dissonance[^1]
> (Festinger) The unpleasant tension felt when one's choice or behaviour appears inconsistent ("dissonant") with one's values or beliefs, which the individual (usually unconsciously) acts to reduce.

# Properties
- After a close decision one feels postdecisional regret ("What have I done?"); dissonance is commonly reduced by devaluing the unchosen alternative.[^1]
- **Information distortion**: one becomes open to information that upgrades the chosen or downgrades the unchosen alternative, and closed to the reverse.[^1]
- Serves to avoid the discomfort of freedom in [[Decision (Existential Psychology)|decisions]].
