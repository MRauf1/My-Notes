# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 (Arc)[^1]
> A [[Curve]] $\gamma$ on $[a, b]$ (a continuous mapping $\gamma: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}^k$) is called an arc if $\gamma$ is one-to-one ([[Injective Function]]).

An arc has no self-intersections, not even at the endpoints, so an arc is never a [[Closed Curve]] (when $a < b$).

# Properties
- A [[Simple Curve]] relaxes injectivity by allowing $\gamma(a) = \gamma(b)$.
- Being an arc is preserved under reparameterization by a continuous one-to-one map of $[c, d]$ onto $[a, b]$ ([[Rectifiable Curve]]).

[^1]: [Principles of Mathematical Analysis](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/77RSE64H?page=145&annotation=B4BR6PHD)
