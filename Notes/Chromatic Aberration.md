# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Chromatic Aberration)[^1]
> The dependence of a lens's [[Point Spread Function]] on the wavelength of light, caused by the wavelength-dependence of the [[Index of Refraction|index of refraction]], so that different wavelengths are brought to focus at different distances behind the lens.

# Properties
- Produces a chromatic fringe at edges in white light, since the different wavelength components of an edge are focused with different degrees of sharpness.
- [[Accommodation]] can bring any single wavelength into sharp focus but cannot focus all wavelengths simultaneously, since each requires a different [[Optical Power]].
- A distinct mechanism for separating light into its spectral components from [[Diffraction]]: chromatic aberration arises from wavelength-dependent [[Refraction]], whereas diffraction separates wavelengths through wavelength-dependent interference.
