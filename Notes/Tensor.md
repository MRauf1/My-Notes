# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Tensor)[^1]
> A multidimensional array, most often of real numbers. The primary data structure of [[Deep Learning|deep learning]]: inputs, activations, parameters, and outputs are all represented as tensors.

# Properties
- When a batch is processed in parallel, the input becomes a multi-dimensional tensor, the generalization of a matrix to arbitrary dimensions.[^2]
- Data that is not natively represented as a tensor must first be converted into a tensor format before being processed by a [[Deep Neural Network|deep net]].
