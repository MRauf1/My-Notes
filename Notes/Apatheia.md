# Definition
> [!info] Apatheia[^1]
> The Stoic ideal of complete freedom from [[Passion (Stoicism)|passion]]. A dispassionate person is not an unfeeling zombie ("unfeeling as a statue") but someone who has changed his mind about the value of passions and experiences only sane feelings ([[Eupatheia]]).

# Properties
- Notorious in antiquity as now.[^1]
- Compatible with fulfilling one's duties to kin and caring for one's body, without thinking them ultimately important.[^2]
- Freedom from passion is the result of the first of the [[Three Disciplines (Epictetus)]].
- Compare [[Mindfulness]] and Buddhist non-attachment.
