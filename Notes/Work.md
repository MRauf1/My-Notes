# Definition
> [!info] Work[^1]
> The energy transferred to an object by a [[Force]] $\mathbf{F}$ acting through a displacement $\mathbf{s}$:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> W = \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{s}
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- Being a [[Dot Product]], only the component of the force along the direction of displacement contributes; a force perpendicular to the displacement does no work.
- Equivalent to the change in energy produced by the force, e.g. the [[Gravitational Potential Energy|potential energy]] gained by an object lifted against gravity, or the [[Kinetic Energy]] gained by an object accelerated from rest.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 11: Vectors](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_11.html)
