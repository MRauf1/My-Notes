# Definition

Collaboration is encouraged, but make sure to reserve times for [[Deep Work]] as well. Make sure to go out and share ideas for others at certain periods of time, but reserve the other periods of time for intense deep work.[^1]

# Properties
- **Hub-and-spoke.** The *theory of serendipitous creativity* says new ideas come from people bumping into each other. Building 20 (MIT) and Bell Labs got this not from open offices but from private offices linked by long shared hallways that acted as hubs. Expose yourself to ideas in hubs, and keep a spoke for deep work; optimize the two separately rather than mixing them.[^2]
- **Whiteboard effect.** For some problems, working with someone at a shared whiteboard pushes you deeper than working alone, because the other person waiting for your next insight short-circuits the instinct to avoid depth (e.g., Bardeen and Brattain on the transistor).[^3]
