# Definition
> [!info] Psychological Fear (Tolle)[^1]
> Fear divorced from any concrete, immediate danger: fear of something that *might* happen, not of something happening now. The mind is in the future while you are in the present, and this creates an "anxiety gap".

# Properties
- You can always cope with the present moment, but you cannot cope with a mind projection. "What if" thoughts are mental phantoms. "The answer, the strength, the right action or the resource will be there when you need it, not before, not after."[^2]
- Ultimately all fear is the [[Ego|ego]]'s fear of death, of annihilation. The ego is vulnerable and insecure and sees itself as constantly under threat. Anyone identified with their mind has fear as a constant companion.[^3]
- **Need to be right.** If you identify with a mental position, being wrong threatens your sense of self with annihilation, which drives arguments and defensiveness. Once disidentified, being right or wrong makes no difference to your sense of self, and you can state your view firmly without aggression.[^4]
- "Power over others is weakness disguised as strength. True power is within."[^5]
- **Handling**: acknowledge the fear, watch it, take your attention into it, and be fully present with it. This cuts the link between fear and thinking.[^6]
- As there are no problems in the Now, there is no fear in it either. Much of what people say and do is motivated by fear.[^7]
- Belongs to the "too much future" class of negativity ([[Psychological Time]]). Compare [[Worry]] and [[Anticipatory Anxiety]].
