# Definition
> [!info] IPv4[^1]
> Version 4 of the [[Internet Protocol]], which limits source and destination addresses to 32 bits.

# Properties
- Its 32-bit address space is a significant limitation, motivating [[IPv6]].[^2]
- `127.0.0.1` is reserved as its [[Loopback Address]].[^3]
