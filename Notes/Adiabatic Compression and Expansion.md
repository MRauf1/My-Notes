# Definition
> [!info] Adiabatic Compression and Expansion[^1]
> When a gas is compressed slowly, the work done on it increases the kinetic energy of its molecules and its temperature rises; when a gas expands slowly, it does work on its surroundings and its temperature falls.

# Properties
- A direct consequence of the [[Kinetic Theory of Pressure]] and the [[Kinetic Theory of Heat]]: compression increases the rate and vigor of molecular collisions, which is registered as higher temperature.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 1: Atoms in Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_01.html)
