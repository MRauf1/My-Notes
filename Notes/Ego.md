# Definition

...

The false self created from the mental image of who we are; created from the unconscious identification with the mind. It is kept going through constant thinking.[^1]

# Time
Obsessed with the past (derives identity from it) and the future (must ensure its survival), and doesn't spend much time in the present. Furthermore, the present it sees is misperceived since it sees it with its biased eyes. Or it reduces the present to a means toward an end in the future.[^1]

# Buddhist View
- The ego (self-concept) is a set of reactions and mental images artificially pasted onto the flowing process of pure awareness. It is essentially a feeling of separation between "me" and "other", kept alive by the [[Five Hindrances]], and [[Mindfulness]] dismantles it piece by piece (see [[Anatta]]).[^2]

# Tolle on the Ego
- Formed through [[Mind Identification]]. It is a derived sense of self, so it must identify with external things and be constantly defended and fed. It carries a deep sense of lack, a "bottomless hole" (see [[Salvation (Spirituality)]]).[^3]
- It sees itself as a separate fragment in a hostile universe, treating other egos as threats or as means to its ends. When egos meet, drama follows.[^4]
- All of its fear is ultimately fear of death ([[Psychological Fear]]). It loves negativity and problems because they strengthen it ([[Life Situation]]).[^5]
