# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Symmetric Relation]] [[Closure]])[^1]
> Let $R$ be a [[Relation]] on $A$. The symmetric closure of $R$ is the smallest set  $S \subseteq A \times A$ such that $R \subseteq S$ and $S$ is [[Symmetric Relation]]. In other words,
> 1) $R \subseteq S$
> 2) $S$ is [[Symmetric Relation]]
> 3) For every [[Relation]] $T \subseteq A \times A$, if $R \subseteq T$ and $T$ is [[Symmetric Relation]], then $S \subseteq T$.
> A symmetric closure for a [[Relation]] $R$ always exists and is unique.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=218)
