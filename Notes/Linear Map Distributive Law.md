# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linear Map]] [[Distributive Property]])[^1]
> Let $U, V, W$ be [[Vector Space]], $T, T_1, T_2 \in \mathcal{L}(U, V)$, and $S, S_1, S_2 \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$. Then
> 1) $S(T_1 + T_2) = ST_1 + ST_2$
> 2) $(S_1 + S_2)T = S_1T + S_2T$

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=102)
