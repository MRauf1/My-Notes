# Definition
> [!abstract] Conditional Probability Primary Properties[^1]
> For an [[Event]] $B$ with $P(B) > 0$,
> 1. $P(A | B) \geq 0$
> 2. $P(B | B) = 1$
> 3. if $A_1, A_2, \dots$ are [[Mutually Exclusive Events|mutually exclusive events]], then $P\left(\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty A_n \,\middle|\, B\right) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty P(A_n | B)$

These are exactly the axioms of [[Probability]] with [[Sample Space]] $B$, so $P(\cdot | B)$ is itself a probability set function, and every theorem about probabilities (e.g. [[Conditional Probability Other Properties]]) holds conditionally.
