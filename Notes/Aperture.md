# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Aperture)[^1]
> The opening in a camera through which light passes to reach the sensor.

# Properties
- Enlarging a [[Pinhole Camera|pinhole camera's]] aperture lets in more light but also allows light from many different surface positions to land on a given sensor position, producing a bright but blurry image; a [[Lens]] instead lets a camera capture more light while still focusing each sensor position to a single surface point.
- Its diameter $A$, relative to a lens's [[Focal Length (Lens)|focal length]] $f$, determines the camera's [[F-Number]], $N = f/A$.
- A narrower aperture increases a camera's [[Depth of Field]], at the cost of reducing the light reaching the sensor plane.

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Lenses](https://visionbook.mit.edu/lenses.html)
