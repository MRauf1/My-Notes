# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Exclusive Or)[^1]
> $P + Q$ (also $P \oplus Q$) means "$P$ or $Q$, but not both":
> $$
> \begin{align}
> P + Q \equiv (P \lor Q) \land \neg(P \land Q) \equiv (P \land \neg Q) \lor (\neg P \land Q)
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- In mathematics, "or" ([[Or]]) means inclusive or; exclusive or must be stated explicitly.
- $P + Q \equiv \neg(P \leftrightarrow Q)$ ([[Biconditional Statement]]). It corresponds to [[Symmetric Difference]] of sets and to addition in $\mathbb{F}_2$ ([[Modular Arithmetic]]).
