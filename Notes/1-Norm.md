# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 (1-Norm)[^1]
> The 1-norm on $\mathbb{R}^n$ or $\mathbb{C}^n$ is defined by
> $$
> \lVert x \rVert_1 := \sum_{j=1}^n |x_j|
> $$

The fact that the 1-norm is indeed a [[Normed Space|norm]] follows from the fact that the [[Absolute Value]] $|\cdot|$ is a norm on $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$.

The [[L1 Norm]] on $C([0,1])$ is the functional analog of the 1-norm.
