# Definition
> [!info] Area Measure[^1]
> The measure $A$ that assigns to a subset of a surface its surface area; used to integrate over the boundary $\partial\Omega$ of a domain and over scene geometry.

# Properties
- An instance of a [[Measure (Measure Theory)|measure]].
- The [[Walk Space Measure]] is built as a product of area measures.

[^1]: Differentiable Monte Carlo — Course Lecture Notes, University of Illinois (Fall 2026)
