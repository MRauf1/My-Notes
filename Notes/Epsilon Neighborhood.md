# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Epsilon Neighborhood)
> In a [[Metric Space]] $(S, d)$, the epsilon neighborhood of a [[Point]] $x \in S$ with [[Radius]] $\epsilon > 0$ is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> B(x, r) = \{a \in S | d(x, a) < \epsilon\}
> \end{align}
> $$

It is an [[Open n-Ball]] of [[Radius]] $\epsilon$. In $\mathbb{R}^k$, Rudin calls this the open ball with center $x$ and radius $r$ (the closed ball is the analogous set with $d(x, a) \leq r$).

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Every Neighborhood is Open)[^1]
> Let $(S, d)$ be a [[Metric Space]]. Every epsilon neighborhood $B(x, r)$ is an [[Open Set]].

# Properties
- [[Star-Shaped Set]] with respect to [[Point]] $x$
- [[Convex Set|Convex]]
