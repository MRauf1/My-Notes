# Definition
> [!info] Infrastructure Profusion[^1]
> A malignant failure mode in which an agent transforms large parts of the reachable universe into infrastructure in the service of some goal, with the side effect of preventing the realisation of humanity's axiological potential.

# Properties
- Exemplified by a [[Wireheading|wireheaded]] AI building ever more infrastructure to secure its reward stream.[^1]
- Follows from open-ended resource acquisition under the [[Instrumental Convergence Thesis]], even for seemingly bounded goals.
- Alongside [[Perverse Instantiation]] and [[Mind Crime]], one of Bostrom's malignant failure modes.

[^1]: [Bostrom, 2014, p. 123](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9ECZCLTQ?page=140&annotation=HQ6A3Z6J)
