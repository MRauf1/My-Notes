# Definition
> [!info] Melting[^1]
> The transition in which the atoms of a [[Crystalline Structure]], which vibrate in place about fixed lattice sites, are driven by rising temperature to vibrate with sufficiently large amplitude that they shake themselves out of their ordered positions.

# Properties
- Governed by the [[Kinetic Theory of Heat]]: temperature measures the amplitude of this in-place vibration.
- As temperature is lowered, vibrational amplitude decreases toward a nonzero residual value at absolute zero rather than vanishing entirely, per [[Zero-Point Motion]].

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 1: Atoms in Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_01.html)
