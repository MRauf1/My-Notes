# Definition
> [!info] Klein-Gordon Equation[^1]
> The [[Wave Equation]] with an extra term proportional to the displacement:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> u_{tt} - c^2 \Delta u + k u = 0, \qquad k > 0.
> \end{align}
> $$

Models a vibrating medium with an additional transverse elastic restoring force, as in a coiled spring obeying [[Hooke's Law]].

# Properties
- Linear, homogeneous, [[Hyperbolic Partial Differential Equation|hyperbolic]] (the zeroth-order term does not affect the type).
- In relativistic quantum mechanics it describes a free spin-0 particle of mass $m$, with $k = m^2 c^4 / \hbar^2$.
- Unlike the plain wave equation, it is dispersive: plane waves $e^{i(\boldsymbol{\xi} \cdot \mathbf{x} - \omega t)}$ satisfy $\omega^2 = c^2 |\boldsymbol{\xi}|^2 + k$, so different frequencies travel at different speeds.
