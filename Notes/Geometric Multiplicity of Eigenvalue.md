# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Geometric [[Multiplicity]] of [[Eigenvalue]])[^1]
> The geometric [[Multiplicity]] of $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}$ as an [[Eigenvalue]] of $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$ is $m_{\lambda}(A) = null(A - \lambda I_n)$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Theorem)[^1]
> $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}$ is an [[Eigenvalue]] of $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$ if and only if $m_{\lambda}(A) > 0$.
