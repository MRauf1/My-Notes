# Definition
> [!info] Length Contraction[^1]
> $$L = L_0\sqrt{1-u^2/c^2}$$
> An object of rest length $L_0$, measured at rest in its own frame, has a shorter length $L$ when measured, along the direction of relative motion, by an observer relative to whom it moves at speed $u$.

# Properties
- Follows directly from the $x$-coordinate equation of the [[Lorentz Transformation]]: a length laid out along $x'$ in the moving frame corresponds to a shorter length in the stationary frame's coordinates, by the same factor $\sqrt{1-u^2/c^2}$.
- Occurs only along the direction of relative motion; lengths measured perpendicular to the motion ($y$, $z$) are unaffected.
- Symmetric between observers: each of two observers in relative motion measures the other's ruler, oriented along the direction of motion, as contracted.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 15: The Special Theory of Relativity](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_15.html)
