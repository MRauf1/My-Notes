# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 (Unitarily Invariant Norm)[^1]
> A norm $\lVert \cdot \rVert$ on matrices is unitarily invariant if $\lVert UAV \rVert = \lVert A \rVert$ for every [[Unitary Matrix|unitary]] $U \in M_m(\mathbb{C})$, $V \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$, and every $A \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{C})$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Exercise 4.5.15)[^1]
> The [[Operator Norm]] and [[Frobenius Norm]] are both unitarily invariant: for [[Unitary Matrix|unitary]] $U \in M_m(\mathbb{C})$, $V \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$, and $A \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{C})$,
> $$
> \lVert UAV \rVert_{op} = \lVert A \rVert_{op}, \qquad \lVert UAV \rVert_F = \lVert A \rVert_F
> $$
