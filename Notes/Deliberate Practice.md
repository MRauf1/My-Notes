# How Much Does Practice Explain?
Pausch: "Hard work is like compounded interest in the bank."[^2] A meta-analysis found that deliberate practice explains a meaningful but not dominant share of performance differences. It explains about 26% of the variance in games, 21% in music, 18% in sports, 4% in education and under 1% in professions (Macnamara, Hambrick and Oswald, 2014).
