# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Simple [[Graph]])[^1]
> [[Graph]] that doesn't have multiple [[Edge]] (there can't be multiple edges between two nodes) and that doesn't have loop [[Edge]] (edge from a [[Vertex]] to itself).
