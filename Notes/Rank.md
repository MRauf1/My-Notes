# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Rank)[^1]
> The rank of a [[Linear Map]] $T$ is the [[Dimension]] of its [[Linear Map Image]] ($rank(T) := dim(image(T))$).
> The rank of a [[Matrix]] $A$ is the [[Dimension]] of its [[Matrix Column Space]] ($rank(A) := dim(C(A))$).
