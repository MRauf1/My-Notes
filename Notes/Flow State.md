# Achieving Flow State
Popular books (*Atomic Habits*, citing Steven Kotler) claim that a task must be roughly 4% beyond one's current abilities to trigger flow. No published study supports this figure.[^2] The evidence-based condition is *challenge–skill balance*: flow is more likely when perceived challenge and skill are both high and matched (Csikszentmihalyi). A meta-analysis confirmed a moderate association between challenge–skill balance and flow (Fong, Zaleski and Leach, 2015).

Clear's *Goldilocks Rule* (peak motivation at the edge of one's ability) is often equated with the Yerkes–Dodson law. The popular inverted-U between arousal and performance is only weakly supported and differs from what Yerkes and Dodson actually measured (Teigen, 1994).[^3]
