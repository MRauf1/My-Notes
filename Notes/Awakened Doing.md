# Definition
> [!info] Awakened Doing (Tolle)[^1]
> Action aligned with one's inner purpose, in which consciousness flows into what you do. Its three modalities are:
> - **Acceptance**: "For now, this is what this situation, this moment, requires me to do, and so I do it willingly." You are at peace while doing it.
> - **Enjoyment**: joy does not come *from* what you do; it flows *into* what you do from within. You enjoy any activity in which you are fully present and that is not just a means to an end.
> - **Enthusiasm**: enjoyment combined with a goal or vision you work toward. Enthusiasm and ego cannot coexist.

# Properties
- "Not what you do, but how you do what you do determines whether you are fulfilling your destiny", and how you do it depends on your state of consciousness.[^2]
- "Maybe tomorrow you can start enjoying…" never comes unless you begin now.[^3]
- Make goals *dynamic*: point them toward an activity that connects you with others and the whole, not toward a static end-state.[^4]
- "You cannot become successful. You can only be successful." Quality needs care and attention, which come with awareness and presence. "The great arises out of small things that are honored and cared for."[^5]
- **Means and end are one.** If the means don't contribute to human happiness, neither will the end. Tolle calls such action "karmic action", the unconscious continuation of unhappiness. What the future holds depends on your state of consciousness now.[^6]
- **Uncertainty**: unaccepted, it becomes fear; accepted, it becomes aliveness, alertness, and creativity. "The desire for safety stands against every great and noble enterprise." (Tacitus)[^7]
