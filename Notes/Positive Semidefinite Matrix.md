# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Exercise 5.4.9)[^1]
> Let $A \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ be [[Self-Adjoint Linear Map|Hermitian]]. The following are equivalent:
> 1. Every [[Eigenvalue]] of $A$ is nonnegative.
> 2. For every $x \in \mathbb{C}^n$, $\langle Ax, x \rangle \geq 0$.

> [!info] Definition 2 (Positive Semidefinite)[^1]
> A matrix satisfying the equivalent properties of Theorem 1 is called positive semidefinite. Every [[Positive Definite Matrix|positive definite]] matrix is positive semidefinite, but not conversely — a positive semidefinite matrix may have a zero eigenvalue.

> [!abstract] Theorem 3 (Exercise 5.4.10a)[^2]
> For any $A \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{C})$, $A^*A$ is positive semidefinite.
