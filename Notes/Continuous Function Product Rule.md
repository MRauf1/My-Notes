# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Continuous Function]] Product Rule)[^1]
> If [[Function]] $f, g$ are [[Continuous Function]] at $a$, then $f \cdot g$ is also continuous at $a$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (On [[Metric Space]])[^2]
> Let $f, g$ be [[Complex-Valued Function|complex]] continuous functions on a [[Metric Space]] $X$. Then $fg$ is continuous on $X$.

# Properties
- Vector-valued version: [[Continuous Function Component Theorem]]
