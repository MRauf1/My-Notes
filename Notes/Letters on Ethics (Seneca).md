# Review[^1]

You have to live your life in the present moment, each and every moment. Of all things available in the universe, one of only things that belong to us is time, specifically the present moment. All of life happens in the present moment, so anytime you're not living in the present moment (either recounting the past or imagining the future), you're wasting your life away as your life goes by you. By living in the present, you also become less dependent on the future.

While one can loan and borrow all kinds of things, time is the one thing that can never be repaid. Treasure it accordingly.

Doesn't matter how little you have. If those things are enough for you, then that's all that matters, and you're not poor.

Read a limited number of works at a time, but thoroughly focus and absorb and dissect each one them. Become intimate with the author. When you are done reading for the day, take one topic from what you've read, and ponder on and digest that topic for the rest of the day.

Real friends are to be trusted fully. That's why it's essential to first make a proper judgement about the potential friend and their character before proceeding to love them as a friend. Once you decide though, treat them fully and truly as a friend. Do not be suspicious of them, hold back things from them, distrust them, or anything like that once you've accepted them as a friend.

Death is instantaneous; it does not linger with you. It comes and immediately goes. And no thing is a great evil or something to be feared if it has an ending, more so if that ending is instantaneous. Death is among the primordial anxieties, so one cannot live a life free of anxiety as long as the fear of death remains present.

Most people are afraid of death, but also do not how to truly live. They become stuck in a state of not wanting to live but not knowing hot to die.

No good thing benefits us while we have it unless we are mentally prepared for the loss of it.

When trying to live a better life, worry about fulfilling your ideals and don't bother about other people's impressions of you. You should not be living a better life for the praise of the others.

Where hope goes, so does fear. The past reminds us the fear, while the future anticipates it. But fear does not exist within the present. If there is danger, you feel in danger and act accordingly. If there is no danger, you feel calm and composed. By living in the present, you get rid of regret of the past and anxiety of the future.

One must seek friendships to further improve oneself in virtues. To have friends not so that the friends would help you in your time of need; the opposite in fact - you must seek friends so that you can help them in their time of need.

Even though a wise man does not *need* friends, it is good to have them and one should strive to have them despite the lack of need for them. After all, the only thing a wise man needs is a sound and upright mind. The seeking of friendship is a natural instinct.

No one is happy who does not believe themselves to be. And you must think this way not just once or twice, but continuously over time.

Certain natural flaws, such as blushing, or knees feeling weak in front of crowds, and such can be treated, but not completely overcome. Their presence does not mean that you're failing in your pursuit of a good life. They are present even in wise men.

Treat and live every single day as though it is your last.

Challenges/adversity breed courage. Never surrender or give up no matter how miserable the universe makes your life. Come out of it stronger than ever before.

No matter the situation, always ask yourself what the situation is truly and objectively is like; don't just listen to and believe the words of others.

Whenever you are worried about something potentially happening in the future, gather all the evidence you can and objectively evaluate just how likely it is to happen. Don't just believe in rumors without evidence. Don't be too quick to give way to opinion.

Uncertainty is more terrifying than certainty as certainty by definition always has a limit, while uncertainty does not. Thus, whenever possible, try to convert an uncertain fear into something concrete and something limited, that has a distinctive end or result.

Treat your body as a tool. It is not a part of you. There is no need to fear losing your body or something bad happening to it. It is not you; harming your body does not harm you. Avoid running away from inevitable dangers or seeking comfort and safety as well. However, also avoid provoking others or making enemies with others. Steer away from such situations, but do it in a way that others do not perceive you as avoiding them.

Philosophy is a practical discipline. It is more about actions than it is about words.

Things like poverty, starvation, and such, that we typically avoid and look down on, can be useful for building character and acquiring things that cannot be acquired elsewhere. They can build a strong foundation that makes one not fear anything in life for there isn't anything that can hurt one that has survived through these obstacles. Thus, try living under these conditions for some time to see that there's nothing to be afraid of.

Desires are endless. Even when you set a limit to yourself to accomplish them and be done with it all, when one ends, a new one begins. Even if you satisfy all of your current desires, brand new ones will appear after. You cannot keep chasing them for you will waste all of your life in this chase. You must learn to let go of these desires instead.

People, especially when it comes to positions of power, come to you not because of you, but because of what they might gain from you.

While a life of struggle can have its own benefits, it's unwise to seek struggle just for the sake of struggle. When having the option, a Stoic would choose a life of peace instead of trouble.

Speech should be meaningful so that it hits someone deep, just like a medicine that stays in one's body to cure them. Speech should not be rushed or disorderly, but come in a manner that others can understand easily. Thus, speak slowly.
