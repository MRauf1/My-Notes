# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Astigmatism)[^1]
> An optical defect in which the [[Point Spread Function]] is not circularly symmetric — narrower along one orientation than another — so that spatial resolution varies with the orientation of the imaged pattern.

# Properties
- Because the [[Point Spread Function]] is orientation-dependent, the [[Line Spread Function]] measured along different orientations differs, and no single [[Accommodation|focus adjustment]] can bring every orientation into sharp focus at the same time.
- In the human eye, astigmatism is commonly modeled as the combined defocus of two one-dimensional imaging systems oriented at right angles to each other, with the defocus at intermediate angles predicted from these two.

[^1]: [Foundations of Vision (Wandell)](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/YYQVJZJ3?page=33&annotation=LH9UEZFB)
