# Definition
> [!info] Relationship with the Present Moment (Tolle)[^1]
> Since life is inseparable from the Now, your relationship with the present moment *is* your relationship with life. Tolle defines the ego simply as "a dysfunctional relationship with the present moment". The ego treats the Now in three ways:
> 1. **As a means to an end**: useful only to reach a more important future moment, so you are never fully here.
> 2. **As an obstacle**: life becomes a series of problems to solve before you can be happy, which brings impatience, frustration, and stress. Each solved problem is followed by another.
> 3. **As an enemy**: the ego's greatest enemy is the present moment, i.e., life itself.

# Properties
- **Three patterns of resentment** that block peace: something *needs to happen* first (and resentment might make it happen); something *happened* that shouldn't have; something *is happening* that shouldn't be.[^2]
- How to be at peace now? "By making peace with the present moment." Decide to make the present moment your friend and welcome it in whatever disguise. Life then seems to become friendlier. The decision must be made "again and again and again" until it is natural, and it is the end of the ego.[^3]
- Seeing the dysfunctional relationship in yourself is already presence: "The moment you see the dysfunction, it begins to dissolve."[^4]
- There is only ever this moment; past and future exist only as thoughts in it. "The elimination of time from your consciousness is the elimination of ego. It is the only true spiritual practice."[^5]
- While the ego runs your life there are two ways to be unhappy: not getting what you want, and getting what you want.[^6]
- Reacting against the form life takes now strengthens your form identity; nonresistance frees consciousness from form.[^7]
- Only by resisting what happens are you at the mercy of it.[^8]
- Seeing the present as marred or deficient hides "the deeper perfection that is inherent in life itself".[^9]
- To end human misery, start by taking responsibility for your inner state now. With awareness of a negative state comes disidentification: you become the witnessing presence.[^10]
