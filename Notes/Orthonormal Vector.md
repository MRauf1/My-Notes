# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Orthonormal [[Vector]])[^1]
> A finite or [[Countably Infinite]] [[Set]] $\{e_i\}$ of [[Vector]] in $V$ is called orthonormal if $\langle e_j, e_k \rangle = \begin{cases}0 & j \neq k \\ 1 & j = k\end{cases}$.
