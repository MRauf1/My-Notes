# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Equivalence Relation]] on [[Set]] and [[Partition]])[^1]
> Let $A$ be a [[Set]] and $F$ be a [[Partition]] on $A$. Then there exists an [[Equivalence Relation]] $R$ on $A$ such that $A / R = F$.
> In particular, $R = \cup_{X \in F} (X \times X)$ and this [[Equivalence Relation]] is unique given the set and partition. Furthermore, for $X \in F$ and $x \in X$, $[x]_R = X$.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=232)
