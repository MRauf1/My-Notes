# Definition
> [!info] Dark Energy
> A form of energy inherent to the vacuum of space itself, of unknown origin, whose repulsive gravitational effect has driven the [[Expansion of the Universe]] to accelerate since roughly 5 billion years ago.

# Properties
- Only became dynamically dominant once the density of matter and radiation had diluted sufficiently through billions of years of expansion, since dark energy density — unlike matter density — does not decrease as the universe expands.
- Distinct from, though phenomenologically similar in character to, the inflaton field thought to have driven [[Cosmic Inflation]]; some speculative models (e.g. "quintessential inflation") propose both are manifestations of a single evolving scalar field.
