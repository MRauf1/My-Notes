# Definition
> [!info] Life-Lie[^1]
> ([[Alfred Adler|Adler]]) Making up all kinds of pretexts to avoid one's [[Life Tasks]]: shifting responsibility for one's situation to other people or the environment.

# Properties
- **Manufactured flaws.** One does not dislike someone because of their flaws. One first has the goal of disliking them, to avoid the relationship, and then looks for flaws. People can find flaws in anyone whenever they want to ([[Teleology (Adlerian Psychology)]]).[^2]
- It denies that one chose one's own [[Lifestyle (Adlerian Psychology)|lifestyle]].[^3]
- **Not a moral issue.** Adler does not discuss life tasks or life-lies in terms of good and evil, but in terms of courage.[^4]
- Living to satisfy others' expectations is also a lie to oneself and to those around one. See [[Desire for Recognition]].[^5]
- **The greatest life-lie** is not living here and now: focusing on the past and future to avoid the present. See [[Energeial Life]].[^6]
- The [[Inferiority Complex]] is a common form. Compare bad faith in [[Responsibility (Existential Psychology)]].
