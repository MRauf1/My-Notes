# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Derivative|Differentiation]] [[Operator]])[^1]
> In [[Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz|Leibniz]] notation, the differentiation operator is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{d}{dx}
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!info] Definition 2 ([[Operator]]-Centric)[^2]
> Differentiation [[Operator]] $D: C^1[a, b] \rightarrow C[a, b]$ is defined by $Df(x) = f'(x)$.
