# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Norm]] [[Addition]] [[Triangle Inequality]])[^1]
> For [[Vector]] $\mathbf{p}, \mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the norm addition is
> $$
> ||\mathbf{p} + \mathbf{q}|| \leq ||\mathbf{p}|| + ||\mathbf{q}||
> $$

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Reverse [[Triangle Inequality]])[^1]
> For [[Vector]] $\mathbf{p}, \mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{R}^n$,
> $$
> \big| ||\mathbf{p}|| - ||\mathbf{q}|| \big| \leq ||\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{q}||
> $$

Equality holds in **either** inequality above if and only if $\mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{q}$ point in the same direction (one is a nonnegative multiple of the other). If they are merely [[Collinear]] with a negative scalar (opposite directions), both inequalities are strict.

This is the $\mathbb{R}^n$/[[Dot Product]] special case of the general [[Normed Space]] result; see [[Triangle Inequality]] for the generalized statement and full equality-case analysis.

[^1]: [Elementary Differential Geometry](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/F6CCEWIU?page=59)
