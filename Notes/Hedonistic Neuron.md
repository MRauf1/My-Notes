# Definition
> [!info] Hedonistic Neuron[^1]
> Harry Klopf's hypothesis that "the neuron is a hedonist": each neuron works to maximise some local approximate notion of "pleasure" and minimise "pain", and the complexity of animal behaviour arises from such cellular hedonists wired into increasingly complex systems.

# Properties
- Klopf held that positive feedback, not just negative (homeostatic) feedback, is the dominant force of life: a homeostatic system is "happy" and stops when its error is zero, whereas he wanted a system that is never satisfied, driving incessant exploration.[^2]
- Neuroscientific evidence (as of 2018) suggests the hypothesis, neurons governed by individual [[Law of Effect|laws of effect]], may not be far off.[^3]
- Influenced the development of [[Reinforcement Learning]].
