# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Theorem 6.27)[^1]
> Let $T \in \mathcal{L}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and let $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ be [[Jordan Measurable|Jordan measurable]]. Then
> $$
> vol(T(\Omega)) = |\det T| \, vol(\Omega)
> $$

The absolute value of the determinant of a linear map is exactly its volume-scaling factor: $T$ expands or contracts the [[Volume]] of every measurable region by the same factor $|\det T|$, regardless of the region's shape.
