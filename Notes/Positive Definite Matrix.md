# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Theorem 5.20)[^1]
> Let $A \in M_n(\mathbb{C})$ be [[Self-Adjoint Linear Map|Hermitian]]. The following are equivalent:
> 1. Every [[Eigenvalue]] of $A$ is positive.
> 2. For every $x \in \mathbb{C}^n$ with $x \neq 0$, $\langle Ax, x \rangle > 0$.

> [!info] Definition 2 (Positive Definite)[^2]
> A matrix satisfying the equivalent properties of Theorem 1 is called positive definite.

> [!abstract] Theorem 3 (Exercise 5.4.10b)[^3]
> Let $A \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{C})$. If $rank(A) = n$ (full column rank), then $A^*A$ is positive definite.
