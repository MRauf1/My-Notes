# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1
> For a [[Function|function]] $f$, $f$ has a local minimum at the [[Point|point]] $(a, b)$ $\iff$ $\exists$ an open [[Interval Notation|interval]] $I$ such that $f(a) \leq f(x)$ $\forall x \in I$.[^1]

> [!info] Definition 2 (Local Minimum on [[Metric Space]])[^2]
> Let $f$ be a real function defined on a metric space $X$. Then $f$ has a local minimum at a point $p \in X$ if there exists $\delta > 0$ such that $f(q) \geq f(p)$ for all $q \in X$ with $d(p, q) < \delta$.

Endpoints cannot be local minimum.
