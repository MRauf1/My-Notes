# Definition

The opposite of [[Deep Work]]. Newport's definition: "Noncognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks, often performed while distracted. These efforts tend not to create much new value in the world and are easy to replicate."[^1]

Increasing evidence shows that the more time you spend pursuing shallow work, the more permanently you reduce your ability to perform deep work.[^2]

It is to be tamed, not eliminated (see [[Deep Work Capacity]]).

[^1]: [Newport, p. 7](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/J6AK883D?page=7&annotation=GCY8EMXP); [p. 134](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/J6AK883D?page=134&annotation=7ILL34FD)
