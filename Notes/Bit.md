# Definition
> [!definition] Bit[^1]
> A bit is a single 0 or 1, the most basic unit of storage in main memory.

# Properties
- [[Main Memory]] is, in its most raw form, a large storage area of bits.
- A particular arrangement of bits is called a [[State (Computing)|state]].
- Also called a binary digit: one of the two numbers in base 2 (0 or 1). The computer alphabet is just these two letters because on/off electrical signals are the easiest for hardware to distinguish, and any [[Instruction]] can be thought of as a number expressed in bits.[^2]
