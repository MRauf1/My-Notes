# Definition
> [!definition] Bit[^1]
> A bit is a single 0 or 1, the most basic unit of storage in main memory.

# Properties
- [[Main Memory]] is, in its most raw form, a large storage area of bits.
- A particular arrangement of bits is called a [[State (Computing)|state]].
