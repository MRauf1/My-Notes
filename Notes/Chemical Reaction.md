# Definition
> [!info] Chemical Reaction[^1]
> A process in which atoms rearrange their partners, forming new molecular combinations — as distinguished from a physical process, in which the same atomic or molecular partners persist throughout.

# Properties
- Contrasted with [[Dynamic Equilibrium]] processes such as [[Evaporation]] or dissolution, which are physical processes that do not rearrange atomic partners.
- The distinction between chemical and physical processes is not sharp.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 1: Atoms in Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_01.html)
