# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Power Property of Exponents)[^1]
> For $x \in \mathbb{R}, m, n \in \mathbb{Z}$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> (x^m)^n = x^{m n}
> \end{align}
> $$

Raising a base with an [[Exponentiation|exponent]] to a power is [[Equality|equal]] to the base with the powers [[Multiplication|multiplied]].

# Properties
- This property is stated for integer $m, n$ only. It does not extend directly to rational exponents on a negative base: if $x < 0$ and a rational exponent (in lowest terms) has an even numerator, evaluating that exponent forces a nonnegative result (e.g. $x^{2/3} = (x^{1/3})^2 \geq 0$), so the sign of $x$ is lost. This is why $(x^{2/3})^{3/2} = |x|$ rather than $x$.
