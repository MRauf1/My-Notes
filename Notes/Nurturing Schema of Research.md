# Definition
> [!info] Nurturing Schema of Research[^1]
> (Alon) A mental model of the research process in which one starts at a question A heading toward answer B, the path soon meanders and loops as experiments fail and assumptions collapse ("the cloud"), and in the midst of confusion a new problem C is sensed in the materials at hand; if C is more interesting and feasible than B, one heads toward C instead.

# Types
- *Objective schema* - research as the shortest path from question A to answer B, as papers are written. It risks treating students as a means to an end, and makes any deviation (failed experiments, struggling students) intolerable, causing stress through the cognitive dissonance between reality and the [[Schema|schema]].[^2]
- *Nurturing schema* - meandering is an integral part of the craft rather than a nuisance; the mentor's task is to support students through the cloud that guards the entry into the unknown, leaving room to notice that C exists and may be more worthwhile than plodding toward B.[^3]

# Properties
- Celebrates the courage needed to sail into the unknown repeatedly and the openness needed to see something different from, and usually richer and stranger than, expectations.[^4]
- Grounded in the view that a lab is a nurturing environment that aims to maximise the potential of students as scientists and as human beings.[^5]
- Complements problem choice via the [[Feasibility-Interest Diagram]].
