# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Geometric Optics)[^1]
> An approximation of how light propagates and interacts with surfaces, treating light as traveling along straight-line rays that bend only at material interfaces according to simple laws such as [[Snell's Law]], while ignoring wave effects of light such as [[Diffraction]].

# Properties
- Consistent with the [[Light Ray]] abstraction, which likewise ignores diffraction effects.
- The approximation under which a [[Lens]] is analyzed to derive quantities such as its [[Focal Length (Lens)|focal length]] via the [[Lensmaker's Equation]].

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Lenses](https://visionbook.mit.edu/lenses.html)
