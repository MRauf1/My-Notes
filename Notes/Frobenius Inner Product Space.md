# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Frobenius [[Inner Product Space]])[^1]
> [[Inner Product]] on the [[Vector Space]] of $M_{m, n}(\mathbb{C})$ with the [[Inner Product]]
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \langle A, B \rangle = tr(A^* B)
> \end{align}
> $$

For $B \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{C})$ with columns $b_1, \dots, b_n$, $\langle B, B \rangle = tr(B^* B) = \sum_i \lVert b_i \rVert^2$, which is exactly $\lVert B \rVert_F^2$, the square of the [[Frobenius Norm]].[^2]
