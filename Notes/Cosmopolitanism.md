# Definition
> [!info] Cosmopolitanism[^1]
> The view, attributed to [[Socrates]] and Diogenes and held by the Stoics, that one is a "citizen of the universe" rather than of any particular city, since all humans share reason and kinship with God.

# Properties
- By nature all human beings, whatever their status, are equal because they all possess reason.[^2]
- Those under one's authority are "kinsmen, brothers by nature, offspring of Zeus", so a higher station is no licence for despotism.[^3]
- Diogenes was "the only one whose country was the whole world": captured by pirates or sold into slavery, he lived as he had in Athens.[^4]
- Follows from [[Stoic Theology]].
