# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Mixed Number)
> $x \in \mathbb{N}, y_1, y_2 \in \mathbb{Z}, y_2 \neq 0$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> x \frac{y_1}{y_2}
> \end{align}
> $$

A combination of a [[Natural Number|natural number]] and a [[Proper Fraction|proper fraction]], where the natural number represents how many wholes the number has, while the fraction represents how many parts of a whole the number has.[^1] The whole part is restricted to a natural number (rather than any integer) because negative mixed numbers are represented by placing a single sign in front of the whole expression (e.g. $-3\frac{1}{2}$ means $-(3+\frac{1}{2})$), not by letting the whole part itself be negative.
