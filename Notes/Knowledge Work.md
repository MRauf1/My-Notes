# Definition
> [!info] Knowledge Work (Newport)[^1]
> The economic activity in which knowledge is transformed into an artifact with market value through the application of cognitive effort.

# Properties
- Unlike farming and manufacturing, it has highly variable effort, complex and constantly shifting workloads, and work organization left largely to individuals.[^2]
- It lacks concrete productivity metrics, which led to [[Pseudo-Productivity]] and opened the [[Metric Black Hole]].[^3]
- Related: [[Deep Work]], [[Shallow Work]], [[Slow Productivity]].
