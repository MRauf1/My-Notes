# Definition
> [!info] Flexible Focus (Vartanian)[^1]
> Oshin Vartanian's finding, from studying the workflows of Nobel laureates and other eminent scientists, that what distinguishes them is not relentless focus but flexible focus. They alternate between extraordinary focus on specific concepts and playful exploration of ideas, applying strategies flexibly to the task's demands. "The key to creativity is being able to switch between a wide-open, playful mind and a narrow analytical frame." (Dean, 2013)

# Properties
- **Kinds of attention.** *Focused* attention is on one thing and lasts only seconds. *Sustained* attention keeps us on one task for longer and is needed to learn, understand, or finish anything; it is the kind most threatened by distraction. Flow is a third, effortless kind.[^2]
- Sustained attention can be trained by avoiding multitasking, removing distractions, and separating different kinds of tasks (cf. [[Multitask]], [[Deep Work]]).[^3]
- Academic writing needs the whole spectrum of attention. Science, like art, needs both focused and floating attention.[^4]
- Compare [[Focused Mode]] and [[Unconscious Thought Theory]].
