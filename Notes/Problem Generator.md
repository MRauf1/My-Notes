# Definition
> [!info] Problem Generator[^1]
> The component of a [[Learning Agent|learning agent]] responsible for suggesting exploratory actions that will lead to new and informative experiences, rather than only the actions that are best given current knowledge.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 75](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=75&annotation=TVL4FY2C)
