# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linearly Independent]] [[Basis]] [[Dimension]])[^1]
> Suppose that $dim(V) = n$ and $\mathcal{B} = (v_1, \dots, v_n)$ is a [[Linearly Independent]] list in $V$. Then $\mathcal{B}$ is a [[Basis]] for $V$.

In the above case, the [[Span]] requirement for a [[Basis]] comes for free.
