# Definition
> [!info] Deep Work Capacity (Ericsson)[^1]
> The daily limit on intense, deliberate cognitive effort ([[Deliberate Practice]]): about **1 hour per day for novices** and up to about **4 hours per day for experts**, but rarely more. Past this limit, extra deep work gives diminishing returns.

# Properties
- Deep work is exhausting because it pushes you to the limit of your abilities.[^1]
- Hence the goal is to *tame* shallow work's footprint, not eliminate it; you can't fill a whole day with depth.[^2]
- Newport himself built his schedule around 3–4 hours a day, 5 days a week, of uninterrupted, directed concentration, rarely working past 5–6 p.m..[^3]
- Capacity can be trained over time (see [[Focus Improvement]]).
