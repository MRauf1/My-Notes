# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Tetrahedron)[^1]
> A polyhedron with four [[Triangle|triangular]] faces, six edges, and four vertices. It is the 3-dimensional simplex: the convex hull of four affinely independent points. The regular tetrahedron, all of whose faces are equilateral triangles, is one of the five [[Regular Polyhedron|regular polyhedra]].

# Properties

## [[Symmetry]]
![[Screenshot 2025-07-10 175731.png]]
- $G_{tetra} \cong A_4 \subseteq S_4$, where $A_4$ is the [[Alternating Group]] on 4 elements: the [[Permutation Parity|even permutations]] of $\{1,2,3,4\}$.
