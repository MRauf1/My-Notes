# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Irreflexive [[Relation]])[^1]
> [[Relation]] $R$ is irreflexive if for all $x \in A$, $(x, x) \notin R$.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=218)
