# Definition
> [!info] Avenues to Meaning (Frankl)[^1]
> According to [[Logotherapy]], the meaning of life always changes but never ceases to be, and can be discovered in three ways:
> 1. **Creative values** - creating a work or doing a deed.
> 2. **Experiential values** - experiencing something (goodness, truth, beauty, nature, culture) or encountering someone in their uniqueness, i.e. loving them.
> 3. **Attitudinal values** - the attitude one takes toward unavoidable suffering.

# Properties
- The third avenue is the most important: even the helpless victim of a fate that cannot be changed may rise above and change himself, turning personal tragedy into triumph; hence life's meaning is **unconditional**, lasting to the last moment ([[Suffering]]).[^2]
- Experiencing can be as valuable as achieving, compensating for our one-sided emphasis on the external world of achievement (Weisskopf-Joelson).[^3]
- Love is the only way to grasp another person's innermost core, seeing and helping actualise their unrealised potential ([[Love]]).[^4]
- Meaning is to be discovered in the world rather than in one's psyche ([[Self-Transcendence]]); the logotherapist widens the patient's visual field so the whole spectrum of potential meaning becomes visible, neither teaching nor preaching.[^5]
- The meaning of life differs from person to person and moment to moment; one should not seek an abstract meaning but one's concrete, irreplaceable vocation. Ultimately it is not we who ask life for its meaning but **life that questions us**, and we answer by being responsible.[^6]
- The cognition of meaning lies between an "aha" experience and a Gestalt perception: becoming aware of a possibility against the background of reality, of what can be done in a situation; **conscience** is the prompter that indicates the direction, applying a hierarchy of values that "we are" rather than consciously adopt.[^7]
- Correspond to Yalom's summary in [[Cosmic and Terrestrial Meaning]]; see also [[Meaning of Life]].
