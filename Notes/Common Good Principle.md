# Definition
> [!info] Common Good Principle[^1]
> Superintelligence should be developed only for the benefit of all of humanity and in the service of widely shared ethical ideals.

# Properties
- Supported by the benefits of collaboration in AI development: less haste, more safety investment, avoided conflict, shared control-problem ideas, and more equitable distribution of gains ([[Differential Technological Development]]).[^2]
- Physical limits on sequential computation mean resources may not convert into lifespan or mental performance at arbitrary scales, so extreme resource concentration yields diminishing personal returns, strengthening the case for sharing.[^3]
- Closely aligned with [[Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence]].
