# Definition
> [!info] Oikeiosis[^1]
> (Appropriation) The Stoic thesis that every creature is naturally "appropriated" to its own interest, loving it first and foremost; what one's behaviour becomes depends on where one places "I" and "mine": in the body, in externals, or in the [[Prohairesis|will]].

# Properties
- If a creature judges something (even a brother, parent, or god) to impede its interest, it loathes and attacks it.[^1]
- If one's interest is placed on the same side of the scales as piety, honour, family, and country, all are safe; set against them, they are all lost.[^1]
- Only if I identify with my will will I be the friend, son, and father I should be, since then my interest is to preserve trustworthiness and self-respect.[^1]
- Hence friendship exists only where people locate the good in will and in the right use of impressions; kinship or shared schooling is no guarantee.[^2]
- The ability to love belongs only to the wise, since love follows prizing what is good and only they can tell good from indifferent.[^3]
- Cf. [[Socratic Intellectualism]].
