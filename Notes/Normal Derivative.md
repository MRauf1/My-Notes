# Definition
> [!info] Normal Derivative[^1]
> For a domain $D$ with outward unit normal $\mathbf{n}$ on $\partial D$, the normal derivative of $u$ at a boundary point is the [[Directional Derivative]] in the direction of $\mathbf{n}$:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{\partial u}{\partial n} \equiv \mathbf{n} \cdot \nabla u.
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- Appears in the [[Neumann Boundary Condition]] and [[Robin Boundary Condition]].
- Measures the flux of $u$ out of $D$ (up to a sign and a material constant), e.g. in Fick's and Fourier's laws.
