# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Upper Triangular Matrix]] [[Lower Triangular Matrix]] [[Eigenvalue]])[^1]
> The [[Eigenvalue]] of [[Upper Triangular Matrix]] are its diagonal entries.
> The [[Eigenvalue]] of [[Lower Triangular Matrix]] are its diagonal entries.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Lemma 6.23 -- Multiplicity)[^2]
> If $A$ is upper triangular and $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of $A$, then the [[Algebraic Multiplicity of Eigenvalue|algebraic multiplicity]] of $\lambda$ is the number of times $\lambda$ appears on the diagonal of $A$.
