# Definition
> [!info] Inner Space (Tolle)[^1]
> Consciousness itself, unobstructed by form: the awareness in which experiences occur. Tolle contrasts it with *object consciousness*, in which everything perceived is immediately named, interpreted, compared, liked or disliked, and judged by the ego. Presence is a state of inner spaciousness.

# Properties
- Every experience has three possible ingredients: sense perceptions, thoughts or mental images, and emotions. The greatest obstacle to finding the *experiencer* is losing yourself in the experience.[^2]
- **Ways in**: become conscious of being conscious; be aware of space (which is being aware of awareness itself); be aware of your breathing; feel the [[Inner Body]] ("your body is just as spacious as the universe"); let go of the need to emphasize your form identity.[^3]
- **"The least thing"**: Nietzsche's line that "little maketh up the best happiness". Simple, unobtrusive things take up so little consciousness that the rest is inner space, which is where the happiness actually comes from.[^4]
- When present, you respond to what the moment needs, still, alert, and open.[^5]
- **Addiction practice.** When a compulsive urge arises, take three conscious breaths. Feel the urge as an energy field for a few minutes, then breathe again. If you still act it out, don't make it a problem. Catch the justifying thoughts and ask "Who is talking here?" (the addiction).[^6]
- Intimate relationships are the most sought-after cover-up for background unease and one of the most common letdowns.[^7]
- Tolle claims excessive TV watching is "largely responsible for attention deficit disorder". ADHD is a neurodevelopmental condition with strong genetic contributions; screen time is at most a correlate ([[Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder]]). His broader point stands: a short attention span makes perceptions and relationships shallow, because quality requires attention.[^8]
