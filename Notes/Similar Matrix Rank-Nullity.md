# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Similar Matrix]] [[Rank]]-[[Nullity]])[^1]
> Suppose $V, W$ are [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space]] and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$. Then $rank(T) = rank([T]_{\mathcal{B}_V, \mathcal{B}_W})$ and $null(T) = null([T]_{\mathcal{B}_V, \mathcal{B}_W})$ for any [[Basis]] $\mathcal{B}_V$ of $V$ and $\mathcal{B}_W$ of $W$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Corollary)[^1]
> If $A, B \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$ are [[Similar Matrix]], then $rank(A) = rank(B)$ and $null(A) = null(B)$.
