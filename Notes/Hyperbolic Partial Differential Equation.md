# Definition
> [!info] Hyperbolic Partial Differential Equation[^1]
> The [[Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]] $\sum_{i,j} a_{ij} u_{x_i x_j} + \sum_i a_i u_{x_i} + a_0 u = 0$ is hyperbolic if none of the [[Eigenvalue|eigenvalues]] $d_1, \dots, d_n$ of its coefficient matrix $A$ vanish and exactly one has the opposite sign from the other $n - 1$. In two variables: $a_{12}^2 > a_{11} a_{22}$.

# Properties
- Canonical form $u_{x_1 x_1} - u_{x_2 x_2} - \cdots - u_{x_n x_n} + \cdots = 0$; prototype: the [[Wave Equation]] (also [[Damped Wave Equation]], [[Klein-Gordon Equation]], [[Inhomogeneous Wave Equation]]).
- The distinguished direction plays the role of time; needs two [[Initial Condition|initial conditions]] (position and velocity).
- Finite speed of propagation; information is transported along characteristics and singularities persist ([[Principle of Causality (Wave Equation)]], [[Wave and Diffusion Equation Comparison]]).
- See [[Canonical Form of Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]].
