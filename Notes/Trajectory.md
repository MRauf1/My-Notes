# Definition
> [!info] Trajectory
> In a [[Markov Decision Process]], the interaction record $\tau = (s_1, a_1, r_1, s_2, \dots, s_{H+1})$ generated by an agent acting in the environment, called a trajectory of length $H$.

# Properties
- Generated by starting at state $s_1$ (possibly sampled from an initial distribution $d_0 \in \Delta(\mathcal{S})$) and, at each time step $t = 1, 2, \dots$, taking action $a_t \sim \pi(s_t)$, receiving reward $r_t = R(s_t, a_t)$, and transitioning to $s_{t+1} \sim P(s_t, a_t)$.
- The [[Discounted Return]] and [[Value Function|value]]/[[Action-Value Function|action-value]] functions are defined as expectations over the randomness of a trajectory.
