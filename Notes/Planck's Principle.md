# Definition
> [!info] Planck's Principle[^1]
> "A new scientific truth does not triumph by convincing its opponents and making them see the light, but rather because its opponents eventually die, and a new generation grows up that is familiar with it."

# Properties
- Reflects that paradigm change is a conversion rather than a proof, and that those who invent new paradigms are usually young or new to the field ([[Scientific Revolution]]).
- Echoes the disappearance of older schools once a synthesis attracts the next generation, with holdouts read out of the profession ([[Pre-Paradigm Science]]).

[^1]: [Kuhn, 1970, p. 163](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/VILEAVJE?page=163&annotation=Y4VYHHAD)
