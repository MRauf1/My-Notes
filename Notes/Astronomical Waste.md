# Definition
> [!info] Astronomical Waste[^1]
> Bostrom's argument that delayed technological development carries an enormous opportunity cost: every moment, stars heat empty rooms and energy is lost to entropy that an advanced civilisation could have used to sustain vast numbers of worthwhile lives, so each second of delay forfeits on the order of a hundred trillion potential lives.

# Properties
- Yet the far larger stake is whether such a civilisation is ever reached: in utilitarian terms, raising the probability of success by one percentage point equals speeding up progress by ten million years.[^2]
- Hence the conclusion is not haste but caution: reducing existential risk dominates acceleration; it seems much easier for a researcher "to reduce extinction risk by one part in a million than to speed up progress by a thousand years."[^3]
- Underlies the reasoning of [[Differential Technological Development]] and the [[Person-Affecting and Impersonal Perspectives|impersonal perspective]]; the resources at stake are the cosmic endowment discussed under [[Singleton]].
