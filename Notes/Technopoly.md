# Definition
> [!info] Technopoly (Postman)[^1]
> Neil Postman's term (1993) for a culture in which technology is deified and alternatives to it are not banned but made invisible: "It does not make them illegal. It does not make them immoral. It does not even make them unpopular. It makes them invisible and therefore irrelevant." Its modern form is *Internet-centrism* (Evgeny Morozov): the belief that anything related to "the Internet" is good, regardless of its effect on producing value.

# Properties
- [[Deep Work]] is at a severe disadvantage in a technopoly. It rests on old-fashioned values (quality, craftsmanship, mastery) and often requires rejecting what is new and high-tech.[^2]
- Depth loses to tweets, likes, and posts not because it is empirically inferior, but because the [[Metric Black Hole]] hides the comparison. Network behaviors are taught as necessary "for no other reason than that they exist".[^2]
- For the individual this is an opportunity: depth becomes rarer and therefore more valuable ([[Deep Work Rarity]]).[^3]
