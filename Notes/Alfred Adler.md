# Definition
> [!info] Alfred Adler[^1]
> Austrian psychiatrist (1870–1937) who founded [[Adlerian Psychology|individual psychology]]. He was an early core member of Freud's Vienna Psychoanalytic Society and broke away in 1911 over theoretical disagreements.

# Properties
- Adler and Freud were close in age and worked as equals. Jung, by contrast, revered Freud as a father figure.[^1]
- "Life in general has no meaning. Whatever meaning life has must be assigned to it by the individual."[^2]
