The Law of Cosines is the generalization of the [[Pythagorean Theorem]] to arbitrary [[Triangle|triangles]].

# Special Case
- [[Pythagorean Theorem]]: When the angle $\gamma$ opposite side $c$ is a right angle, $\cos \gamma = 0$ and the law reduces to $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$.
