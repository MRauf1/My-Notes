# Definition
> [!info] Inner Body (Tolle)[^1]
> The felt "aliveness" of the body from within, i.e., its inner energy field. Tolle identifies it with *chi* and calls it the doorway into [[Being (Spirituality)|Being]], lying halfway between the world of form and [[The Unmanifested]]. It is accessed by directing attention away from thinking and into the body, feeling rather than thinking about it.

# Properties
- **Practice.** Feel life in the hands, arms, legs, feet, abdomen, and chest. Prefer the feeling to any image, because images are already defined in form. The feeling is formless and can always be deepened.[^2]
- **Meditation** (10–15 min): sit upright without leaning back, relax, close your eyes, take a few deep breaths into the lower abdomen, and then feel the whole inner energy field. Merge with it until the observer–observed duality dissolves: "By going deeply into the body, you have transcended the body." Afterwards, look around without labeling while still feeling the inner body.[^3]
- Keeping attention in the body anchors you in the Now and makes it easy to observe the mind. Use waiting time for it, and keep some attention within during daily life.[^4]
- **Transformation through the body, not away from it.** Religions spread the belief that "you are not your body", which led to asceticism and body denial. Tolle claims no one has become enlightened by fighting the body or through out-of-body experiences.[^5]
- An unacknowledged emotion blocks access to the inner body, so feel and accept it first. In a fully functional organism an emotion is short-lived. Without body presence it can linger for weeks and merge into the [[Pain-Body]].[^6]
- Use the mind together with the inner body: listen to others with the whole body, and think creatively from a body-rooted state of conscious stillness.[^7]
- Conscious breathing is a powerful meditation that gradually puts you in touch with the body (cf. [[Breath as Meditation Object]]).[^8]
- Tolle further claims it slows aging, makes the body's molecular structure "less dense", and strengthens both the immune system and a "psychic immune system". He offers no evidence and invites readers to test these claims themselves.[^9]
