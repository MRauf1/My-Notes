# Definition
> [!info] CIA Triad[^1]
> The three commonly accepted goals used to judge whether a system is secure: confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

# Types
- Confidentiality — only authorized parties can access information.
- Integrity — information can only be modified by authorized parties, in authorized ways.
- Availability — authorized parties can access information and resources when they need to.
