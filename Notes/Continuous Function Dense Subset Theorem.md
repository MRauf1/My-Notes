# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Continuous Function]] [[Dense Subset]] Theorem)[^1]
> Let $f, g: X \rightarrow Y$ be continuous mappings of a [[Metric Space]] $X$ into a [[Metric Space]] $Y$, and let $E$ be a [[Dense Subset]] of $X$. Then
> 1) $f(E)$ is dense in $f(X)$;
> 2) if $g(p) = f(p)$ for all $p \in E$, then $g(p) = f(p)$ for all $p \in X$.

A continuous mapping is determined by its values on a dense subset of its domain.

# Properties
- Uniqueness half of [[Uniformly Continuous Function Extension of Function Theorem]]: a continuous [[Extension of Function|extension]] from a dense subset, if it exists, is unique.

[^1]: [Principles of Mathematical Analysis](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/3BD27IHF?page=108)
