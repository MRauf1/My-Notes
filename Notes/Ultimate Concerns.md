# Definition
> [!info] Ultimate Concerns[^1]
> (Yalom) The four givens of existence, discovered by deep personal reflection (in solitude, silence, time, and freedom from everyday distraction), each of which generates a core existential conflict:
> - **Death** - the awareness of death's inevitability versus the wish to continue to be.
> - **Freedom** - the absence of external structure: we are the authors of our world, so beneath us is groundlessness, versus our wish for ground and structure.
> - **Isolation** - an unbridgeable gap between oneself and any other being and the world, versus our wish for contact, protection, and belonging to a larger whole.
> - **Meaninglessness** - a meaning-seeking creature thrown into a universe with no inherent meaning.

$$\begin{align}
\text{awareness of ultimate concern} \longrightarrow \text{anxiety} \longrightarrow \text{defense mechanism}
\end{align}$$

# Properties
- These sources of dread are part of the human condition, not the province of the psychologically troubled; only the universality of suffering explains why "patienthood is ubiquitous".[^2]
- **Psychopathology** depends not on the mere presence of stress but on the interaction between ubiquitous existential stress and the individual's mechanisms of defense; it is by definition an ineffective defensive mode, and even successful defenses constrict growth.[^3]
- "If a way to the Better there be, it exacts a full look at the Worst" (Thomas Hardy).[^4]
- Deepest inquiry ("unconcealment", Heidegger) reveals that we are finite, must die, are free and cannot escape freedom, and are inexorably alone.[^5]
- Individual notes: [[Death Anxiety]], [[Responsibility (Existential Psychology)]], [[Existential Isolation]], [[Cosmic and Terrestrial Meaning]]; the basis of [[Existential Psychotherapy]].
