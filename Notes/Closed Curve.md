# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Closed [[Curve]])[^1]
> [[Curve]] $C$ whose starting and ending points are the same ($r(a) = r(b)$)

Rudin: a continuous $\gamma: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}^k$ with $\gamma(a) = \gamma(b)$.[^2] Contrast with an [[Arc]], which is one-to-one.

[^1]: [Calculus: Early Transcendentals](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EEFDQ9Y5?page=1121)
[^2]: [Principles of Mathematical Analysis](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/77RSE64H?page=145&annotation=B4BR6PHD)
