# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Conservative [[Vector Field]])[^1]
> Vector Field $V$ is conservative if
> $$
> \begin{align}
> V = \nabla f
> \end{align}
> $$
> for some [[Real-Valued Function]] $f$. $f$ is called a potential function of $V$.

# Properties
- [[Conservative Vector Field Existence With Curl Theorem]]
- The physical analogue is a [[Conservative Force]], such as gravity, whose potential function is (up to sign) its [[Potential Energy]].
