# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Orthonormal Basis]] [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space|Finite-Dimensional]] [[Inner Product Space]])[^1]
> Every [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space|finite-dimensional]] [[Inner Product Space]] has an [[Orthonormal Basis]].

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Corollary 4.13 -- [[Extension of Orthonormal List to Orthonormal Basis]])
> Suppose $\mathcal{B} = (v_1, \dots, v_k)$ is an [[Orthonormal Vector|orthonormal]] list in a [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space|finite-dimensional]] [[Inner Product Space]] $V$. Then $\mathcal{B}$ can be extended to an [[Orthonormal Basis]] $\mathcal{B}'$ of $V$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 3 (Corollary -- [[Orthogonal Vector]] List)
> Every non-zero [[Orthogonal Vector|orthogonal]] list in a [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space|finite-dimensional]] [[Inner Product Space]] can be extended to an [[Orthogonal Basis]] (or normalized and extended to an [[Orthonormal Basis]]).

> [!abstract] Theorem 4 (Corollary -- General List of [[Vector|Vectors]])
> Any [[Linearly Independent]] list of [[Vector|vectors]] in a [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space|finite-dimensional]] [[Inner Product Space]] can be transformed via the [[Gram-Schmidt Algorithm]] and extended to an [[Orthonormal Basis]] (or [[Orthogonal Basis]]) of $V$.
