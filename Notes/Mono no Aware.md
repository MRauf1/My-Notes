# Definition
> [!info] Mono no Aware[^1]
> (Japanese, roughly "the pathos of things") A poignant, gentle melancholy at the impermanence of things, together with the additional beauty that things acquire because their time is fleeting.

# Properties
- An aesthetic counterpart of the Buddhist mark of impermanence ([[Anicca]]): impermanence is a source of beauty rather than only of suffering.
- Related: [[Memento Mori]].

[^1]: [Burkeman, 2012, p. 121](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/CQLUAMWS?page=121&annotation=W8BIECIV)
