# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linear Dependence Lemma]] [[Linearly Independent]])[^1]
> Given a list of [[Vector]] $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ in $V$ with $v_1 \neq 0$, suppose that for each $k \in \{2, \dots, n\}$, $v_k \notin \langle v_1, \dots, v_{k-1} \rangle$. Then $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ is [[Linearly Independent]].

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=165)
