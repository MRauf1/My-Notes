# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Coordinates]] in [[Orthonormal Basis]])[^1]
> Let $\mathcal{B}_V = (e_1, \dots, e_n)$ and $\mathcal{B}_W = (f_1, \dots, f_m)$ be [[Orthonormal Basis]] of $V, W$ respectively. Then for every $v \in V$ and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$,
> $$
> \begin{align}
> [v]_{\mathcal{B}_V} &= \begin{bmatrix}\langle v, e_1 \rangle \\ \dots \\ \langle v, e_n \rangle\end{bmatrix} \\
> [[T]_{\mathcal{B}_V, \mathcal{B}_W}]_{jk} &= \langle Te_k, f_j \rangle
> \end{align}
> $$

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=261)
