# Definition
> [!info] Insight (Existential Psychotherapy)[^1]
> (Yalom) Insight effects change by (1) fostering the therapist–patient relationship and (2) liberating the patient's stifled [[Will (Existential Psychology)|will]] through four realisations:
> 1. "Only I can change the world I have created."
> 2. "There is no danger in change."
> 3. "To get what I really want, I must change."
> 4. "I have the power to change."

# Properties
- **Relationship**: the therapist's unconditional regard raises self-regard, which begets the belief that one has the right to wish and act; the will is first exercised safely in therapy and then generalises.[^2]
- (1) Responsibility is continuous: one is continuously creating oneself, so responsibility for past creation implies responsibility for future change; no one else can change us.[^3]
- (2) Patients often hold an unconscious fantasied calamity (engulfment, rejection, retribution, abandonment); naming it and enacting the dreaded behaviour by degrees extinguishes the dread.[^4]
- (3) Self-sabotage satisfies conflicting, often unconscious needs; each decision (including not deciding) has a "payoff", and the patient must relinquish needs that cost too much integrity.[^5]
- (4) Explanation counters "confusion-spawned impotence": "the explanation of a phenomenon is the first step toward control of that phenomenon".[^6]
- **Pragmatic truth of interpretation**: the process of providing insight matters more than its content; an interpretation is "true" to the extent that it offers a sense of potency. Id, ego, archetypes, and ego states are all fictions justified by explanatory power; better interpretations are credible, consistent, empirically bolstered, fit the patient's frame, "click", generalise, are heard, and are well timed.[^7]
- **Beyond "originology"**: explanation need not be past-based; future determinism (purpose, goals, awareness of death) and Galilean current field forces also explain behaviour. The past is constituted by the present (hermeneutic circle), and parents can be re-understood as struggling humans.[^8]
- Compare [[Existential Anxiety]] (mastery through naming).
