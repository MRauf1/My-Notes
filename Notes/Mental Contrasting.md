# Definition
> [!info] Mental Contrasting (Oettingen)[^1]
> Imagining a desired future and then the obstacles in present reality that stand in the way. Combined with if-then plans, it becomes *mental contrasting with implementation intentions* (MCII, or WOOP: Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan). Goggins: visualization "isn't simply about daydreaming of some trophy ceremony". You must also visualize the challenges likely to arise and how you will attack them.

# Properties
- **Evidence.**
	- Positive fantasies *alone* predict less effort and achievement (Oettingen and Mayer, 2002) and drain the energy to act (Kappes and Oettingen, 2011).
	- A meta-analysis found MCII improves goal attainment ($d \approx 0.28$) (Wang et al., 2021).
- **Answer "why?" in advance.** In every ordeal the question "Why am I here?" arises, and the urge to quit builds up gradually rather than all at once. Have your answer ready before the moment comes, and catalog your weaknesses so you can get ahead of them.[^2]
- **Backstops.** Pre-planned markers that signal you have gone off course, plus alternative routes to the same objective (a land-navigation term). They apply to race plans and daily schedules alike.[^3] This is contingency planning in the form of if-then plans.
- "Visualization will never compensate for work undone."[^4]
- Related: [[Implementation Intention]], [[Goal-Setting Theory]].
