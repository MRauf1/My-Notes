# Definition
> [!info] Universal Mental Factors[^1]
> (*Sabbacitta-sadharana cetasikas*) In the Abhidhamma ([[Tipitaka]]), the seven mental factors present in every moment of consciousness:
> 1. Contact (*phassa*)
> 2. Feeling (*vedana*)
> 3. Perception (*sanna*)
> 4. Volition (*cetana*; Gunaratana calls it "mental formations")
> 5. One-pointedness, i.e. concentration (*ekaggata*)
> 6. Life faculty (*jivitindriya*)
> 7. Attention (*manasikara*; Gunaratana calls it "awareness")

# Properties
- Because every conscious moment contains some one-pointedness, Vipassana needs only shallow, *momentary* concentration ([[Samadhi]]), not deep absorption.[^1]
- Insight should examine each factor (and each of the [[Five Aggregates]]) separately rather than lumping them all together as "feeling".[^2]
