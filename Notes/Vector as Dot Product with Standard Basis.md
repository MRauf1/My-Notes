# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Vector]] as [[Dot Product]] with [[Standard Basis Vector]])[^1]
> Given a [[Standard Basis]] in $\mathbb{R}^n$ and [[Vector]] $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$, for each $i$, $v_i = v \cdot e_i$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=246)
