# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Conditional Probability]] [[Multiplication]] Rule)[^1]
> $$
> \begin{align}
> P(A \cap B) = P(A) P(B | A) = P(B) P(A | B)
> \end{align}
> $$
> where $P(A) > 0$ in the first case and $P(B) > 0$ in the second case

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Generalized [[Conditional Probability]] [[Multiplication]] Rule)[^1]
> $$
> \begin{align}
> P(A_1 \cap A_2 \cap A_n) = P(A_1) P(A_2 | A_1) \dots P(A_n | A_{n-1} \cap \dots \cap A_2 \cap A_1)
> \end{align}
> $$
