# Definition
> [!info] Genus[^1]
> The genus $g$ of a surface is its number of handles.

# Properties
- **Classification**: among surfaces that are connected (one piece), [[Compact Set|compact]], orientable, and without boundary, the genus is the only topological invariant; two such surfaces are topologically equivalent iff they have the same genus.[^1]
- Related to the [[Euler Characteristic]] by $\chi = 2 - 2g$ (Euler–L'Huilier formula).[^1]
- The sphere has $g = 0$, the torus $g = 1$.

[^1]: [Crane, p. 22](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9N6JDPI2?page=23&annotation=EZ2RRU5L)
