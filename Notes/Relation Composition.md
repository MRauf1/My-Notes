# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Relation]] [[Composition]])[^1]
> For [[Relation]] $R: A \rightarrow B, S: B \rightarrow C$, their composition is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> S \circ R = \{(a, c) \in A \times C | \exists b \in B, (a, b) \in R \land (b, c) \in S\}
> \end{align}
> $$

The sets in the composition must match.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=187)
