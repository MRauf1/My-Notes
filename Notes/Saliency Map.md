# Definition
> [!info] Saliency Map[^1]
> A "heat map" highlighting which parts of an input (e.g. image regions) were most influential in a model's decision, used as a diagnostic sanity check that the system attends to what we think it should.

# Properties
- Showing where in an image a prediction comes from lends trust and validity to results.[^2]
- Combined with [[Multitask Learning]], saliency revealed previously overlooked sex differences in the retina, and where to find them.[^2]
- Criticised as operating on raw pixels rather than the high-level concepts humans think in, motivating [[Concept Activation Vector|TCAV]].[^3]
- A technique of [[Interpretability (Machine Learning)]].
