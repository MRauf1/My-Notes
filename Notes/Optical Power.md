# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Optical Power)[^1]
> A measure of how strongly a [[Lens]] bends incoming light rays, defined as the reciprocal of its [[Focal Length (Lens)|focal length]] $f$ measured in meters:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> P = \frac{1}{f}
> \end{align}
> $$
> specified in units of diopters.

# Properties
- Inversely related to focal length: a short-focal-length lens bends incident rays more strongly, and so has higher optical power, than a long-focal-length lens.
- The optical power of the human [[Eye Lens]] is adjusted during [[Accommodation]] to bring sources at different distances into sharp focus on the [[Retina]].
