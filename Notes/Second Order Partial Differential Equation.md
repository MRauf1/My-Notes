# Definition
> [!info] Second Order Partial Differential Equation[^1]
> A [[Partial Differential Equation]] whose highest-order [[Partial Derivative]] is of order 2. The most general second-order PDE in two [[Independent Variable|independent variables]] is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> F(x, y, u, u_x, u_y, u_{xx}, u_{xy}, u_{yy}) = 0.
> \end{align}
> $$
> More generally, in $n$ independent variables $\mathbf{x} = (x_1, \dots, x_n)$,
> $$
> \begin{align}
> F(\mathbf{x}, u, u_{x_1}, \dots, u_{x_n}, u_{x_1 x_1}, u_{x_1 x_2}, \dots, u_{x_n x_n}) = 0.
> \end{align}
> $$

# Types
- [[Second Order Linear Partial Differential Equation]]

# Properties
- The [[Wave Equation]], [[Diffusion Equation]], [[Laplace Equation]], and [[Schrödinger Equation]] are all second order.
