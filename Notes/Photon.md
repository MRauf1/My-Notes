# Definition
> [!info] Photon[^1]
> The massless quantum of the [[Electromagnetic Field]], carrying electromagnetic energy and momentum; the mediator of the electromagnetic interaction between charged particles in [[Quantum Electrodynamics]].

# Properties
- Has zero rest mass, and correspondingly can never be at rest: it always travels at the speed of light.
- Exhibits [[Wave-Particle Duality]]: the same electromagnetic phenomenon is described as an oscillating wave of a given frequency or as a stream of photons, depending on which description is more evident at that frequency.
- Is its own [[Antiparticle]].
- Coupled to all electrically charged particles; the strength of this coupling is set by the fine-structure constant, approximately 1/137.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 2: Basic Physics](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_02.html)
