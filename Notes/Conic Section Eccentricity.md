# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Focus–Directrix Definition of a Conic)[^1]
> Given a line $L$ (the directrix), a point $F$ not on $L$ (a focus) and $e > 0$ (the eccentricity), a [[Conic Section]] is the set of all points $P$ with
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \frac{\text{distance from } P \text{ to } F}{\text{distance from } P \text{ to } L} = e
> \end{align}
> $$

# Types
- $0 < e < 1$: [[Ellipse]] (agrees with [[Ellipse Eccentricity]] $c/a$).
- $e = 1$: [[Parabola]] (points equidistant from focus and directrix).
- $e > 1$: [[Hyperbola]].
- $e = 0$ is the limiting case of a [[Circle]], which has no directrix.[^2]

# Properties
- Unifies the three non-degenerate conics in a single definition; in polar coordinates centred at the focus it gives [[Conic Section Polar Form]].
