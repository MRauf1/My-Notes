# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linear Map]] [[Function Composition]])[^1]
> Let $U, V, W$ be [[Vector Space]]. If $T: U \rightarrow V, S: V \rightarrow W$ are [[Linear Map]], then $ST, TS$ are also [[Linear Map]].
