# Definition

> [!info] Theorem 1 (Finite Intersection Property)[^1]
> Let $(X, d)$ be a [[Metric Space]] and $\{K_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in A}$ be a collection of [[Compact Set|compact]] [[Subset|subsets]] of $X$ such that the intersection of every finite subcollection of $\{K_\alpha\}$ is nonempty. Then $\bigcap_{\alpha \in A} K_\alpha$ is nonempty.

> [!abstract] Corollary 2 (Nested Compact Sets)[^2]
> Let $(X, d)$ be a [[Metric Space]] and $\{K_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a [[Sequence]] of nonempty [[Compact Set|compact]] [[Subset|subsets]] of $X$ such that $K_n \supseteq K_{n+1}$ for every $n$. Then $\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty} K_n$ is nonempty.
