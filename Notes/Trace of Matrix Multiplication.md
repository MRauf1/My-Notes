# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Trace]] of [[Matrix Multiplication]])[^1]
> If $A \in M_{m, n}(\mathbb{F})$ and $B \in M_{n, m}(\mathbb{F})$, then $tr(AB) = tr(BA)$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Cyclic Property)[^1]
> If $A \in M_{m, n}(\mathbb{F})$, $B \in M_{n, p}(\mathbb{F})$, and $C \in M_{p, m}(\mathbb{F})$, then $tr(ABC) = tr(BCA) = tr(CAB)$.
