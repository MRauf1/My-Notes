# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Function]] Image)
> For a function $f: X \rightarrow Y$, its image is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> f(X) := \{f(x) | x \in X\} \subseteq Y
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!info] Definition 2 (Image of a Subset)[^2]
> For a function $f: X \rightarrow Y$ and a subset $E \subseteq X$, the image of $E$ under $f$ is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> f(E) := \{f(x) | x \in E\} \subseteq Y
> \end{align}
> $$
> Definition 1 is the special case $E = X$, so $f(X)$ is the range of $f$. If $f(X) = Y$, $f$ is said to map $X$ onto $Y$.

See [[Preimage]] for the corresponding notion of mapping subsets of $Y$ back into $X$.

> [!info] Definition 3 (Group Homomorphism Image)
> For a [[Group Homomorphism]] $\phi: G \rightarrow H$, its image is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \phi(G) := \{\phi(g) | g \in G\} \leq H
> \end{align}
> $$
