# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Nonlinear System of Equations)[^1]
> A system of [[Equation|equations]] in which at least one equation is not linear (e.g. $x^2 + y = 1$, $xy = 5$). A solution is a point that satisfies every equation at once, i.e. a point where the graphs intersect.

Unlike a [[Linear System of Equations]], a nonlinear system has no general solution algorithm. The tools are substitution and elimination, combined with whatever is needed to solve the resulting single-variable equations.

# Properties
- The solution count is not restricted to $0$, $1$ or infinitely many: e.g. a circle and a [[Conic Section]] can meet in $0$ to $4$ points.[^2]
- Squaring, substituting into a restricted [[Domain]] (e.g. $y = \sqrt{x+1}$), or dividing by a variable can create extraneous or lost solutions. Check candidates in the original system or against a graph.
- Systems of conics are often linear in $x^2$ and $y^2$: eliminating one of them by linear combination reduces the system to one variable.
