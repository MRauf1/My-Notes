# Definition
> [!info] Spacetime[^1]
> Einstein's unification of the three spatial dimensions and time into a single four-dimensional continuum, which becomes curved in the presence of mass-energy to represent [[Gravitation]].

# Properties
- Replaces the earlier picture of three-dimensional space and time as separate, independent structures within which classical mechanics operates.
- Under general relativity, gravitational attraction is a consequence of the curvature of spacetime rather than a [[Force]] acting instantaneously at a distance.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 2: Basic Physics](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_02.html)
