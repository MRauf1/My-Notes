# Definition
> [!info] Knowledge-Based Agent[^1]
> An agent design, specified by Kenneth Craik, comprising three steps: (1) a stimulus is translated into an internal representation, (2) the representation is manipulated by cognitive processes to derive new internal representations, and (3) these new representations are retranslated back into action.

# Properties
- Frames intelligent action as the manipulation of internal representations of the world, connecting [[Artificial Intelligence]] to theories of [[Cognitive Science|cognition]].
- Anticipates the [[Model-Based Reflex Agent|model-based reflex agent]], which maintains an internal state built from a [[Transition Model|transition model]] and [[Sensor Model|sensor model]] to reflect unobserved aspects of the world.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 31](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=31&annotation=LPTYJESM)
