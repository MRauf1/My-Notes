# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Divergence of Vector Field]] of [[Curl of Vector Field]])[^1]
> If $F = Pi + Qj + Rk$ is a [[Vector Field]] on $\mathbb{R}^3$ and $P, Q, R$ have [[Continuous Function]] second-order [[Partial Derivative]], then
> $$
> \begin{align}
> div(curl(F)) = 0
> \end{align}
> $$

[^1]: [Calculus: Early Transcendentals](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/EEFDQ9Y5?page=1138)
