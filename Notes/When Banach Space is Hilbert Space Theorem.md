# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (When [[Banach Space]] is [[Hilbert Space]] Theorem)
> [[Banach Space]] is a [[Hilbert Space]] (its [[Norm]] comes from an [[Inner Product]]) if and only if for every pair of [[Vector]] $x, y$, the [[Parallelogram Identity]] is true.

The [[Polarization Identity]] gives the explicit formula for the inner product that must be recovered from the norm.
