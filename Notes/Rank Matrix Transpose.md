# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Rank]] [[Matrix Transpose]])[^1]
> Let $A$ be a [[Matrix]]. Then the [[Row Rank]] of $A$ is the same as the [[Rank]] of $A$. In other words, $rank(A^T) = rank(A)$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Exercise 5.2.6)[^2]
> $rank(A^*) = rank(A)$, where $A^*$ is the [[Matrix Conjugate Transpose]] of $A$. This generalizes Theorem 1, since $A^* = A^T$ for real matrices.

> [!abstract] Theorem 3 (Exercise 5.2.7)[^2]
> $rank(AA^*) = rank(A^*A) = rank(A)$.
