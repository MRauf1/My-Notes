# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Rank]] [[Matrix Transpose]])[^1]
> Let $A$ be a [[Matrix]]. Then the [[Row Rank]] of $A$ is the same as the [[Rank]] of $A$. In other words, $rank(A^T) = rank(A)$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=194)
