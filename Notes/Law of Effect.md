# Definition
> [!info] Law of Effect[^1]
> (Thorndike) When a modifiable connection between a situation and a response is made and is accompanied or followed by a satisfying state of affairs, that connection's strength is increased; when followed by an annoying state of affairs, its strength is decreased. The strengthening (or weakening) effect varies with the closeness of the connection between the outcome and the bond.

# Properties
- In short: "the one impulse, out of many accidental ones, which leads to pleasure, becomes strengthened and stamped in"; the clearer the action-outcome connection, the stronger the change.[^2]
- Foundation of [[Operant Conditioning]] and of [[Reinforcement Learning]]; compare Turing's [[Pleasure-Pain System]] and the habit-forming [[Law of Reward]].
- Klopf extended it to single cells ([[Hedonistic Neuron]]).
