# Definition
> [!info] Moral Uncertainty[^1]
> Uncertainty about which moral framework or normative theory is correct, and the question of how to act well under it: "What is the right thing to do when you don't know the right thing to do?"

# Properties
- For MacAskill, moral uncertainty is not only descriptive but prescriptive: we need methods for deciding under it, and we ought to cultivate that very uncertainty rather than act with dogmatic confidence.[^2]
- Parallels the value uncertainty that supports [[Corrigibility]] in AI systems, and relates to [[Indirect Normativity]] and [[Normative Ethics]].
