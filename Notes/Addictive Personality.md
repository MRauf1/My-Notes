# Definition
> [!info] Addictive Personality[^1]
> The idea that some people are made prone to addiction by a particular personality type. It is largely based on the fact that many addicts are addicted to more than one thing.

# Properties
- **Carr's view.** It is "just a theory". The traits addicts share are the result of addiction, not its cause, and believing you have an addictive personality is self-fulfilling. "If you think you have an addictive personality, it's simply because you got addicted."[^2]
- **Evidence.** No single addictive personality has been identified, and drug use does change traits such as impulsivity, so Carr is partly right. But risk isn't equal across people:
	- Impulsivity, sensation seeking, negative emotionality, and low childhood self-control predict *later* substance dependence (e.g., Moffitt et al., 2011, Dunedin cohort).
	- Twin studies put the heritability of addictions at roughly 40–60%.
