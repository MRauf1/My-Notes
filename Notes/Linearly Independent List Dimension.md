# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linearly Independent]] [[Dimension]])[^1]
> Suppose that $V \subseteq \langle v_1, \dots, v_n \rangle$, and that $(w_1, \dots, w_n)$ is a [[Linearly Independent]] list in $V$. Then $m \geq n$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=183)
