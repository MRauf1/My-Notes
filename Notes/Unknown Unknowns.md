# Definition
> [!info] Unknown Unknowns[^1]
> A manifestation of [[Software Complexity]] in which it is not obvious which pieces of code must be modified, or what information a developer needs, in order to complete a task.

# Properties
- The worst of the three manifestations of complexity, since the developer does not even know what must be done, whereas [[Change Amplification]] and [[Cognitive Load (Software Design)]] are merely tedious.
- Reduced primarily by eliminating [[Obscurity (Software Design)]], its main cause.

[^1]: [A Philosophy of Software Design](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/283RR677?page=1)
