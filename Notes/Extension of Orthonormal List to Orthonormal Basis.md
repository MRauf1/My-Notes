# Definition

> [!abstract] Corollary 4.13 (Extension of Orthonormal List to Orthonormal Basis)[^1]
> Suppose $\mathcal{B} = (v_1, \dots, v_k)$ is an [[Orthonormal Vector|orthonormal]] list in a [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space|finite-dimensional]] [[Inner Product Space]] $V$. Then $\mathcal{B}$ can be extended to an [[Orthonormal Basis]] $\mathcal{B}'$ of $V$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=266)
