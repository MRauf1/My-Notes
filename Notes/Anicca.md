# Definition
> [!info] Anicca[^1]
> (Impermanence) The first of the [[Three Marks of Existence]]: all conditioned things are inherently transitory. Change is incessant, and perpetual alteration is the essence of the perceptual universe.

# Properties
- Nothing is wrong with change; it is the nature of the universe. Suffering comes from the odd responses culture has taught us to it ([[Three Unwholesome Roots|grasping, rejecting, ignoring]]).[^2]
- Humans view impermanent things as permanent. Even a small desire for permanence in an impermanent situation causes unhappiness ([[Dukkha]]).[^3]
- Fully seen, impermanence is no cause for grief. The response is wondrous joy at a universe that is "all moving, dancing and full of life".[^4]
