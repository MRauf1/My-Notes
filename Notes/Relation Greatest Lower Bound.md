# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Relation]] Greatest [[Relation Lower Bound]])[^1]
> Let $R$ be a [[Partial Order]] on $A$ and $B \subseteq A$. Let $L$ be the set of all [[Relation Lower Bound]] for $B$. If $L$ has a [[Relation Largest Element]], then this largest element is the greatest lower bound of $B$.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=211)
