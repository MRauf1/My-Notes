# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Computational [[Error]])[^1]
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \text{Computational error} = \hat{f}(\hat{x}) - f(\hat{x})
> \end{align}
> $$

[[Error]] resulting from the computations/algorithms. By definition, computational error is the sum of [[Truncation Error]] and [[Rounding Error]]; one of the two is usually the dominant factor in a given computation.[^2]

- [[Rounding Error]]
