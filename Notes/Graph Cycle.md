# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Graph Cycle]])[^1]
> [[Closed Graph Walk]] that has
> 1) At least 3 [[Vertex]] (to form a ring that can enclose a region)
> 2) No [[Vertex]] is used more than once except for the starting and ending vertices, which must be the same.

[^1]: [Building Blocks for Theoretical Computer Science](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/5IGT8C55?page=122)
