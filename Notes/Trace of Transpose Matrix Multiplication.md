# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Trace]] of [[Matrix Transpose]] Multiplication)[^1]
> If $A, B \in M_{m, n}(\mathbb{F})$, then $tr(A^T B) = tr(B^T A)$.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Real Case)[^1]
> If $A, B \in M_{m, n}(\mathbb{R})$, then $\langle A, B \rangle = tr(A^T B)$ defines the [[Frobenius Inner Product Space|Frobenius Inner Product]].
