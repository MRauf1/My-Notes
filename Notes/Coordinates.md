# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Coordinates)[^1]
> The coordinates of $v \in V$ with respect to a [[Basis]] $\mathcal{B}$ are the (unique ordered list of) scalars $a_1, \dots, a_n$ such that $v = a_1 v_1 + \dots + a_n v_n$.
> The coordinate representation $[v]_{\mathcal{B}} \in \mathbb{F}^n$ of $v$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$ is $[v]_{\mathcal{B}} = \begin{bmatrix}a_1 \\ \vdots \\ a_n\end{bmatrix}$.
