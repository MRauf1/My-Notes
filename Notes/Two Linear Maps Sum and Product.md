# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Two [[Linear Map]] Sum and Product)[^1]
> If $S, T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$ are [[Linear Map]] and $c \in \mathbb{F}$, then $S + T$, $cT$ are also [[Linear Map]] from $V$ to $W$.
> $\mathcal{L}(V, W)$ is itself a [[Vector Space]] over $\mathbb{F}$ with these operations.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=101)
