# Definition

Views [[Probability]] $P(A)$ where $A$ is an [[Event]] as an individual's degree of belief, which can be thought of as the amount the individual would be willing to wager that event $A$ will occur.[^1]

It extends probability to situations that cannot be repeated under identical conditions, where the [[Relative Frequency]] approach does not apply, by treating probability as a rational measure of belief.[^2]
