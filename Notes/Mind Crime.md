# Definition
> [!info] Mind Crime[^1]
> The moral catastrophe that could result when a machine superintelligence creates internal processes (e.g. detailed simulations of humans or other sentient minds) that have moral status, and then mistreats or destroys them.

# Properties
- Normally computation is considered morally significant only through its external effects, but simulations with high moral status could suffer and die on an enormous scale; routinely creating and deleting them could be equivalent to genocide.[^1]
- Especially hard to avoid when evolutionary methods are used to produce human-like intelligence, and a risk in institutions composed of emulated subagents.[^2]
- In [[Multipolar Scenario|multipolar]] emulation economies, digital workers might be spawned and erased daily ([[Whole Brain Emulation]]).
- Alongside [[Perverse Instantiation]] and [[Infrastructure Profusion]], one of Bostrom's malignant failure modes.
