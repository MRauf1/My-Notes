# Definition
> [!info] Anticipatory Anxiety[^1]
> A fear characterised by producing precisely that of which the patient is afraid: "the fear is mother of the event" (as "the wish is father to the thought"). E.g. fear of blushing or sweating in company brings on blushing or sweating.

# Properties
- It forms a vicious circle: a symptom provokes a phobia, the phobia triggers the symptom, and the symptom reinforces the phobia; in obsessive-compulsive cases, fighting the obsessions strengthens them, since "pressure precipitates counter pressure".[^2]
- It often generates [[Hyper-Intention|hyper-intention]] (e.g. fear of sleeplessness forces the intention to sleep, which prevents sleep).[^3]
- Treated by [[Paradoxical Intention]].[^4]
- A realistic fear such as fear of death cannot be tranquilised away by psychodynamic interpretation, while a neurotic fear such as agoraphobia cannot be cured by philosophical understanding.[^5]
