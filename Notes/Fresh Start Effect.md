# Definition
> [!info] Fresh Start Effect (Dai, Milkman and Riis)[^1]
> Temporal landmarks (the start of a week, month or year, birthdays, holidays) increase aspirational behavior such as gym visits, goal-related searches and commitment-contract sign-ups (Dai, Milkman and Riis, 2014). They open a new "mental accounting period" that distances the current self from past failures. "A fresh start feels motivating."

# Properties
- **New Year's resolutions.** In a prospective study, about 46% of resolvers were still succeeding at six months versus 4% of comparable non-resolvers who wanted to change (Norcross, Mrykalo and Blagys, 2002). Approach goals ("start X") succeeded more often than avoidance goals ("stop X") (Oscarsson et al., 2020).
- A fresh start helps *initiation*. Follow-through depends on the rest of the habit loop, e.g., a concrete [[Implementation Intention]].
- Related: [[Habit]], [[Law of Cue]].
