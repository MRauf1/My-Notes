# Definition
> [!info] Holism (Adlerian Psychology)[^1]
> The view of a person as "I as a whole": an indivisible being that cannot be broken down into parts such as mind and body, reason and emotion, or conscious and unconscious. The whole person chooses goals and uses emotions and thoughts as means ([[Teleology (Adlerian Psychology)]]). This is the origin of the name *individual* (Latin *individuus*, "indivisible") psychology.

# Properties
- **Harmony of life.** A way of living that lacks harmony sees only part of things and judges the whole, for example concluding from one attacker that everyone is bad. In every case it is "that person" who attacks you who has the problem.[^2]
- Basis of [[Adlerian Psychology]]. It contrasts with Freud's division into conflicting psychic agencies.
