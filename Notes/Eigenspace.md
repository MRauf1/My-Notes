# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Eigenspace)[^1]
> Let $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$ and suppose that $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}$ is an [[Eigenvalue]] of $T$. The $\lambda$-eigenspace of $T$ is the [[Vector Subspace]] $Eig_{\lambda}(T) \subset V$ defined by
> $$
> \begin{align}
> Eig_{\lambda}(T) := \{v \in V | T(v) = \lambda v\}
> \end{align}
> $$
> If $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}$ is the [[Eigenvalue]] of $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$, the $\lambda$-eigenspace of $A$ is the $\lambda$-eigenspace of the [[Linear Map]] given by $A$.
> In particular, $Eig_{\lambda}(T) = ker(T - \lambda I)$ and eigenspaces are [[Vector Subspace]] of $V$.

It is the [[Set]] of all [[Eigenvector]] and the zero [[Vector]].
