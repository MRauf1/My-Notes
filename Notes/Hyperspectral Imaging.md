# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Hyperspectral Imaging)[^1]
> Imaging that records light intensity at many distinct wavelengths, rather than the three wavelength bands effectively captured by the human eye's [[Trichromatic Theory of Color Vision|cone photoreceptors]].

# Properties
- Can distinguish some pairs of [[Metamerism|metameric]] spectra that appear identical to the human eye.
- In practice, adds only modest additional information over an ordinary trichromatic image for most natural images, since the three cone projections already capture much of the relevant variation.

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Color](https://visionbook.mit.edu/color.html)
