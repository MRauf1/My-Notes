# Definition

- $lim((1 + \frac{1}{n})^n) = e$[^1]
- $lim(\frac{1}{n^p}) = 0$ for $p > 0$[^2]
- $lim(a^n) = 0$ if $|a| < 1$[^2]
- $lim(n^{\frac{1}{n}}) = 1$[^2]
- $lim(a^{\frac{1}{n}}) = 1$ for $a > 0$[^2]
- $lim(\frac{n^{\alpha}}{(1+p)^n}) = 0$ for $p > 0$ and any real $\alpha$[^3]

[^1]: [Elementary Analysis: The Theory of Calculus](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/GUY2WR3V?page=49)
[^2]: [Elementary Analysis: The Theory of Calculus](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/GUY2WR3V?page=60)
[^3]: [Principles of Mathematical Analysis](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/3BD27IHF?page=66)
