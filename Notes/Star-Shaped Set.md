# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Star-Shaped [[Set]])[^1]
> [[Subset]] $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ is star-shaped with respect to [[Point]] $p \in S$ if for every $x \in S$, the [[Line Segment]] from $p$ to $x$ lies in S.

[^1]: [Introduction to Manifolds](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/4Y95WRJU?page=24)
