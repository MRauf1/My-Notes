# Definition
> [!info] Resonance (Rosa)[^1]
> Sociologist Hartmut Rosa's term for the capacity of daily life to touch, move, and absorb us. It arises from a reciprocal relationship with the world, including other people: you act, and then see how the world responds. The more we try to make the world controllable, the more it eludes us and the more resonance is lost.

# Properties
- Being delighted by a person or moved by a landscape or artwork requires not being in full control. Great achievements often depend on serendipity and unplanned opportunities.[^2]
- A good life still involves bold action, creating things, and making an impact, just without the hidden agenda of full control.[^2]
- Not knowing the future, not guaranteeing plans, never feeling on top of things: these are "mysteriously central to what makes life worth living". The uncertain stretches of life are often the ones remembered most fondly.[^3]
- "Almost everything that happens is either a good time or a good story."[^3]
