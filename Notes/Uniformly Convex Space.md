# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 (Uniformly Convex Space)
> A normed space is uniformly convex if it is [[Strictly Convex Space|strictly convex]] in a way that is uniform: for every $\epsilon > 0$, there is a $\delta > 0$ such that for any two unit vectors $v, w$ with $\lVert v - w \rVert \geq \epsilon$, $\left\lVert \tfrac{1}{2}(v+w) \right\rVert \leq 1 - \delta$.

Uniform convexity implies [[Strictly Convex Space|strict convexity]], but not conversely. If a normed space is complete and uniformly convex, it is reflexive, by the Milman–Pettis theorem.
