# Review[^1]

A good source for a starting CS student to learn about the basics of theoretical computer science. It covers a variety of fields, like discrete mathematics, graph theory, combinatorics, computability, and more. The one problem is the lack of exercises in the textbook.

[^1]: [Building Blocks for Theoretical Computer Science](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/5IGT8C55?page=1)
