# Review[^1]

His suggested proof writing method looks something that is similar to automatic prover systems like Lean.

Personally, while he does have some good points as to how this method would be better, I don't have much problem with the traditional method of proof-writing. Yes, there can be steps that jump that might be confusing and such, but I feel like my ability to proof-write and think about proofs has also improved through me trying to decipher some of these confusing steps within proofs of others. Traditional proofs are also shorter than the structured proofs.

Overall, he presents some good ideas and cases for the structured proofs.

[^1]: [How to Write a Proof](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/P7KZECMR?page=1)
