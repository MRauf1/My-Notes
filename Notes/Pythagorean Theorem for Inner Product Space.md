# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Pythagorean Theorem]] for [[Inner Product Space]])[^1]
> If $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ is an [[Orthogonal Vector]] list, then
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \lVert v_1 + \dots + v_n \rVert^2 = \lVert v_1 \rVert^2 + \dots + \lVert v_n \rVert^2
> \end{align}
> $$

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=250)
