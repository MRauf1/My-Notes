# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Median)
> The median of a sorted dataset of $n$ datapoints is the middle value. When $n$ is odd, it is the singular middle value; when $n$ is even, it is the [[Mean|arithmetic mean]] of the two middle values.[^1]

> [!info] Definition 2 (Median using [[Quantile Function]])
> Median is $Q(0.5)$.

For a [[Random Variable]], the median is the quantile $\xi_{1/2}$, also called the second quartile; it is a measure of the center of the distribution, paired with the [[Interquartile Range]] as a measure of spread.[^2]

The sample median of a [[Random Sample]] is $Q_2 = Y_{(n+1)/2}$ for $n$ odd and $(Y_{n/2} + Y_{n/2+1})/2$ for $n$ even, in terms of the [[Order Statistic|order statistics]]; a [[Distribution-Free Confidence Interval for Quantile|distribution-free confidence interval]] for the median uses a pair $(Y_i, Y_j)$.[^3]
