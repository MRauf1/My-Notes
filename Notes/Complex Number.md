# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Complex Number)[^1]
> [[Number|Number]] of the form $a + bi$, where $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$, and $i$ is the [[Imaginary Unit|imaginary unit]].

[[Multiplication]] of $z_2$ by $z_1$ rotates $z_2$ by $arg(z_1)$ and scales it by $|z_1|$.

# Properties
- $\mathbb{R} \subset \mathbb{C}$
- [[Absolute Value of Complex Number]]
- [[Argument (Complex Number)]]
- **Product**: $(a + bi)(c + di) = (ac - bd) + (ad + bc)i$; the minus sign comes from $i^2 = -1$.[^sigworth]
- **Real and imaginary parts**: $\operatorname{Re}(a + bi) = a$, $\operatorname{Im}(a + bi) = b$; the absolute value is $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$.
- Exponentials extend to complex arguments by their power series ([[Complex Exponential Function]]).
