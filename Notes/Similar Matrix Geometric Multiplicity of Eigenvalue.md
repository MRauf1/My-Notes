# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Similar Matrix]] [[Geometric Multiplicity of Eigenvalue]])[^1]
> If $A, B$ are [[Similar Matrix]], then for each [[Eigenvalue]] $\lambda_i \in \mathbb{F}$, the [[Geometric Multiplicity of Eigenvalue]] $m_{\lambda_i}(A) = m_{\lambda_i}(B)$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=234)
