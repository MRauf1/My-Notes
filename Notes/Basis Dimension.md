# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Basis]] [[Dimension]])[^1]
> Suppose that $dim(V) = n \neq 0$ and that $V = \langle \mathcal{B} \rangle$, where $\mathcal{B} = (v_1, \dots, v_n)$. Then $\mathcal{B}$ is a [[Basis]] for $V$.

If the dimension of a list [[Span|spanning]] the [[Vector Space]] is the same as the dimension of the vector space, then the [[Linearly Independent|linear independence]] comes for free.
