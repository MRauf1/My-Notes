# Definition
> [!info] Nonreaction (Tolle)[^1]
> Not reacting to the ego in others, which is possible only once you recognize their behavior as coming from ego. It is one of the most effective ways of going beyond ego in yourself and of dissolving the collective human ego. "Non reaction is not weakness but strength. Another word for nonreaction is forgiveness."

# Properties
- **Allowing diminishment of the ego.** When criticized, blamed, or insulted, do nothing: neither retaliate nor defend, inwardly or outwardly. Feel what the diminished self-image is like. After brief discomfort, an intensely alive inner spaciousness can appear. Through becoming "less" in the ego's eyes, you expand. Tolle reads Jesus' "Deny yourself" and "Turn the other cheek" this way.[^2]
- Related practices: take the lowest seat; refrain from showing off, standing out, or demanding attention; occasionally don't voice your opinion when everyone else does.[^3]
- **Grievances.** Don't try to let go or force forgiveness. Be aware of the thoughts that keep the grievance alive and feel the emotion the body produces in response. Forgiveness happens naturally once you see that the grievance only props up the ego: "The seeing is freeing." The past has no power to stop you being present now.[^4]
