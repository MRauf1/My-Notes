# Definition
> [!info] Existential Vacuum[^1]
> ([[Viktor Frankl|Frankl]]) The common first stage of a meaninglessness syndrome, also called "existential frustration": a subjective state of boredom, apathy, emptiness, cynicism, and lack of direction, often surfacing in free time ("Sunday neurosis"), which shows there is nothing one wants to do. If overt neurotic symptoms fill the vacuum (a psychological *horror vacui*), it becomes an **existential (noogenic) neurosis**.

# Types
Maddi's forms of existential sickness:[^2]
- **Crusadism** - compulsively dedicating oneself to dramatic causes regardless of content, to stay ahead of meaninglessness.
- **Nihilism** - an active proclivity to discredit others' meanings, from despair.
- **Vegetativeness** - the extreme: aimlessness, apathy, blandness, and indiscriminate behaviour.

# Properties
- Modern dilemma: instinct no longer tells us what we must do, nor tradition what we should do, and we do not know what we want; common reactions are conformity and totalitarianism.[^3]
- Pre-industrial life supplied meaning through religion, survival tasks, creative work close to nature, family and community roles, and intrinsically worthwhile work; secular, urban life with much leisure and meaningless work invites crises of meaning, since free time thrusts freedom upon us.[^4]
- Treated by [[Engagement (Existential Psychotherapy)|engagement]] and [[Dereflection]]; see [[Logotherapy]].
