# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Dimension]] [[Algorithm]])[^1]
> Let $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ be a list of [[Vector]] in $\mathbb{F}^m$. To find the [[Dimension]] of $\langle v_1, \dots, v_n \rangle$:
> 1) Put the [[Matrix]] $A$ (with these [[Vector]] as columns of $A$) into [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]].
> 2) The [[Dimension]] is the number of [[Pivot Variable]].
