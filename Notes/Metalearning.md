# Definition

The study of learning to learn. The [[Hypothesis Space|hypothesis space]] is the [[Set|set]] of all learning [[Algorithm|algorithms]].[^1]

# Properties
- Handles the case where the future problem to be solved is itself a learning problem, such as "find the least-squares line fit to these data points"; one way to train for this is by example.
- Under the broad view of [[Machine Learning|learning]] as an algorithm that searches for well-performing algorithms, biological [[Evolution]] itself qualifies as a learning algorithm.

[^1]: https://visionbook.mit.edu/intro_to_learning.html
