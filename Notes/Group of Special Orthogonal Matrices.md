# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Group]] of [[Set of Special Orthogonal Matrices]])
> $(SO(n), \cdot)$ is a [[Group]].

# Properties
- Together with translations, it forms the [[Special Euclidean Group]] $SE(n) \cong \mathbb{R}^n \rtimes SO(n)$ of rigid motions.
