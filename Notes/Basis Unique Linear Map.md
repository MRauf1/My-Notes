# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Basis]] Unique [[Linear Map]])[^1]
> Suppose that $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ is a [[Basis]] for $V$, and let $w_1, \dots, w_n \in W$ be any [[Vector]]. Then there is a unique [[Linear Map]] $T: V \rightarrow W$ such that $T(v_i) = w_i$ for each $i$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=175)
