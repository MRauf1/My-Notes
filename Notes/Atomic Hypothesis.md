# Definition
> [!info] Atomic Hypothesis[^1]
> All matter is composed of atoms: small particles in perpetual motion, attracting one another when separated by a small distance, but repelling one another when squeezed together.

# Properties
- Serves as the microscopic foundation for the [[Kinetic Theory of Heat]] and the [[Kinetic Theory of Pressure]].
- Extends beyond physics: the behavior of living matter is presumed to be fully governed by the same atomic laws, without exception.
- Macroscopic states that appear static (e.g. [[Dynamic Equilibrium]]) can conceal continuous underlying atomic motion.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 1: Atoms in Motion](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_01.html)
