# Definition
> [!info] Belief[^1]
> A concrete physical structure within the brain representing an agent's onboard map of the world, analogous to how paper maps represent the world with ink but instead using connections between neurons.

# Properties
- Since beliefs cannot be directly observed, they are known, like any other unobservable, through their effects on observable behavior; in particular, an agent's [[Credence|degree of belief]] can be inferred from their betting behavior.
- An agent stores many such maps rather than a single one, since they are not certain of everything; each map is assigned a [[Credence|credence]] representing how certain the agent is of its accuracy.

[^1]: [A Critical Introduction to Formal Epistemology](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9XYCZDPF?page=10)
