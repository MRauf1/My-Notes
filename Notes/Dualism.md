# Definition
> [!info] Dualism[^1]
> The view that there is a part of the human mind (or soul/spirit) that is outside of nature, exempt from physical laws.

# Types
- [[Substance Dualism]]
- [[Property Dualism]]

# Properties
- Contrasted with [[Materialism]], which holds that the mind is constituted entirely by the brain's operation according to the laws of physics.

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 24](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=24&annotation=D5G9ALN6)
