# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Graph]] [[Isomorphism]])[^1]
> Let $G_1 = (V_1, E_1)$ and $G_2 = (V_2, E_2)$. An [[Isomorphism]] between $G_1, G_2$ is a [[Bijective Function]] $f: V_1 \rightarrow V_2$ such that [[Vertex]] $a, b$ are joined by an [[Edge]] if and only if $f(a), f(b)$ are joined by an [[Edge]].

# Properties
## Preserved Under [[Isomorphism]]
- Number of [[Vertex]]
- Number of [[Edge]]
- The [[Set]] of [[Degree of Vertex]] of every [[Vertex]]
- Any [[Subgraph]] of $G_1$ must have a matching [[Subgraph]] of $G_2$
