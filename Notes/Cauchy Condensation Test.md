# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Cauchy Condensation Test)[^1]
> Let $a_1 \geq a_2 \geq a_3 \geq \dots \geq 0$ be a non-increasing sequence of non-negative real numbers. Then the [[Infinite Series]] $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ converges if and only if the "condensed" series $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} 2^k a_{2^k}$ converges.

# Properties
- Used to establish the convergence/divergence of [[p-Series|p-series]].

[^1]: [Principles of Mathematical Analysis](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/3BD27IHF?page=71)
