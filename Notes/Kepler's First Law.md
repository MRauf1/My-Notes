# Definition
> [!info] Kepler's First Law[^1]
> Each planet moves around the sun in an [[Ellipse]], with the sun at one focus.

# Properties
- A special case of the general fact, derivable from [[Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation]], that a bound orbit under any inverse-square central force is a [[Conic Section]]; a closed planetary orbit takes the elliptical form.
- One of three [[Kepler's Second Law|Kepler's]] [[Kepler's Third Law|laws]] of planetary motion, discovered empirically before being derived from [[Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation]].

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 7: The Theory of Gravitation](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_07.html)
