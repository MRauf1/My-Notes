# Definition
> [!info] Generativity[^1]
> (Erikson) The adult life-cycle stage, typically in one's forties and fifties, defined as "the concern in establishing and guiding the next generation", in which meaning is found in self-transcendent ventures: care for one's progeny or, more broadly, for the species.

# Properties
- Follows earlier self-centred tasks of adolescence and early adulthood (identity, intimacy, mastery), unless an earlier task was not negotiated; corroborated by 1970s adult life-cycle research.[^1]
- Shows that meaning must be viewed developmentally ([[Self-Transcendence]], [[Cosmic and Terrestrial Meaning]]).
