# Definition
> [!info] Boundary of Simplicial Complex[^1]
> For a pure $k$-subcomplex $K' \subseteq K$ of an [[Abstract Simplicial Complex]], the boundary $\operatorname{bd}(K')$ is the [[Simplicial Closure|closure]] of the set of all $(k-1)$-simplices that are faces of exactly one $k$-simplex of $K'$. The interior is everything else:[^2]
> $$\begin{align}
> \operatorname{int}(K') = K' \setminus \operatorname{bd}(K').
> \end{align}$$

Crane phrases the condition as "proper faces of exactly one simplex of $K'$"; the precise (standard) form restricts to codimension-1 faces of the top-dimensional simplices, as above.

# Properties
- Combinatorial analogue of the topological [[Boundary Set]] and [[Interior Set]].
- For any [[Simplicial Surface]], $\operatorname{bd}(K)$ is a collection of zero or more closed loops of edges.[^3]
