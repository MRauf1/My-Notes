# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Associative Laws)[^1]
> For [[Proposition|statements]] $P, Q, R$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> P \land (Q \land R) &\equiv (P \land Q) \land R \\
> P \lor (Q \lor R) &\equiv (P \lor Q) \lor R
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- Because of these, $P \land Q \land R$ and $P \lor Q \lor R$ can be written without parentheses ([[Logical Equivalence]]).
