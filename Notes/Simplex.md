# Definition

$(K - 1)$ simplex, denoted $\Delta^{K-1}$, is the [[Set|sSet]] of all $K$-[[Dimension|dimensional]] [[Vector|vectors]] whose elements [[Addition|sum]] to $1$.[^1]

# Properties
- A [[Probability Mass Function|pmf]] over $K$ classes, viewed as a $K$-dimensional vector with elements in $[0,1]$ summing to $1$, is a point on $\Delta^{K-1}$.
- $K$-dimensional [[One-Hot Encoding|one-hot codes]] live on the vertices of $\Delta^{K-1}$.

[^1]: https://visionbook.mit.edu/intro_to_learning.html
