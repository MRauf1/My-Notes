# Definition
> [!info] Superstitious Behavior[^1]
> Behaviour that is strengthened by reinforcement that is in fact independent of it: the organism comes to act as if its behaviour caused the reward (Skinner, 1948).

# Properties
- **Humans.** Students told to earn points from a panel of levers, with points actually delivered at random, developed idiosyncratic, often elaborate response patterns and believed they had discovered the rule (Ono, 1987).[^1]
- The brain is a "meaning machine" that generates meaning from associations between experiences. It is imperfect, and once it has created a meaning it holds on to it and discounts contradicting evidence ([[Confirmation Bias]]).[^2]
- Closely related to the *illusion of control* (Langer, 1975) and to [[Operant Conditioning]].
