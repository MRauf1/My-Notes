# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Eigenvalue]] [[Kernel]])[^1]
> Let $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$ and $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}$. Then $\lambda$ is an [[Eigenvalue]] of $T$ if and only if $ker(T - \lambda I) \neq \{0\}$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=142)
