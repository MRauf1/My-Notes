# Definition
> [!info] Productivity Debt (Burkeman)[^1]
> The feeling of starting each day in a hole you must climb out of through work, so as to justify your existence as a minimally acceptable member of society. It is typical of "insecure overachievers", whose accomplishments are ultimately driven by feelings of inadequacy.

# Properties
- "One never notices what has been done; one can see only what remains to be done." (Marie Curie)[^2]
- "We produce against the feeling of lack." (Byung-Chul Han)[^3]
- **Origins**: children raised to feel valued only when excelling, and consumerism, which profits from keeping people feeling inadequate.[^4]
- It turns success into punishment: each accomplishment raises the standard, so the debt grows harder to pay off.[^5]
- **Way out.** Religiously, through grace. For agnostics and atheists, if there is no god, no authority can demand that you earn the right to exist: "You just do exist, and that has to be sufficient." We already belong to the world.[^6]
- **Done list.** Record the tasks you have completed today, not the ones planned. It compares your output against doing nothing at all instead of against everything you could do. It motivates, improves choices about focus, and wastes less energy stressing over neglected tasks.[^7]
- Related: [[Imperfectionism]], [[Self-Esteem]], [[Perfectionism]].
