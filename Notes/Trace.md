# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Trace]])[^1]
> Let $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$. The trace of $A$ is $tr(A) = \sum_{i=1}^n a_{ii}$.

It is the sum of the diagonal entries.

> [!info] Definition 2 (For [[Linear Map]])
> Let $V$ be a [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space]] and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. The trace of $T$ is $tr(T) = tr([T]_{\mathcal{B}})$, where $\mathcal{B}$ is any [[Basis]] of $V$.
