# Definition
> [!definition] Subnet[^1]
> A subnet is a connected group of hosts, usually on the same physical network, whose [[IP Address|IP addresses]] share a common prefix defined by a subnet mask.

# Properties
- The Internet's overall topology is decentralized, made up of many interconnected subnets.[^2]
- A host communicates directly only with other hosts on its own local subnet; to reach hosts elsewhere on the Internet, it must go through a [[Router|router]].[^3]
