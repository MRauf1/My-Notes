# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 ($L^1$ Norm)[^1]
> The $L^1$ norm on the space $C([0,1])$ is defined by
> $$
> \lVert f \rVert_1 := \int_0^1 |f(x)| \, dx
> $$

This is the functional analog of the [[1-Norm]] on $\mathbb{R}^n$/$\mathbb{C}^n$.
