# Definition
> [!info] Author and Committer[^1]
> Each commit in [[Git]] records two roles: the author, who originally wrote the work, and the committer, who last applied it to the repository.

# Properties
- When a maintainer applies a patch submitted by someone else, both people are credited on the resulting commit: the submitter as author, and the maintainer as committer.

[^1]: [Pro Git](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/Y53JUJJW?page=49&annotation=XYMIATTY)
