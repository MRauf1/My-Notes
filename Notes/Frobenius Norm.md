# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Frobenius [[Vector Norm]])[^1]
> [[Vector Norm]] in [[Frobenius Inner Product Space]] is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \lVert A \rVert_F = \sqrt{tr(A A^*)}
> \end{align}
> $$
