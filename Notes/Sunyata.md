# Definition
> [!info] Sunyata[^1]
> (Emptiness) The Buddhist teaching, especially in [[Mahayana Buddhism]], that the essence of all things is emptiness. Its best-known statement is the Heart Sutra's "Form is emptiness, emptiness is form."

# Properties
- Tolle reads it as the Unmanifested appearing in the world as space: every object comes from "no-thing", is surrounded by it, and returns to it (see [[The Unmanifested]]).[^1]
- Related: [[Anatta]].
