In differential geometry, the Jacobian is the coordinate representation of the differential/pushforward $df$ ([[Tangent Map]]); for a [[Smooth Surface]] the [[Induced Metric]] is $\mathrm{I} = J^TJ$.

In deep learning's [[Backpropagation]], one can think of a Jacobian as a locally linear approximation to the loss surface.[^1]
