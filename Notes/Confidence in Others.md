# Definition
> [!info] Confidence in Others (Adlerian Psychology)[^1]
> Believing in others **unconditionally**, without requiring security or guarantees. It is the basis of interpersonal relations in [[Adlerian Psychology]]. It contrasts with **trust**, which is conditional on collateral or evidence ("I'll lend it if he won't take advantage of me"). It is the second of the three ingredients of [[Community Feeling]] and is essential to the switch from attachment to self to concern for others.

# Properties
- **Doubt is detected.** Others sense doubt instantly, and no positive relationship can grow from it. Looking with doubt makes everything look like evidence of betrayal, even when there is none.[^2]
- **Being taken advantage of is their task.** Whether the other person exploits one's confidence is their task ([[Separation of Tasks]]). One only asks "What should I do?"[^3]
- Unconditional confidence is a means of improving a relationship and building a horizontal one ([[Vertical and Horizontal Relationships]]).[^4]
- Confidence gives the courage to enter deeper relationships, which increases the joy of relationships and of life.[^5]
- "We can believe. And we can doubt. But we are aspiring to see others as our comrades."[^6]
- Corresponds to the objectives "to live in harmony with society" and "people are my comrades".[^7]
- Note: confidence does not mean tolerating attacks. When someone attacks, it is that person who has the problem ([[Holism (Adlerian Psychology)|harmony of life]]).
- **Respect comes first.** One cannot have confidence in someone one does not respect ([[Respect (Adlerian Psychology)]]). Both are products of one's own resolve, whoever the other person is.[^kk1]
- **Believing is not credulity.** One is skeptical of a person's statements and thinks for oneself, yet believes in the whole person, even one who lies. Since no one can fully know another, believing in them "as an unknowable being" is the only way.[^kk2]
- **Believe first.** If you want someone to believe in you, believe in them first, even if they try not to believe in you.[^kk3]
- **"Love thy neighbour as thyself."** If one cannot believe in or love oneself, one cannot believe in or love others. Self-centredness does not come from liking oneself. It comes from not accepting oneself and being beset by anxiety ([[Self-Acceptance]]).[^kk4]
