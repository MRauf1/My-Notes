# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Markov's [[Inequality]])[^1]
> Let $u(X)$ be a non-negative [[Function]] of [[Random Variable]] $X$. If $E[u(X)]$ exists, then for every positive constant c
> $$
> \begin{align}
> P(u(X) \geq c) \leq \frac{E[u(X)]}{c}
> \end{align}
> $$
