# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Rank-Nullity Theorem]] [[Invertible Linear Map]] Equal [[Dimension]])[^1]
> Suppose that $dim(V) = dim(W)$ and that $S \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$ and $T \in \mathcal{L}(W, V)$. If $ST = I$, then $TS = I$ as well. In particular, $S$ and $T$ are [[Invertible Linear Map]] and $T^{-1} = S$.
> Suppose that $A, B \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$. If $AB = I_n$, then $BA = I_n$ as well. In particular, $A$ and $B$ are [[Invertible Linear Map]] and $A^{-1} = B$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=199)
