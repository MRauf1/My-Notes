# Definition
> [!info] Empiricism[^1]
> The view, per John Locke's dictum that "nothing is in the understanding, which was not first in the senses," that all knowledge derives from sensory experience.

# Properties
- Originates with Francis Bacon's *Novum Organum*.
- Motivates the [[Principle of Induction (Epistemology)|principle of induction]], and, combined with rationalism, [[Logical Positivism|logical positivism]].

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 24](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=24&annotation=82YEWHPI)
