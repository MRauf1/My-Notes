# Definition
> [!info] Minimal Surface[^1]
> A surface with zero [[Mean Curvature]] everywhere, $H \equiv 0$.

The name comes from area: minimal surfaces are exactly the critical points of the [[Surface Area]] functional under variations with fixed boundary (soap films). Strictly they are *stationary*, not always area-minimizing.[^1]

# Properties
- Principal curvatures have equal magnitude and opposite sign ($\kappa_1 = -\kappa_2$), so minimal surfaces tend to be saddle-like and have $K = -\kappa_1^2 \le 0$ ([[Gaussian Curvature]]).[^1]
- In [[Conformal Map|conformal coordinates]], $f$ is minimal iff its coordinate functions are harmonic ([[Harmonic Function]]), since $\Delta f = 2He^{u}N$ when the [[Induced Metric]] is $e^u\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ (O'Neill's sign convention for $H$).
