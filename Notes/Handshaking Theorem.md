# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Handshaking Theorem)[^1]
> Each [[Edge]] contributes to two [[Degree of Vertex]]. Thus, it follows that
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \sum_{v \in V} deg(v) = 2 |E|
> \end{align}
> $$
