# Definition
> [!info] Phase Velocity[^1]
> The speed at which a point of constant [[Phase (Waves)|phase]] (e.g. a crest) of a [[Sinusoidal Wave]] moves:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> v_p = \frac{\omega}{k} = \lambda f,
> \end{align}
> $$
> obtained by setting $kx - \omega t = \text{const}$, so $dx/dt = \omega/k$. Its direction is the direction of propagation ([[Wave Vector]]).

# Properties
- **Group velocity** $v_g = d\omega/dk$ is the speed of a wave packet's envelope, i.e. of energy and information. It equals $v_p$ only for a non-dispersive medium, where $\omega/k$ is constant, as for the [[Wave Equation]] with $\omega = c|k|$.
- For matter waves, $v_p$ can exceed the speed of light, while $v_g$ equals the particle's velocity.
