# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Theorem 6.11)[^1]
> If $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$, then $\det(A^T) = \det(A)$.

Since every row of $A$ is a column of $A^T$, this implies that the [[Determinant]] is also an [[Alternating Multilinear Function|alternating multilinear function]] of the rows of a matrix, not just its columns.[^2]
