# Definition
> [!info] Being (Tolle)[^1]
> The eternal, ever-present "One Life" beyond the countless forms of life that are born and die. It is also present deep within every form as that form's invisible, indestructible essence. It is therefore accessible now as one's own deepest self, felt as the sense of presence: "I am", which comes before "I am this" or "I am that".

# Properties
- It cannot be grasped or understood by the [[Mind Identification|mind]]. It can only be felt when the mind is still and attention is fully in the Now. It cannot become an object of knowledge, because in Being subject and object merge.[^2]
- Tolle prefers the word "Being" to "God". God has become a *closed* concept that immediately calls up a mental image; Being is an *open* concept. No word can define the reality behind it. The only question is whether a word helps or hinders the experience it points to.[^3]
- Losing the felt connection with Being creates the illusion of separation from oneself and the world. One then experiences oneself as an isolated fragment, and fear and conflict follow.[^4]
- Being, consciousness, and life are synonymous for Tolle. When Being becomes conscious of itself, that is [[Presence (Spirituality)|presence]].[^5]
- There is "infinitely more intelligence in Being than in your mind".[^6]
- Love, joy, and peace are aspects of connectedness with Being (see [[States of Being]]).
- Access points to it are described in [[The Unmanifested]]. Regaining awareness of Being and abiding in it is [[Enlightenment (Spirituality)|enlightenment]].
- *A New Earth*: Tolle claims consciousness is timeless, never born and never dying, and only *appears* to evolve when it becomes the manifested universe. He also claims "the brain does not create consciousness, but consciousness created the brain". This contradicts the mainstream neuroscientific view that consciousness depends on brain activity, and he offers no evidence for it.[^7]
- Each life form is "a unique way in which the universe experiences itself"; when it dissolves, "a world comes to an end".[^8]
