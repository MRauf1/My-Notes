# Definition
> [!info] Converse Dutch Book Theorem (Kemeny, 1955; Lehman, 1955)[^1]
> If an agent's betting prices satisfy the rules of [[Probabilism|probability]], then there does not exist a Dutch Book consisting of bets at those prices.

# Properties
- Converse of the [[Dutch Book Theorem]]: probabilistic agents are never susceptible to a sure loss.
- Defends premise P3 of the [[Dutch Book Argument]] against the objection that all agents are susceptible to a sure loss, which would make such susceptibility unavoidable and hence not irrational.

[^1]: [A Critical Introduction to Formal Epistemology](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9XYCZDPF?page=43)
