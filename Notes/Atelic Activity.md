# Definition
> [!info] Atelic Activity (Setiya)[^1]
> An activity done for its own sake, with no endpoint at which it is completed (going for a walk, listening to music), as opposed to *telic* activities aimed at completion (projects, goals). Telic activities are self-undermining. Either you haven't achieved the goal yet, so you're dissatisfied for lack of it, or you have, so you're dissatisfied because it's no longer there to strive towards.

# Properties
- **Schopenhauer.** Having "objects of willing" is inherently painful: not having them is bad, and getting them is arguably worse.[^2]
- **Midlife crisis.** When your relationship with time is almost entirely instrumental, the present loses meaning. Midlife is when mortality makes the absurdity of living solely for the future impossible to ignore.[^3]
- **Hobbies you'll never excel at** let you set aside, for a while, the anxious need to "use time well".[^4]
