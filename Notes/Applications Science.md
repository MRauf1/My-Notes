# Definition
> [!info] Applications Science[^1]
> A science of (environmental) applications, distinguished from basic Earth and environmental science by being oriented to societal needs and decisions: "we seek solutions, not just scientific papers".

# Properties
- Core characteristics:[^2]
	- *Need driven versus curiosity driven* - guided by societal needs; rather than answering questions, it builds the ability to seek courses of action and determine their consequences.
	- *Externally constrained* - must inform actions on the schedule of external drivers, not academic schedules.
	- *Consequential and recursive* - actions based on knowledge of the Earth change the Earth, requiring new knowledge ([[Feedback Loop (Machine Learning)|cf. model feedback loops]]).
	- *Useful even when incomplete* - actions are often needed despite partial knowledge.
	- *Scalable*, *robust*, and *data intensive*.
- Each characteristic requires new knowledge types and tools; it is integrative, coupling physical understanding with research on how people and organisations use knowledge to decide, including social science data and near-real-time cyberinfrastructure.[^3]
- Model-data coupling: predictive models of complex systems (e.g. oceans) are progressively refined by continual comparison with real-time, multi-scale sensor data; disagreements trigger adaptive sensing of unfolding events, and coherently indexed archives let many investigators explore parameter space.[^4]
- Sensor networks in harsh environments must be energy-efficient and robust, flag invalid data, push computation near data sources, compress data (ideally computing on compressed data), and record metadata and provenance; coarse model grids cause severe errors in heterogeneous terrain.[^5]
- The academic reward system (disciplinary, publication- and tenure-driven) misaligns the supply of knowledge with societal demand, hampering timely responses to e.g. climate change; new organisations should integrate natural and socioeconomic science, systems thinking, and knowledge creation with use.[^6]
- An application of [[Data-Intensive Science]]; the public can contribute via [[Citizen Science]].
