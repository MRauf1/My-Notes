# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Bipartite [[Graph]])[^1]
> [[Graph]] $G = (V, E)$ is a bipartite graph if we can split $V$ into 2 non-overlapping [[Subset]] $V_1, V_2$ such that every [[Edge]] in $G$ connects a [[Vertex]] of $V_1$ to a [[Vertex]] of $V_2$. No [[Edge]] connects two [[Vertex]] from the same part of division.
