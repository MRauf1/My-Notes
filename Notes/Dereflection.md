# Definition
> [!info] Dereflection[^1]
> ([[Viktor Frankl|Frankl]], [[Logotherapy]]) A technique of diverting patients' gaze away from themselves, their dysphoria, and the sources of their neuroses, toward the intact parts of their personalities and the meanings available to them in the world; since "happiness cannot be pursued, it can only ensue".

# Properties
- For excessively self-absorbed patients, a long inward search for causes compounds the problem; Frankl would present anxiety as a fixed baseline and redirect work toward attitude and meaning.[^2]
- Yalom finds the technique simplistic and its appeal to authority ("we doctors will pilot you") undermining of responsibility, but endorses helping the patient develop curiosity and concern for others.[^3]
- Rests on [[Self-Transcendence]].
