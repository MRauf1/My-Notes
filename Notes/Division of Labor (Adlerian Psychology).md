# Definition
> [!info] Division of Labor (Adlerian Psychology)[^1]
> Adler's final conclusion about why humans form society. The division of labor is the survival strategy humans developed to make up for their physical inferiority. We work to survive and build society to divide labor, so living, working and building society are inseparable. Whatever one's morals, one has no choice but to work, divide labor and build relationships. Humans cannot live alone at the level of survival, not merely at the level of loneliness.

# Properties
- **Tasks of work are relationships.** The [[Life Tasks]] concern the relationships attached to work, friendship and love, not the labor itself.[^2]
- **Work relationships run on trust**, which is conditional. Friendships run on [[Confidence in Others|confidence]], which is unconditional. Dividing labor requires believing in others.[^3]
- **All professions are honorable.** A person's worth lies in how they fulfil their role in the community's division of labor, not in what the work is.[^4]
- **Ability matters in work.** Within the division of labor, each person's ability counts, which is why belonging sought only through work success stays conditional. See [[Community Feeling]].[^5]
- Rooted in the [[Feeling of Inferiority]] of the human species. Related: [[All Problems Are Interpersonal Relationship Problems]].
