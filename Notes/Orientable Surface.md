# Definition
> [!info] Orientable Surface[^1]
> A surface $M$ is orientable if one can pick the direction of the unit [[Surface Normal]] $N$ (either $+N$ or $-N$) consistently, i.e. continuously over the whole surface.

The circular band (cylinder strip) is orientable; the Möbius band is not: transporting $N$ once around its core returns $-N$.[^1]

# Properties
- For orientable surfaces, $N$ becomes a continuous map $N: M \rightarrow S^2$, the [[Gauss Map]].
- The analogous combinatorial notion: [[Oriented Simplicial Complex]].
