# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Circle of Confusion)[^1]
> The blurred disc formed on a camera's sensor plane by light from a point that is not in sharp focus. A point lying on the plane conjugate to the sensor plane under the [[Lensmaker's Equation]] is imaged sharply, while a point elsewhere on the focal plane is instead imaged as a circle of confusion.

# Properties
- Its diameter grows as the imaged point moves farther from the plane of focus, and shrinks as the [[Aperture]] narrows.
- Whether the circle of confusion at a given point is smaller than some tolerance determines whether that point lies within the camera's [[Depth of Field]].

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Lenses](https://visionbook.mit.edu/lenses.html)
