# Definition
> [!info] Pain-Body (Tolle)[^1]
> The accumulated residue of past emotional pain, lodged in mind and body. Each emotional pain that is not met with presence leaves such a residue and merges with earlier pain. Tolle describes it as a semi-autonomous energy field of "trapped life-energy" that has split off through mind identification and turned "anti-life". It has two modes: dormant and active.

# Properties
- **Two levels of pain**: pain created now, which is always some form of nonacceptance or resistance to what is ([[Surrender (Spirituality)]]), and pain from the past that still lives on (the pain-body).[^2]
- Anything can trigger it, especially something that resonates with a past pain pattern. Watch for any sign of unhappiness, since it may be the pain-body awakening.[^3]
- It survives only through unconscious identification and through your unconscious fear of facing the pain. It feeds only on pain (anger, drama, negative thinking): once it takes over, you want to inflict pain, suffer it, or both. Dwelling on what someone did to you means it has "become you".[^4]
- **Transmutation.** You cannot fight it, because fighting creates more inner conflict. Observe it, feel its energy, take your attention into it, accept it, and don't let the feeling turn into thinking or into an identity. Sustained attention cuts its link to thought, and the pain becomes "fuel for the flame of your consciousness". Tolle calls this the esoteric meaning of alchemy.[^5]
- After disidentification it keeps running for a while on momentum and tries to trick you back into identification.[^6]
- Resistance to letting it go comes from having made an unhappy self out of it. The unconscious fear of losing that identity resists disidentification. Making the resistance conscious ends it.[^7]
- **Victim identity** is the belief that the past is more powerful than the present and that others are responsible for who you are now. In truth, all power lies in present awareness, and you are responsible for your inner space.[^8]
- Nobody can do it for you, but an intensely present teacher or therapist can speed the process up.[^9]
- Tolle also posits a *collective* pain-body (e.g., the accumulated suffering of women) and attributes premenstrual pain to it, and claims it causes illnesses and accidents. These claims have no scientific support.[^10]
- Every addiction starts and ends with pain: it is an unconscious refusal to face and move through one's own pain.[^11]
- "Unconsciousness creates it; consciousness transmutes it into itself."[^5]
- Related: [[Emotion]], [[Ego]], [[Forgiveness]].
- *A New Earth*: any negative emotion not fully faced as it arises leaves a remnant of pain, and no one gets through childhood without it. Some pain-bodies respond to only one kind of trigger that echoes a past pain.[^12]
- People hang on to old emotion because their emotional thinking has become their self. "It is not so much that you cannot stop your train of negative thoughts, but that you don't want to."[^13]
- Whatever share of it is collective (national or racial) or personal, you go beyond it only by taking responsibility for your inner state now. Blame feeds it. "There is only one perpetrator of evil on the planet: human unconsciousness. That realization is true forgiveness."[^14]
- Freedom begins with realizing you have a pain-body and noticing it when it activates. Then *accept* it, without concluding that something is wrong with you, which the ego loves.[^15]
- People with heavy pain-bodies often have a better chance of awakening, because the unhappiness becomes unbearable.[^16]
