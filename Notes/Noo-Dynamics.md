# Definition
> [!info] Noo-Dynamics[^1]
> ([[Viktor Frankl|Frankl]]) The existential dynamics in a polar field of tension, one pole being a meaning to be fulfilled and the other the person who has to fulfil it: the tension between what one has achieved and what one ought to accomplish, between what one is and what one should become. Frankl holds that such tension, not homeostasis, is indispensable to mental health.

# Properties
- It is a "dangerous misconception of mental hygiene" that what man needs first is equilibrium (a tensionless state); what he needs is "the striving and struggling for a worthwhile goal, a freely chosen task", even more so in neurotic individuals.[^1]
- One should therefore not hesitate to challenge a person with a potential meaning, which evokes the [[Will to Meaning|will to meaning]] from latency.[^1]
- "There is nothing in the world … that would so effectively help one to survive even the worst conditions as the knowledge that there is a meaning in one's life."[^2]
- Compare Yalom's [[Existential Guilt]] (the gap between actual and potential self).
