# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Rank-1 Matrix Operator Norm)[^1]
> If $A \in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{R})$ has [[Rank]] 1, then
> $$
> \lVert A \rVert_{op} = \lVert A \rVert_F
> $$

For a rank-1 matrix, the [[Operator Norm]] and [[Frobenius Norm]] coincide, even though in general $\lVert A \rVert_{op} \leq \lVert A \rVert_F$ (see [[Operator Norm and Frobenius Norm Inequality]]).
