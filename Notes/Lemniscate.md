# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Lemniscate of Bernoulli)[^1]
> A lemniscate is the graph in the [[Polar Coordinate System]] of
> $$
> \begin{align}
> r^2 = a^2\cos(2\theta) \quad \text{or} \quad r^2 = a^2\sin(2\theta), \qquad a \neq 0
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- Figure-eight shaped, through the origin. Points exist only where the right-hand side is $\geq 0$ (e.g. $\cos 2\theta \geq 0$, i.e. $|\theta| \leq \frac{\pi}{4}$ modulo $\pi$).
- Rectangular form of the $\cos$ version: $(x^2 + y^2)^2 = a^2(x^2 - y^2)$, from $r^4 = a^2 r^2 \cos(2\theta)$ and $r^2\cos 2\theta = x^2 - y^2$ ([[Cartesian and Polar Coordinate System Change]], [[Double Angle Identities]]).
