# Definition

Volume is a measure of how much it takes to fill up the insides of a shape. It is measured in cubic units.[^1]

In linear algebra, how a [[Linear Map]] scales volume is governed by its [[Determinant]]; see the [[Determinant Volume Scaling Theorem]].
