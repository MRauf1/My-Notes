# Definition
> [!info] Standard Model of Artificial Intelligence[^1]
> The prevailing paradigm in AI research, in which a machine ([[Rational Agent|rational agent]]) is built to optimize a fixed, fully specified objective supplied by its designer.

# Properties
- Although it has been a useful guide for AI research since the field's inception, the standard model is probably not the right model in the long run, since it assumes the designer can supply a fully specified objective to the machine.
- When the objective given to the machine does not perfectly match true human preferences, this produces the [[Value Alignment Problem]].

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 22](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=22&annotation=KW4NNGUS)
