# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Relation]] Least [[Relation Upper Bound]])[^1]
> Let $R$ be a [[Partial Order]] on $A$ and $B \subseteq A$. Let $U$ be the set of all [[Relation Upper Bound]] for $B$. If $U$ has a [[Relation Smallest Element]], then this smallest element is the least upper bound of $B$.

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=211)
