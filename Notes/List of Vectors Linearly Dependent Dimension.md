# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (List of [[Vector]] [[Linearly Dependent]] [[Dimension]])[^1]
> Suppose that $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ is a list of [[Vector]] in $\mathbb{F}^m$ with $n > m$. Then this list is [[Linearly Dependent]].
