# Definition
> [!info] Frequency (Waves)[^1]
> For a [[Sinusoidal Wave]] $u = A\cos(kx - \omega t + \varphi_0)$ observed at a fixed point:
> - the **frequency** $f$ is the number of oscillations per unit time (in Hz = cycles/s);
> - the **period** $T = 1/f$ is the time for one oscillation;
> - the **angular frequency** is $\omega = 2\pi f$ (in rad/s).

# Properties
- It is related to wavelength by $v_p = \lambda f$ ([[Phase Velocity]]). The relation $\omega(k)$ between frequency and [[Wavenumber|wavenumber]] is the *dispersion relation* of the medium. For the [[Wave Equation]] it is $\omega = c|k|$.
- Frequency is temporal; its spatial counterpart is the [[Spatial Frequency|spatial frequency]] $1/\lambda$.
- In quantum mechanics, $E = h f = \hbar\omega$.
- Not to be confused with frequency in statistics ([[Frequency]]).
