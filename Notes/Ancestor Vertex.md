# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Ancestor [[Vertex]])[^1]
> In a [[Tree]], if you can get from $x$ to $g$ by following zero or more [[Parent Vertex]] links, then $g$ is an ancestor vertex of $x$, and $x$ is a descendant vertex of $g$.

[^1]: [Building Blocks for Theoretical Computer Science](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/5IGT8C55?page=164)
