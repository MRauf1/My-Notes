# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Diagonalizable [[Linear Map]])[^1]
> A [[Linear Map]] $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$ is diagonalizable if there exists a [[Basis]] $\mathcal{B}$ of $V$ such that $[T]_{\mathcal{B}}$ is a [[Diagonal Matrix]]. We refer to finding such a [[Basis]] as diagonalizing the [[Linear Map]].

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Corollary)[^1]
> [[Linear Map]] $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$ is diagonalizable if and only if there exists a [[Basis]] $\mathcal{B}$ of $V$ consisting entirely of [[Eigenvector]] of $T$.
