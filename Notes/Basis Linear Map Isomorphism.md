# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Basis]] [[Linear Map Isomorphism]])[^1]
> Let $T \in \mathcal{L}(V, W)$ and let $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ be a [[Basis]] of $V$. Then $T$ is a [[Linear Map Isomorphism]] if and only if $(T(v_1), \dots, T(v_n))$ is a [[Basis]] of $W$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=176)
