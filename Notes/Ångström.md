# Definition
> [!info] Ångström[^1]
> A unit of length: $1\ \text{Å} = 10^{-10}\ \text{m} = 0.1\ \text{nm}$.

It is the natural scale of molecular structure. A C–C bond is $\approx 1.5$ Å, consecutive Cα atoms of a protein chain are $\approx 3.8$ Å apart, an α-helix is $\approx 10$ Å wide, and a ribosome is $\approx 250$ Å across.

# Properties
- Cryo-EM [[Resolution (Cryo-EM)|resolution]] and detector pixel sizes ($\approx 0.5$–$1.5$ Å at the specimen) are quoted in Å.
- **Electron wavelength.** By wave–particle duality, $\lambda = h/(m_e v)$ with $m_e$ the relativistic mass. At $100$–$300$ keV, $\lambda \approx 0.037$–$0.020$ Å. This is far below molecular scales, so the wavelength does not limit cryo-EM resolution; in practice, objective-lens aberrations limit it to about $1$ Å.

[^1]: [Angstrom (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angstrom)
