# Definition
> [!info] Musonius Rufus[^1]
> Gaius Musonius Rufus, a Roman [[Stoicism|Stoic]] of the first century CE and a member of the equestrian order, the foremost Stoic teacher in Rome and the teacher of [[Epictetus]].

# Properties
- Wrote nothing; a few discourses survive as a student's transcripts, plus short fragments.[^1]
- Specialised in Stoically tinged advice on everyday topics: marriage, family life, and self-presentation.[^2]
- Treated women as equal to men in their capacity for rationality and virtue, and held marriage and child-rearing compatible with a philosophical life; unlike Epictetus's more conventional view.[^2]
- Spoke so that each listener felt someone had informed on his faults: "If you've got nothing better to do than praise me, my speaking is a waste of time."[^3]
