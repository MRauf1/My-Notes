# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Moment Generating Function]] Existence Theorem)[^1]
> Let $X$ be a [[Random Variable]], $m \in \mathbb{N}$, and $E[X^m]$ exists. Then for all $k \leq m$, $E[X^k]$ exists.

[^1]: [Introduction to Mathematical Statistics](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/P3TUBR4A?page=94)
