# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Nth [[Derivative]])[^1]
> Nth [[Derivative]] is the [[Differentiation Operator]] applied $n$ times on a [[Function]] $f$. It is denoted as (and in [[Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz|Leibniz]] notation)
> $$
> \begin{align}
> f^{(n)}(x) = \frac{d^nf}{dx^n}
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!info] Definition 2 (Derivative of Order $n$)[^2]
> If $f$ has a derivative $f'$ on an interval and $f'$ is itself differentiable, its derivative is $f''$. Continuing, we obtain $f, f', f'', f^{(3)}, \dots, f^{(n)}$, each the derivative of the preceding one; $f^{(n)}$ is the $n$th derivative of $f$.

For $f^{(n)}(x)$ to exist at a point $x$, $f^{(n-1)}(t)$ must exist in a neighborhood of $x$ (one-sided if $x$ is an endpoint), and $f^{(n-1)}$ must be differentiable at $x$. Hence $f^{(n-2)}$ must be differentiable in that neighborhood.[^2]
