# Definition
> [!info] Udevadm[^1]
> Udevadm is the administration program for [[Udev|udevd]], used to reload its rules, trigger events, search for and explore system devices, and monitor uevents as udevd receives them from the kernel.

[^1]: [How Linux Works: What Every Superuser Should Know](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/B4TILA8A?page=82&annotation=ZIX37GPU)
