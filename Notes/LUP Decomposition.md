# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (LUP Decomposition)[^1]
> Like [[LU Decomposition]], but there is an additional [[Permutation Matrix]] $P$, which is a product of [[Elementary Matrix]]/[[Row Operations]] R3, such that $PA = LU$.

Every [[Matrix]] has an LUP Decomposition.

> [!abstract] Theorem 2 (Exercise 6.2.4b -- Determinant)[^2]
> Suppose $PA = LU$ is an LUP decomposition of $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$, where $P = A_\sigma$ is the [[Permutation Matrix]] for some $\sigma \in S_n$. Then
> $$
> \det(A) = (sgn\,\sigma) \, u_{11} \cdots u_{nn}
> $$
