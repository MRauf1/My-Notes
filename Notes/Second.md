# Definition
> [!info] Second[^1]
> The second is the standard unit of time. Historically it was defined as $1/86{,}400$ of an average solar day, tying all clocks to the earth's period of rotation. It is now defined operationally via [[Atomic Clock|atomic clocks]], using the period of a natural atomic vibration as the time reference.

# Properties
- The earth's rotation was the best available time reference until atomic clocks, which are far more constant since their internal period is insensitive to temperature and other external effects.
- Modern atomic clocks keep time to an accuracy of one part in $10^9$ or better.

[^1]: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. I, Ch. 5: Time and Distance](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_05.html)
