# Definition
> [!info] Wavelength[^1]
> The spatial period $\lambda$ of a [[Sinusoidal Wave]]: the distance over which the wave's shape repeats at a fixed time, e.g. from one crest to the next ([[Crest and Trough]]). In $u = A\cos(kx - \omega t + \varphi_0)$, $\lambda = 2\pi/k$.

# Properties
- It is related to frequency by $\lambda = v_p / f$ ([[Phase Velocity]]).
- Its reciprocal $1/\lambda$ is the [[Spatial Frequency|spatial frequency]] (cycles per unit length), and $2\pi/\lambda$ is the [[Wavenumber|wavenumber]].
- For matter waves it is the de Broglie wavelength $\lambda = h/p$ ([[Wave-Particle Duality]]), e.g. $\approx 0.02$ Å for 300 keV electrons ([[Ångström]]).
- It sets the scale of wave effects such as [[Diffraction]]: features much larger than $\lambda$ behave geometrically.
