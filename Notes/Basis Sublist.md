# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Basis]] Sublist)[^1]
> Let $V$ be a non-zero [[Vector Space]], $\mathcal{B} = (v_1, \dots, v_n)$, and $V = \langle \mathcal{B} \rangle$. Then there is a sublist of $\mathcal{B}$ which is a [[Basis]] of $V$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=173)
