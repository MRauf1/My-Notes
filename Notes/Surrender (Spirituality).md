# Definition
> [!info] Surrender (Tolle)[^1]
> Yielding to the flow of life rather than opposing it: letting go of mental-emotional resistance to what is, i.e., accepting the "isness" of the present moment without labeling or judging it. It is not passivity, resignation, or "I don't care anymore". It is fully compatible with planning, taking action, and achieving goals.

# Properties
- **Resistance creates pain.** Pain created now is always some form of nonacceptance. On the level of thought, resistance shows up as judgment; on the emotional level, as negativity. "Mind and resistance are synonymous," so acceptance immediately frees you from mind dominance.[^2]
- **Accept, then act.** Accept whatever the moment contains as if you had chosen it. Then, if you want out of the "mud", act from that non-reactive state, doing and focusing on one thing at a time.[^3]
- **Three options** when your here and now is intolerable: leave the situation, change it, or accept it totally. Choose now and accept the consequences: "No excuses. No negativity." Drop negativity before acting if possible. Any action is often better than staying stuck.[^4]
- If you cannot surrender, take action immediately. If you cannot accept the external condition, accept the internal one.[^5]
- Complaining is nonacceptance and makes you a victim.[^6]
- Nonresistance doesn't mean doing nothing; it means doing becomes non-reactive. You can say "no" firmly, or walk away, in complete inner nonresistance.[^7]
- **Acceptance of non-peace**: don't look for any state other than the one you are in. Forgive yourself for not being at peace; fully accepted non-peace becomes peace.[^8]
- Acceptance practiced for a long time must lead to the next stage, where negative emotions are no longer created. Otherwise "acceptance" becomes a label that lets the ego keep indulging in unhappiness.[^9]
- **Negativity** never works: it keeps undesirable conditions in place and only strengthens the [[Ego|ego]]. It is contagious. Ordinary negativity can simply be dropped once you see you don't need it; deep pain needs acceptance plus sustained attention ([[Pain-Body]]). Until then, use negativity as a signal to become more present.[^10]
- Illness, focused on in the instant without labels, reduces to pain, weakness, discomfort, or disability. Suffering can push you into presence.[^11]
- Happiness depends on conditions being perceived as positive; inner peace does not. In grief one cannot be happy but can be at peace beneath the sadness.[^12]
- Tolle presents surrender as one of the portals to [[The Unmanifested]]. Compare the Stoic acceptance in Marcus Aurelius ("Accept whatever comes to you woven in the pattern of your destiny") and [[Stoicism]].[^13]
