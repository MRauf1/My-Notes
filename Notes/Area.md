# Definition

Measure of the [[Surface|surface]] that is covered by a [[Shape|shape]]. It is measured in square units.[^1] Intuitively, area extends [[Length|length]] by one dimension, the same way [[Perimeter|perimeter]] extends a point over a line and [[Volume|volume]] extends a line segment over a plane.

> [!example]- Examples 1 (Area)
> > [!example]- Example 1.1 (Area of a [[Square|Square]])
> > $$
> > \begin{align}
> > \mathrm{For a square with length $4$, the area is} \\
> > 4 \cdot 4 = 16
> > \end{align}
> > $$
