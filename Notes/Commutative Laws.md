# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Commutative Laws)[^1]
> For [[Proposition|statements]] $P, Q$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> P \land Q &\equiv Q \land P \\
> P \lor Q &\equiv Q \lor P
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- A [[Logical Equivalence]]; set analogues $A \cap B = B \cap A$, $A \cup B = B \cup A$ ([[Set Basic Properties]]).

[^1]: [HOW TO PROVE IT: A Structured Approach, Second Edition](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/THI2Q4PN?page=35)
