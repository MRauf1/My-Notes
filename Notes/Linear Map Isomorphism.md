# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Linear Map]] [[Isomorphism]])[^1]
> [[Isomorphism]] between two [[Vector Space]] $V, W$ is a [[Bijective Function]] [[Linear Map]] $T: V \rightarrow W$. We say that $V$ is isomorphic to $W$.

The [[Dimension]] of these two [[Vector Space]] must be the same if they are [[Finite-Dimensional Vector Space]]. If they are [[Infinite-Dimensional Vector Space]], then the [[Set Cardinality]] of their [[Basis]] must be the same.
