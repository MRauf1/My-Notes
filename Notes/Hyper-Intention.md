# Definition
> [!info] Hyper-Intention[^1]
> (Logotherapy) Excessive, forced intention that makes impossible what one forcibly wishes, just as fear brings about what one fears. Its companion is **hyper-reflection**: excessive attention to oneself, which is also pathogenic.

# Properties
- Pleasure, happiness, and success are by-products: they are destroyed to the degree they are made goals in themselves; "happiness cannot be pursued; it must ensue", as the unintended side-effect of dedication to a cause greater than oneself or surrender to another person.[^2]
- A human being is not in pursuit of happiness but in search of a *reason* to be happy; once the reason is found, one becomes happy automatically. Likewise optimism, faith, and love cannot be commanded.[^3]
- Sexual neuroses often combine anticipatory anxiety, hyper-intention to perform, and hyper-reflection on oneself instead of the partner; refocusing attention on the partner resolves them.[^4]
- Hyper-intention and hyper-reflection are counteracted by [[Dereflection]], which ultimately requires orientation toward one's specific vocation ([[Self-Transcendence]]); [[Anticipatory Anxiety]] is counteracted by [[Paradoxical Intention]]. "The cue to cure is self-transcendence!"[^5]
