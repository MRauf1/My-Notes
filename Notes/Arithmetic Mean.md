# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Arithmetic Mean)[^1]
> For a dataset $\{x_1, ..., x_n\}$, the arithmetic mean is
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \overline{x} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i
> \end{align}
> $$

The arithmetic mean is the average of a set of numbers, found by summing them and dividing by their count. Its symbol is $\overline{x}$. It is a type of [[Mean|mean]].
