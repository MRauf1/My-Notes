# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Partition of Interval Notation)[^1]
> Partition of [[Interval Notation]] $[a, b]$ is any finite ordered [[Subset]] $P$ of the form
> $$
> \begin{align}
> P = \{a = t_0 < t_1 < \dots < t_n = b\}
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!info] Definition 2 (Partition of [[Set]])[^2]
> [[Set]] of [[Set]] $F$ is called a partition of the [[Set]] $A$ if
> 1) $\cup F = A$
> 2) $F$ is pairwise [[Disjoint Sets]]
> 3) $\forall X \in F, X \neq \emptyset$
