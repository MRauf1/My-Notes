# Definition
> [!info] Definition 1 (Linear Operator)[^1]
> A linear operator is a [[Linear Map]] $T: V \rightarrow W$ between [[Vector Space|vector spaces]]. A linear operator is an abstract [[Function]], distinct from any particular [[Matrix]] used to represent it: choosing a [[Basis]] of $V$ and $W$ produces a matrix representation of $T$, but $T$ itself does not depend on that choice.

Whether $T$ can be represented by a matrix at all depends on the dimensionality of $V$ and $W$.
