# Definition
> [!info] Loving-Kindness Meditation[^1]
> (*Metta*) The practice of generating good feelings and wishes first toward oneself, after banishing self-hatred and self-condemnation. They are then extended outward to those closest to you, and gradually to enemies and all living beings everywhere. It is traditionally recited, with genuinely felt intention, at the start of each [[Meditation|meditation]] session.

# Properties
- It is practiced to purify one's *own* mind, so you can act without bias, prejudice, or hate. Compassion is loving-kindness in action.[^2]
- You cannot share with others what you do not have within yourself, so it starts with yourself.[^3]
- It counters resentment, the most damaging psychic irritant that arises when the mind is quiet. Hatred harms the hater first: pain, raised heart rate, tension, loss of appetite and sleep, and inability to see the truth.[^4]
- "If all of your enemies are well, happy and peaceful, they would not be your enemies." The practical solution to enemies is to help them overcome their problems.[^5]
- Recommended at bedtime and on waking: it is said to help sleep, prevent nightmares, and make you more open toward everyone.[^6]
- It is an instance of using a [[Skillful and Unskillful Thoughts|skillful thought]] (positive emotion) to cancel a negative one, which weakens the harmful aspects of [[Ego|ego]] so that [[Mindfulness|mindfulness]] meets less resistance.[^7]
