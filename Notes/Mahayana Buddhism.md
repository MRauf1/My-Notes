# Definition
> [!info] Mahayana Buddhism[^1]
> One of the two broad streams of [[Buddhism]], prevailing throughout East Asia and shaping the cultures of China, Korea, Japan, Nepal, Tibet, and Vietnam.

# Types
- **Zen** — the most widely known Mahayana system (Japan, Korea, Vietnam, United States). Uses two approaches to awareness: a direct plunge into awareness by sheer force of will, and (in the Rinzai school) tricking the mind out of conscious thought into pure awareness. Zen is tough.[^2]
- **Tantra** (Tantric/Vajrayana Buddhism) — nearly the reverse of Zen. It seeks pure awareness by destroying the [[Ego|ego]] image through visualization: the practitioner takes off their own identity and puts on another, watches how an ego is constructed, recognizes the arbitrary nature of all egos (including their own), and is freed from bondage to the ego.[^3]

# Properties
- Counterpart of [[Theravada Buddhism]].[^1]
