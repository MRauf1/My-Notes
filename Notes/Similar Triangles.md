# Definition

Similar triangles have the same corresponding [[Angle|angles]] and their corresponding side lengths are in the same ratio.[^1]

More generally, this is a special case of similar figures: two [[Shape|shapes]] are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding side lengths share a common ratio.
