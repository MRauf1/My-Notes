# Definition

Specifies the value of the unknown [[Function]] at some given point in the [[Domain]].[^1]

> [!info] Initial Condition (Partial Differential Equations)[^2]
> For a time-dependent [[Partial Differential Equation]], an initial condition specifies the physical state at a particular time $t_0$:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> u(\mathbf{x}, t_0) = \phi(\mathbf{x}),
> \end{align}
> $$
> with $\phi$ a given function.

# Properties
- An equation of order $k$ in $t$ needs $k$ initial conditions: one for the [[Diffusion Equation]] and [[Schrödinger Equation]]; two for the [[Wave Equation]], position $u(\mathbf{x}, t_0) = \phi(\mathbf{x})$ and velocity $u_t(\mathbf{x}, t_0) = \psi(\mathbf{x})$.[^3]
- Usually paired with [[Boundary Condition|boundary conditions]] when the domain has a boundary.
