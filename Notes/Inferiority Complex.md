# Definition
> [!info] Inferiority Complex[^1]
> The state of using one's [[Feeling of Inferiority]] as an **excuse**, following the logic "A is the situation, so B cannot be done" (for example, "I'm not well educated, so I can't succeed"). It replaces realistic effort with giving up before trying. A "complex" in general is an abnormal mental state made up of a tangled group of emotions and ideas.

# Properties
- **Apparent cause and effect.** One convinces oneself of a causal link where there is none. Teleologically, "I can't succeed" really means "I don't want to succeed". See [[Teleology (Adlerian Psychology)]].[^2]
- **Hidden implication.** Saying "A, so not B" implies that without A one would be capable and valuable. It keeps the possibility alive without testing it. Cf. [[Self-Handicapping]].[^3]
- **Cause.** Lack of courage to compensate through striving and growth.[^4]
- **Bragging about misfortune.** Using one's misfortune to be "special" and to control others. Adler: "In our culture weakness can be quite strong and powerful." Whoever uses misfortune this way will always need it.[^5]
- It can turn into a [[Superiority Complex]]. Both grow out of competition and comparison ([[Pursuit of Superiority]]) and are dissolved by [[Vertical and Horizontal Relationships|horizontal relationships]].[^6]
- A form of the [[Life-Lie]].

# Evidence
- Self-handicapping, which creates obstacles to protect one's sense of ability from failure, is well documented (Berglas & Jones, 1978). It gives empirical support to the "excuse" function.
