# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Basis]] [[Algorithm]])[^1]
> Let $(v_1, \dots, v_n)$ be a list of [[Vector]] in $\mathbb{F}^m$. To find a [[Basis]] $\mathcal{B}$ for $\langle v_1, \dots, v_n \rangle$:
> 1) Put $A = [v_1 \dots v_n]$ in [[Reduced Row Echelon Form]].
> 2) If the $i$th column contains a [[Pivot Variable]], include $v_i$ in $\mathcal{B}$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=174)
