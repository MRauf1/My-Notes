# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 (Increasing Property of [[Square Root|Square Root]])[^1]
> For $a, b \geq 0$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> a \leq b \iff \sqrt{a} \leq \sqrt{b}
> \end{align}
> $$

Square root on non-negative numbers preserves the [[Inequality|inequality]] sign in both directions, since $\sqrt{\cdot}$ is strictly increasing on $[0, \infty)$.
