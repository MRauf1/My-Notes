According to a 2012 study by [[Wilhelm Hoffman]] and [[Roy Baumeister]], people fight desire all the time.

In [[Buddhism]], desire (craving) is the cause of suffering ([[Dukkha]]), though humans mistake it for the source of happiness. The goal is to recognize desires without being controlled by them.[^1]
