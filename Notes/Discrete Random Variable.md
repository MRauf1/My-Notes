# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Discrete|Discrete]] [[Random Variable|Random Variable]])[^1][^2]
> A random variable $X$ is discrete if its space (range) $\mathcal{D}$ is finite or [[Countable Set|countable]].

The condition is on the range of $X$, not on the [[Sample Space]]: a discrete $X$ can be defined on an uncountable $\mathcal{C}$ (e.g. the indicator of an interval).
