# Definition
> [!info] AI Control Problem[^1]
> The problem of ensuring that a superintelligence being built will not harm the interests of its builders or of humanity. It comprises two principal-agent problems:
> - *First principal-agent problem* - between a human principal and human agents (e.g. developers) acting on its behalf; arises mainly in the development phase.
> - *Second principal-agent problem* - between the project (principal) and the superintelligent system (agent); specific to the intelligence explosion and threatening mainly in the operational phase.

# Types
- [[Capability Control Methods]] - control what the superintelligence *can* do.[^2]
- [[Motivation Selection Methods]] - control what it *wants* to do.[^2]

# Properties
- Control methods must be implemented before the system becomes superintelligent; they cannot be imposed after it obtains a [[Decisive Strategic Advantage]], and must succeed in the very first system to attain superintelligence, which makes a "controlled detonation" daunting.[^3]
- Complicated by the [[Treacherous Turn]], [[Perverse Instantiation]], and the choice of system caste ([[AI System Castes]]).
- The superintelligence-level form of the [[Value Alignment Problem]].
