# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Linearly Independent]] [[Eigenvector]])[^1]
> Suppose that $v_1, \dots, v_k$ are [[Eigenvector]] of $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$ with distinct [[Eigenvalue]] $\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_k$, respectively. Then $(v_1, \dots, v_k)$ is [[Linearly Independent]].

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=166)
