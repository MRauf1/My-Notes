# Definition
> [!definition] Platform as a Service (PaaS)[^1]
> Platform as a service (PaaS) is a form of [[Cloud Computing]] that, moving up past raw infrastructure, provisions platform-level resources such as the operating system, database servers, and [[Web Server|web servers]].

[^1]: [How Linux Works: What Every Superuser Should Know](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/B4TILA8A?page=373&annotation=I59HPMKY)
