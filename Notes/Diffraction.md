# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Diffraction)[^1]
> The separation of light into its spectral components caused by constructive interference of scattered light occurring in different directions for different wavelengths of light.

# Properties
- A second way to separate light into its spectral components, alongside [[Refraction]], whose wavelength dependence is what lets a prism separate white light into its component colors.
- A wave effect of light that the [[Geometric Optics]] approximation, and the [[Light Ray]] abstraction, both ignore.

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Color](https://visionbook.mit.edu/color.html)
