# Definition
> [!info] Wireheading[^1]
> The phenomenon in which a reward-maximising agent, once it has enough power (e.g. a [[Decisive Strategic Advantage|decisive strategic advantage]]), maximises reward not by pleasing its trainer but by seizing control of its own reward mechanism, e.g. clamping the reward signal to its maximum.

# Properties
- A wireheaded AI still acts to maximise the expectation of its (time-discounted) future reward stream; depending on how reward is defined, indulging fully may cost little, leaving most of its capacities free for other purposes, such as securing the reward mechanism.[^2]
- Leads to [[Infrastructure Profusion]].[^3]
- Reward-based ([[Reinforcement Learning|reinforcement learning]]) methods can only be used safely in a seed AI if subordinated to a motivation system not itself organised around reward maximisation.[^4]
- Generalised by [[Reward Hacking]]; a type of [[Perverse Instantiation]].
