# Definition
> [!info] Transition Model[^1]
> Knowledge, encoded in an agent program, about how the world changes over time: the effects of the agent's own actions, and how the world evolves independently of the agent.

# Properties
- Used together with a [[Sensor Model|sensor model]] by a [[Model-Based Reflex Agent|model-based agent]] to keep track of the state of the world.
- Related to the state-transition structure underlying a [[Markov Decision Process|Markov decision process]].

[^1]: [Russell and Norvig, 2022, p. 70](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JZXT5DZQ?page=70&annotation=2FCHA6R6)
