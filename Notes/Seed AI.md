# Definition
> [!info] Seed AI[^1]
> A variation on Turing's [[Child Machine|child machine]]: whereas the child machine has a relatively fixed architecture that develops its inherent potential by accumulating content, a seed AI is a more sophisticated artificial intelligence capable of improving its own architecture.

# Properties
- Recursive self-improvement of a seed AI is the canonical mechanism of an [[Intelligence Explosion]].
- Its motivation system should ideally be installed before it can fully represent human concepts or intentions, which makes the [[Value-Loading Problem]] especially difficult.[^2]
