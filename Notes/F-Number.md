# Definition
> [!info] Definition (F-Number)[^1]
> A camera's f-number, $N$, is the ratio of a lens's [[Focal Length (Lens)|focal length]] $f$ to the diameter $A$ of its [[Aperture]]:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> N = \frac{f}{A}
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- A smaller f-number corresponds to a larger aperture relative to the focal length, admitting more light but reducing the [[Depth of Field]].

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Lenses](https://visionbook.mit.edu/lenses.html)
