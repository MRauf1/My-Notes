# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Concave Lens)[^1]
> A [[Lens]] whose surface curves inward toward the center of the lens. By the reflection symmetry of the lens surface angles, a concave lens bends parallel rays away from the lens center by the same amount that a [[Convex Lens]] of the same radii of curvature would bend them toward the center, causing parallel rays to diverge as if from a virtual focus.

# Properties
- Has a negative [[Focal Length (Lens)|focal length]] under the same [[Lensmaker's Equation]] that describes a convex lens.
- Its virtual focal point lies on the same side of the lens as the incoming parallel rays, at distance $-f$ from the lens; rays leaving the lens appear to emanate from this point.

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Lenses](https://visionbook.mit.edu/lenses.html)
