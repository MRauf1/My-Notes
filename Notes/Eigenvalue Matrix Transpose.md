# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Eigenvalue]] [[Matrix Transpose]])[^1]
> Let $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$. Then $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}$ is an [[Eigenvalue]] of $A$ if and only if $\lambda$ is an [[Eigenvalue]] of $A^T$.

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=199)
