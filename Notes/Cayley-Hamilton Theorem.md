# Definition
> [!abstract] Theorem 1 (Theorem 6.26 -- The Cayley-Hamilton Theorem)[^1]
> If $A \in M_n(\mathbb{F})$, then $p_A(A) = 0$, where $p_A$ is the [[Characteristic Polynomial]] of $A$.

Every matrix satisfies its own characteristic polynomial (substituting the matrix $A$ itself, with $A^0 := I_n$, for the variable $x$).
