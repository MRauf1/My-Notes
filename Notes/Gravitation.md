# Definition
> [!info] Gravitation[^1]
> The long-range attractive interaction between all matter, whose strength varies inversely with the square of the distance between two bodies; reinterpreted by general relativity as a manifestation of curved [[Spacetime]] rather than a force acting at a distance.

# Properties
- Unlike the electromagnetic interaction between [[Electric Charge|electric charges]], gravitation is always attractive and universal: every particle attracts every other particle.
- By far the weakest of the [[Four Fundamental Interactions]].
- Couples to all mass-energy, not to a distinct "gravitational charge".
- General relativity replaces the Newtonian picture of gravitation as a [[Force]] transmitted through a fixed space with curvature of [[Spacetime]] itself.
