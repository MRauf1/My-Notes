# Definition
> [!info] Definition (Index of Refraction)[^1]
> A scalar $n$ characterizing a material's effect on light, such that both the wavelength and the speed of light within the material are inversely proportional to $n$. The index of refraction of a vacuum is $1$.

# Properties
- Appears in [[Snell's Law]], $n_1 \sin(\theta_1) = n_2 \sin(\theta_2)$, relating the bending of a light ray to the indices of refraction on either side of a material interface.
- Also appears in the [[Lensmaker's Equation]], where a lens's [[Focal Length (Lens)|focal length]] depends on the index of refraction of the lens material.

[^1]: [MIT Vision Book - Lenses](https://visionbook.mit.edu/lenses.html)
