# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Cycle]] [[Graph]])[^1]
> [[Graph]] with [[Vertex]] $v_1, \dots, v_n$ and with [[Edge]] $v_i \rightarrow v_{i+1}$ and $v_n \rightarrow v_1$.
> It is denoted as $C_n$ where $n$ is the number of [[Vertex]].
