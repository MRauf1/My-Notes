# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Probability Function|Probability]] Density Function)[^1]
> For a [[Continuous Random Variable]] $X$, its pdf, denoted as $f(x)$ or $f_X$, is the [[Function]] used to calculate probabilities with [[Riemann Integral|Integration]].
> It is the [[Derivative]] of the [[Cumulative Distribution Function]].
> $f_X(X = x) = 0$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$
