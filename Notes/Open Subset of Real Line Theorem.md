# Definition
> [!abstract] Open Subset of Real Line Theorem[^1]
> Every [[Open Set|open set]] $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ is a [[Countable Set|countable]] [[Disjoint Set Union|disjoint union]] of open intervals.

Here $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ is open if, for every $x \in U$, $U$ includes an interval centered at $x$.[^2] The intervals are the connected components of $U$ (possibly unbounded, e.g. $(a, \infty)$); countability follows because each contains a distinct rational.

# Properties
- Makes the Lebesgue measure of an open subset of $\mathbb{R}$ the sum of the lengths of its component intervals (see [[Lebesgue Measure]]).
- Fails in $\mathbb{R}^n$ for $n \geq 2$: open sets are only countable unions of almost-disjoint closed cubes.
- Relies on [[Connected Subset of Real Line Theorem]] (connected subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ are intervals).
