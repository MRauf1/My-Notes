# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Inner Product]] and [[Vector Norm]] under [[Orthonormal Basis]])[^1]
> Let $\mathcal{B} = (e_1, \dots, e_n)$ be an [[Orthonormal Basis]] for $V$. Then for any $u, v \in V$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \langle u, v \rangle &= \sum_{j=1}^n \langle u, e_j \rangle \overline{\langle v, e_j \rangle} \\
> \lVert v \rVert^2 &= \sum_{j=1}^n |\langle v, e_j \rangle|^2
> \end{align}
> $$

[^1]: [Linear Algebra (Cambridge Mathematical Textbooks) -- Elizabeth S_ Meckes, Mark W_ Meckes](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HG5B3R7J?page=263)
