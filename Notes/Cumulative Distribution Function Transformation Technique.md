# Definition

> [!abstract] Theorem 1 ([[Cumulative Distribution Function]] [[Transformation]] Technique)[^1]
> Let $X$ be [[Continuous Random Variable]] with pdf $f_X$ and support $S_X$. Let $Y = g(X)$ where $g$ is [[Injective Function]] and [[Differentiable Function]] on $S_X$. Then
> $$
> \begin{align}
> f_Y(y) = f_X(g^{-1}(y)) |\frac{dx}{dy}|, y \in S_Y
> \end{align}
> $$
> where $\frac{dx}{dy} = \frac{d[g^{-1}(y)]}{dy}$ (also called the [[Jacobian Matrix|Jacobian]] of the transformation) and $S_Y$ is the support of $Y$. 

[^1]: [Introduction to Mathematical Statistics](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/P3TUBR4A?page=71)
