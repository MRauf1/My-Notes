# Definition
> [!info] IPv6[^1]
> A newer version of the [[Internet Protocol]] that resolves many of [[IPv4]]'s limitations, including simplifying routing tables and expanding addresses to 128 bits.

# Properties
- `::1` (`0:0:0:0:0:0:0:1`) is its [[Loopback Address]], corresponding to IPv4's `127.0.0.1`.[^2]
