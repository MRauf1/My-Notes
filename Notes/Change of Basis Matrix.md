# Definition

> [!info] Definition 1 ([[Change of Basis]] [[Matrix]])[^1]
> Let $\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{B}'$ be two different [[Basis]] of $V$. Then for any $v \in V$, $[v]_{\mathcal{B}'} = [I]_{\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{B}'} [v]_{\mathcal{B}}$.
> The [[Matrix]] $S = [I]_{\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{B}'}$ is the change of basis [[Matrix]] from $\mathcal{B}$ to $\mathcal{B}'$.
> The change of basis [[Matrix]] is always [[Square Matrix]] and [[Invertible Linear Map]].

Change of basis translates [[Coordinates]] within the exact same [[Vector Space]] with two different [[Basis]].
