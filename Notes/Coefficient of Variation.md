# Definition
> [!info] Coefficient of Variation[^1]
> For a [[Random Variable]] $\theta$,
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \mathrm{CV}(\theta) = \frac{\mathrm{sd}(\theta)}{E(\theta)}
> \end{align}
> $$

# Properties
- Ratio of the [[Standard Deviation]] to the [[Expectation|mean]]; dimensionless, so it measures relative spread and is invariant to rescaling $\theta \mapsto c\theta$, $c > 0$.
- Meaningful for positive quantities; undefined when $E(\theta) = 0$.
- Multiplicative counterpart: the [[Geometric Standard Deviation]].

[^1]: [Gelman et al., p. 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/HDF44SF4?page=16&annotation=3BPERD6Z)
